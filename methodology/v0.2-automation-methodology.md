# v0.2 Automation Methodology / v0.2 自动化方法论

This project uses automation to triage and draft evidence summaries. It does not claim to replace formal systematic review methods.

本项目使用自动化做筛选、分层和草稿生成，但不声称替代正式系统综述方法。

## Reference Frameworks / 参考框架

The v0.2 workflow is informed by mature evidence appraisal systems:

v0.2 工作流参考以下成熟证据评价体系：

- GRADE: certainty of evidence and strength of recommendations.
- Cochrane RoB 2: risk of bias assessment for randomized trials.
- ROBINS-I: risk of bias assessment for non-randomized intervention studies.
- AMSTAR 2: methodological quality of systematic reviews.
- Oxford CEBM Levels of Evidence: evidence hierarchy by question type.

## What Automation Can Do / 自动化可以做什么

- Extract bibliographic metadata, abstracts, publication types, DOI/PMID/PMCID, and trial-style signals.
- 抽取题录、摘要、出版类型、DOI/PMID/PMCID 和试验相关信号。

- Classify draft study design, species, endpoint class, and topic.
- 草判研究设计、物种、终点等级和主题。

- Generate public draft paper cards and topic pages with clear caveats.
- 生成带明确警示的公开草稿论文卡片和主题页。

- Rank candidates for human review using available proxy indicators.
- 使用可获得的代理指标，为人工复核排序。

## What Automation Cannot Do / 自动化不能做什么

- It cannot perform a formal GRADE evidence profile.
- 不能完成正式 GRADE 证据概要。

- It cannot make a definitive RoB 2, ROBINS-I, or AMSTAR 2 judgment from metadata alone.
- 不能只靠元数据完成确定性的 RoB 2、ROBINS-I 或 AMSTAR 2 判断。

- It cannot turn animal, biomarker, or mechanism evidence into human medical advice.
- 不能把动物、biomarker 或机制证据转成人类医疗建议。

## Proxy Credibility Score / 代理可信度评分

Because Journal Impact Factor is not reliably available through open automated sources, v0.2 uses a proxy score based on observable metadata:

由于 IF 不能通过开放自动来源稳定获得，v0.2 使用可观察元数据构建代理可信度：

| Component | Max | Signal |
|---|---:|---|
| Study design | 35 | systematic review/meta-analysis, RCT, cohort, animal, mechanism |
| Endpoint value | 20 | hard endpoint, functional endpoint, clinical risk marker, biomarker, clock, mechanism |
| Human relevance | 15 | human directness and population relevance |
| Authority signal | 10 | DOI, PMID, PMCID/open access, peer-reviewed journal, trial/review publication type |
| Atlas utility | 10 | fills a priority topic and improves coverage |
| Safety boundary clarity | 10 | ability to state unsupported claims and supervision needs |

Scores are draft triage signals. They must not be interpreted as final truth or clinical recommendation strength.

评分只是草稿筛选信号，不能被解释为最终真理或临床推荐强度。

## Status Labels / 状态标签

- `needs_review`: raw candidate, not public-ready.
- `public_draft_not_fully_reviewed`: public draft with strong caveats.
- `formal_include`: reviewed and accepted into formal evidence matrix.
- `exclude`: excluded after review.
- `duplicate`: duplicate source.

## Publication Rule / 发布规则

Every public draft must include:

每个公开草稿必须包含：

- Chinese and English draft warning.
- 中英双语草稿警示。
- Supported claim.
- 支持的结论。
- Unsupported claim.
- 不支持的结论。
- Overinterpretation risk.
- 过度解读风险。
- Medical supervision boundary.
- 是否需要医生监督的边界。

## Sources / 参考来源

- GRADE Working Group: transparent approach to grading quality/certainty of evidence and strength of recommendations.
- Cochrane RoB 2: revised risk-of-bias tool for randomized trials.
- ROBINS-I: risk-of-bias tool for non-randomized intervention studies.
- AMSTAR 2: assessment tool for systematic reviews.
- Oxford CEBM Levels of Evidence: evidence hierarchy for clinical questions.
