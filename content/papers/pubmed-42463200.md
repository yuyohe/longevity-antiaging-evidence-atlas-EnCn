## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Non-tuberculous mycobacterial pulmonary disease (NTM-PD) is a chronic debilitating infection requiring prolonged multidrug therapy. Common symptoms contribute to inactivity, malnutrition and sarcopenia, and low muscle mass is associated with increased mortality. Evidence supporting exercise in NTM-PD is scarce, and no randomised controlled trial has evaluated its efficacy. This study aims to determine whether a 6-week hybrid, circuit-based exercise programme improves cardiorespiratory fitness compared with delayed intervention/usual care and to evaluate its effects on physical function, activity, pulmonary function and patient-reported outcomes.
