## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In vitro, DAd-MR showed the lowest cytotoxicity (IC50 = 232.63 ± 7.88 μg/mL) and the highest wound closure rate (85.19 ± 2.82%) in the scratch assay. In vivo, topical application of 5% DAd-TM and 5% DAd-MR for 10 days in male Swiss mice significantly accelerated wound closure to 91% and 97% (P < 0.05), and compared to Mebo® (a commercial reference β-sitosterol-based preparation) by 41% and 52% (P < 0.05). Both fractions markedly reduced malondialdehyde (MDA) levels while increasing glutathione (GSH), collagen type I, MIP-2 (a key chemokine involved in inflammatory cell recruitment), and FOXO1 (a transcription factor that regulates oxidative stress response and keratinocyte migration/re-epithelialization). Histopathological examination with semi-quantitative scoring confirmed enhanced re-epithelialization, collagen deposition, and reduced inflammation, particularly with 5% DAd-MR.
