## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The associations between all diet-quality indices and stroke mortality were consistent in direction; a low-quality diet was associated with a greater risk of stroke death, but the HEI-2010 was the strongest predictor. The PAR for stroke death based on HEI-2010 was 7.9% (95%-CI: 3.7-12.2%), indicating the preventable percentage of deaths if the total population had the same diet quality as those in the highest tertile for this diet-quality index. The lowest as compared to the highest tertile of the HEI-2010 was associated with a 1.23-fold (95%-CI: 1.13-1.34) risk. The PARs for low and medium adherence to the indices were similar by sex and follow-up time, but varied by ethnicity, with the highest PAR in Whites (15.4%) and no association in Latinos.
