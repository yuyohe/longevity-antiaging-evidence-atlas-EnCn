## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 2240 residents, 1230 completed the survey (55% response rate) with 1081 eligible for analysis. Respondent frailty levels were as follows: Not Frail = 67% (n = 720), Prefrail = 14% (n = 157), Mildly Frail = 11% (n = 123), Moderately-Severely Frail = 7% (n = 81). For individuals 85-89 years old, age was significantly associated with increased odds of frailty (OR = 3.40; 95% CI: 1.62-7.09; p = .001).
