## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Intensive blood pressure-lowering treatment after spontaneous intracerebral haemorrhage reduces recurrent stroke, mainly by preventing recurrent haemorrhage, without evidence of excess serious adverse events. Effects were consistent across patient characteristics, including time since the index event and baseline level of blood pressure. The early accrual and durability of benefit after treatment initiation strengthens the case for sustained long-term blood pressure control as the cornerstone of secondary prevention after intracerebral haemorrhage.
