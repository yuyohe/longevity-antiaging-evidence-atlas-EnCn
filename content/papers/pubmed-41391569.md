## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: In vivo studies demonstrated that LY3537021 reduced body weight and improved glycemia during a glucose challenge in rats. The phase 1 study demonstrated that the long-acting GIPR agonist LY3537021 was well tolerated, induced weight loss, and improved glucose control in humans. These observations better define the therapeutic benefit of long-acting GIPR agonists and support a distinct contribution of GIP agonism to the benefits observed with multi-agonist peptides that act via the GIPR. Future studies are needed in more diverse populations and in cohorts with overweight/obesity to confirm these findings.
