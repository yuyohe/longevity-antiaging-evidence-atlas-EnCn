## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Based on the final inclusion of 13 randomized controlled trials, combining BWE with conventional rehabilitation programs was found to significantly reduce pain intensity (Hedges' g = -0.997, 95% CI = -1.373 to -0.620) and improve disability (Hedges' g = -1.015, 95% CI = -1.326 to -0.703). Subgroup analyses revealed larger effect sizes for comfortable walking speed, and treadmill walking. Significant differences were observed across all control group comparisons (p < .05).
