## Main Finding / 主要发现

EN: Background/Objectives: Cirrhosis is associated with increased mortality. In this study, we aimed to investigate the prognostic relevance of 6-min-walk-distance- and cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET)-derived peak oxygen uptake (VO2) as estimates of exercise capacity in outpatients with cirrhosis. Methods: Patients underwent a comprehensive clinical characterization including cardiopulmonary exercise testing, six-minute-walking-test-derived distance, and echocardiography. We stratified the cohort using established prognostic thresholds for the six-minute-walking-test-derived distance (440 m) and peak VO2 (65% predicted) and Child-Pugh class (A vs. B/C).
