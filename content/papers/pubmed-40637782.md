## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Six studies (five RCTs, one cohort) including 4043 patients were analyzed. Five studies evaluated semaglutide and one tirzepatide. GLP-1 RAs reduced the composite outcome of all-cause mortality and HF hospitalization by 27% (HR 0.73; 95% CI: 0.60-0.90; I2 = 0%). Subgroup analyses revealed greater benefits in patients with atrial fibrillation.
