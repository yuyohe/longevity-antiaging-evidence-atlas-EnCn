## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The cohort included 609 patients with CS and 3018 controls. Pre-existing AF/AFL was more common among patients with CS than controls (3.6% vs. 2.1%; OR 1.70, 95% CI 1.04-2.78). During a mean follow-up of 15 years, patients with CS had a significantly higher risk of developing new-onset AF/AFL compared with controls (HR 1.55, 95% CI 1.19-2.03). This increased risk was observed in both Cushing's disease (CD) (HR 1.53, 95% CI 1.01-2.32) and adrenal CS (HR 1.70, 95% CI 1.06-2.74). AF/AFL risk did not significantly differ according to remission status, although a trend toward lower risk was observed. Multivariate analysis identified older age at diagnosis, male sex, hypertension, vascular disease, and higher BMI as predictors for new-onset AF/AFL.

## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: CS is associated with an increased risk of AF/AFL. This elevated risk is observed across both CD and adrenal CS and persists despite disease remission, underscoring the need for heightened awareness and close cardiovascular surveillance in this population.
