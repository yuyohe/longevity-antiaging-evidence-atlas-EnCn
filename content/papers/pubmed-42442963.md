## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 15 studies were included. MBS demonstrated superior effects compared with pharmacological therapies in reducing MACE and improving most cardiovascular risk factors. This benefit remained significant at 5years (odds rati = .66, 95% confidence interval: .59 to .73) and continued to increase over time. Regarding cardiovascular risk factors, MBS showed greater improvements than pharmacological therapies during follow-up periods of 0-1 year (HbA1c, TC, LDL-C), 0-2 years (TG, HDL-C), 2-5 years (SBP, DBP), and 0-5 years (FBG).
