## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among the 9470 studies identified, nine studies comparing acute-care settings and 21 studies on exercise interventions (one in HaH) were included. Findings suggest that HaH may positively affect functional and cognitive outcomes. Inpatient exercise interventions significantly improved physical performance [standardised mean difference (SMD) = 0.42, 95% confidence interval (CI) = 0.12-0.72] and functional independence (SMD = 0.45, 95% CI = 0.14-0.77) at discharge.
