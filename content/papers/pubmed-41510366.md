## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Seventy-three participants completed the study (beta-glucan: n = 34; placebo: n = 39). After 8 weeks, the beta-glucan group showed greater reductions in body weight, BMI, BMI-for-age z-score, waist circumference, and hip circumference compared with placebo (all adjusted-p-values < 0.01). For selected biochemical outcomes, unadjusted between-group comparisons showed significantly greater reductions in fasting blood sugar (p-value = 0.04), triglycerides (p-value = 0.02), and ALT (p-value = 0.01) in the beta-glucan group. No significant differences were observed for insulin, HOMA-IR, LDL-C, HDL-C, or total cholesterol. Total energy intake decreased more substantially in the beta-glucan group than in the placebo group (p < 0.001).
