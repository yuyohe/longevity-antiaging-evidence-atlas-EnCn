## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 3296 individuals were ultimately included in the study cohort. Over a median follow-up of 82 months (IQR, 44-129), 912 participants (27.7%) died, with 315 (9.6%) from cardiovascular disease. A nonlinear association between TyG index and mortality was observed, with inflection points at 8.9 for all-cause mortality and 9.0 for cardiovascular mortality. Hazard ratios (95% CI) were 0.85 (0.62-1.16) and 1.38 (1.14-1.67) for all-cause mortality and 0.70 (0.44-1.01) and 1.78 (1.27-2.48) for cardiovascular mortality, below and above these points, respectively. In participants aged < 65 years, elevated TyG index levels showed significantly greater susceptibility to all-cause mortality [HR = 1.69 (1.20-2.39), p < 0.003, interaction p = 0.01].
