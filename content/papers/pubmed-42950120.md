## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among patients with dementia, 30-day mortality was lower in patients receiving donepezil 5 mg compared with matched untreated controls (10.3% vs 13.2%; p = 0.00046) and similarly for 10 mg (10.3% vs 13.4%; p = 0.0014). Among patients with dementia and diabetes, donepezil-metformin use was associated with the lowest mortality, approaching levels in matched individuals without dementia or diabetes. Metformin or donepezil alone was associated with modest reductions. Among donepezil users, those with diabetes treated with metformin had lower mortality than those without diabetes (30-day: 7.6% vs 10.2%, p = 0.016; significant only after FDR correction). No significant mortality differences by diabetes or metformin use were observed among dementia patients not receiving donepezil.
