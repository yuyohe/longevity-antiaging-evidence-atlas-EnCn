## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over a median follow-up of 4.85 years, there were 438 deaths, including 151 because of CVD. Sitting ≥8 h/d was associated with higher risks of all-cause mortality (hazard ratio [HR], 1.73; 95% confidence interval [CI], 1.25-2.40) and CVD-specific mortality (HR, 1.97; 95% CI, 1.04-3.74). A statistically significant interaction between sitting time and MVPA was observed for all-cause mortality (P for interaction=.006).
