## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 2428 participants were included (mean age 62.05, SE 0.33 years; n=1218, 50.15% female). During a median follow-up of 6.75 (IQR 4.20-8.90) years, among the 2428 patients, 719 (29.6%) deaths occurred. The random forest model demonstrated superior performance (AUC=0.873, 95% CI 0.856-0.891) compared to light gradient boosting machine (AUC=0.785), decision tree (AUC=0.732), extreme gradient boosting (AUC=0.792), and logistic regression (AUC=0.783).
