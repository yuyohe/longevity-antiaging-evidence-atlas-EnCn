## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In both sexes fibrinogen and IL-6 demonstrated linear increases across the age range, hs-CRP remained relatively stable, ImmActScore followed a sigmoidal curve relative to age, while TNF-α exhibited a late-life acceleration in females. Significant sex-by-age interactions were observed for TNF-α, hs-CRP and ImmActScore, indicating sex-specific shape in the age curves. Chronological age was independently associated with elevated mean circulating levels of IL-6, TNF-α and fibrinogen, but not hs-CRP. Comparison across age groups demonstrated that the transition from an immune activation-free to an activated state, as assessed by ImmActScore, was detected at approximately 35 years of age for females and 26 years for males.
