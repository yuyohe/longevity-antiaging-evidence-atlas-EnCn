## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 1927 eligible patients (average age: 56.4 ± 10.5 years; 298 [15.5%] women), 52.6% had OSA. The prevalence of OSA did not increase with age. During 2.9 years (interquartile range: 1.5 years to 3.6 years) of follow-up, the risk increase effect of OSA versus non-OSA on MACE gradually decreased with age and was no longer pronounced in the subpopulations age 58 years and older.
