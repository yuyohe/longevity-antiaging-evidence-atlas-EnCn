EN: Obstructive Sleep Apnea is a prevalent condition linked to various health issues, including cardiovascular disease and cognitive decline. This systematic review evaluates the comparative efficacy and patient adherence of two primary treatment modalities: Continuous Positive Airway Pressure and Mandibular Advancement Devices. This review incorporates studies from 2004 to 2024, applying Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses guidelines and focusing on randomised controlled trials. Continuous Positive Airway Pressure is widely recognised as the gold standard for treating moderate to severe OSA, effectively reducing the Apnea-Hypopnea Index and improving oxygenation.
