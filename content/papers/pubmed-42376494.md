EN: Canagliflozin (RR 0.62, 95% CI 0.40-0.97), empagliflozin (0.70, 0.51-0.95), dapagliflozin (0.76, 0.63-0.92), and injectable semaglutide (0.64, 0.49-0.84) were associated with lower ACOS risk than control.
