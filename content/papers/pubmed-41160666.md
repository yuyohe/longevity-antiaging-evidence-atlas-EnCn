## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Time-restricted eating (TRE) is a promising strategy to improve metabolic outcomes. However, it remains unclear whether TRE has cardiometabolic benefits in an isocaloric setting and whether its effects depend on the eating timing. We conducted a randomized crossover trial in 31 women with overweight or obesity to directly compare the effects of a 2-week early TRE (eTRE; eating from 8:00 to 16:00) and a 2-week late TRE (lTRE; eating from 13:00 to 21:00) on insulin sensitivity, cardiometabolic risk factors, and the internal circadian phase. During the restricted 8-hour eating period, participants were asked to consume their habitual food quality and quantity.
