## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The overall prevalence of osteosarcopenia was 26.7% (95% CI 22.6-31.3%). Prevalence was similar in women and men (26.3% vs 28.6%, p = 0.68). On multivariable analysis, older age (per 1-year increase; OR 1.06, 95% CI 1.02-1.10), lower BMI (per 1 kg/m2 increase; OR 0.57, 95% CI 0.50-0.65), coronary artery disease (OR 2.07, 95% CI 1.09-3.95), and diabetes (OR 2.48, 95% CI 1.35-4.56) were independently associated with osteosarcopenia.
