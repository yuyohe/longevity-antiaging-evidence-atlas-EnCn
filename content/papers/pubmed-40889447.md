## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A review of 23 studies involving 27,585 participants found that OF in community-dwelling older adults was associated with physical frailty (OR = 1.78, 95% CI: 1.19, 2.68), sarcopenia (OR = 2.01, 95% CI: 1.64, 2.47), falls (OR = 1.58, 95% CI: 1.37, 1.82), high oral microbial counts (OR = 3.05, 95% CI: 1.35, 6.88), malnutrition (OR=2.18, 95% CI: 1.63, 2.92), and low dietary diversity (OR=1.98, 95% CI: 1.15, 3.39).
