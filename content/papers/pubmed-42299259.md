EN: We identified four distinct clusters containing 44% ('healthy'), 45% ('suboptimal'), 4% ('dyslipidaemics') and 7% ('hypertensives') of the study sample, respectively. During a mean follow-up of 26 years, 61 241 died from an NCD.Compared with the cluster with the healthiest profile, other clusters had markedly shorter survival across sex and different birth cohorts.
