## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: D + Q improved kidney function and reduced markers of kidney injury (glomerular and tubular), fibrosis, senescence (p16Ink4a), macrophage- and senescence-associated inflammation (versus diabetic controls) without altering glucose levels. Additionally, geroprotective factors (α-Klotho, Sirtuin-1) increased. D + Q treatment in vitro reduced high glucose-induced senescence and inflammation (NF-κB) in HK2, HUVECs, and macrophages.
