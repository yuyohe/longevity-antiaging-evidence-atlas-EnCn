## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: After matching, each treatment group included 11,466 patients without T2DM and 7,327 patients with T2DM. MBS was associated with a lower risk of the composite cardiovascular outcome than semaglutide therapy among patients without T2DM (4.4% vs 6.6%; hazard ratio [HR], 0.402; 95% CI, 0.356-0.454) and with T2DM (9.0% vs 14.6%; HR, 0.421; 95% CI, 0.381-0.465).
