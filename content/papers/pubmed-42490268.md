## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Six of the 10 studies adjusted for total energy intake, and three adjusted for macronutrient intake. Meta-analysis showed that higher consumption of UPFs was significantly associated with an increased risk of frailty (pooled OR = 1.43, 95% confidence interval [1.02, 2.005], p = .041). Narrative synthesis further supported a positive association between UPF intake and frailty.
