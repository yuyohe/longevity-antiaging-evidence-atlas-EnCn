EN: Positive associations were observed between levels of nicotinic acid (NA, a NAD+ precursor in the Preiss-Handler pathway) and right- or left-ear hearing thresholds at frequencies of 1000 Hz (right: r = 0.480, p = 0.001; left: r = 0.422, p = 0.003), 2000 Hz (right: r = 0.507, p < 0.001, left: r = 0.629, p < 0.001), and 4000 Hz (left: r = 0.366, p = 0.029).
