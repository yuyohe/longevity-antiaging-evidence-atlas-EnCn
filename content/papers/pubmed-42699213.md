## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 7769 participants enrolled from 5 regions, the median age was 50 years, and 2419 (31%) were women. The prevalence of baseline hypertension was 36% (n = 2780) with geographic variation. Among these, 1911 (69%) individuals had a preexisting hypertension diagnosis; the remainder (n = 869, 31%) were undiagnosed. Region and traditional CVD risk factors were associated with hypertension prevalence, with obese and overweight body mass index having the highest adjusted prevalence ratios (1.80 [95% CI, 1.67-1.95] and 1.38 [95% CI, 1.28-1.49], respectively). Among those with preexisting hypertension, 40% were uncontrolled, with regional variation. Hypertension at baseline was associated with an increased hazard of MACE (hazard ratio, 2.00; 95% CI, 1.57-2.55) and key components of MACE, including myocardial infarction and stroke.
