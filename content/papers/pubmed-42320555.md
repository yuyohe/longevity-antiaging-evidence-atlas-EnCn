## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Considering all participants, the changes in fat mass ranged from -11.0 to +7.0 kg or -9.6 to +5.7 percentage points. Changes in fat mass were positively associated with changes in circulating endotrophin (unstandardized B-coefficient [95% CI] = 0.76 [0.45, 1.07] ng/mL/kg and 0.89 [0.51, 1.28] ng/mL/percentage point, P < 0.001). Exploratory mediation analyses showed that endotrophin mediated 15% [4, 51] of the effect of fat mass on total cholesterol.
