## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Galangin attenuated UVB-induced oxidative stress, cellular senescence, MMP accumulation, and collagen degradation in HDFs. Pharmacological inhibition of autophagy or silencing of SIRT1 or LC3 abolished the protective effects of galangin. Mechanistically, galangin promoted SIRT1-dependent deacetylation of LC3, thereby enhancing autophagic flux and facilitating MMP clearance. Moreover, the deacetylation-mimetic LC3 mutant (K49R/K51R) enhanced autophagy and recapitulated the protective effects of galangin, whereas the acetylation-mimetic mutant (K49Q/K51Q) attenuated these effects. In UVB-irradiated mouse skin, topical galangin increased SIRT1 expression while reducing MMP-1, MMP-3, and 8-OHdG levels.
