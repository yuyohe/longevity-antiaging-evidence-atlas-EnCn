## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Seventy-one participants were randomized (35 CPAP, 36 control). LAVI did not change significantly from baseline to 12 months in either group, and the between-group difference in 12-month LAVI change (control-CPAP) was nonsignificant (mean difference, 0.5 mL/m2 [95% CI, -3.2 to 4.1 mL/m2]; P=0.804). A linear mixed-effects model for LAVI revealed a significant group×time interaction (P=0.005). NT-proBNP declined in the CPAP group (P=0.029) without between-group differences. Among 63 participants who completed follow-up, a significant quadratic interaction was observed, with a downward LAVI trajectory in the CPAP group and an upward trajectory in controls after 6 months (P=0.020). A modified Rankin Scale of 0 at 12 months was more frequent with CPAP (46.9% versus 22.6%; P=0.043).
