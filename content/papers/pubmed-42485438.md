EN: Sickle cell disease (SCD) is a blood disorder affecting millions worldwide. Emerging evidence reveals that SCD pathophysiology increases the risk of myeloid malignancies and hematopoietic stem cell (HSC) dysfunction, likely because of chronic stress on bone marrow.
