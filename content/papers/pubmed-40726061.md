## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 43 included studies, high UPF consumption was consistently associated with increased frailty risk, multimorbidity, systemic inflammation and sarcopenia. A dose-dependent relationship was evident, with higher UPF intake linked to reduced grip strength, slower gait speed, elevated inflammatory markers (CRP, IL-6, TNF-α) and lower muscle mass indices (MMI, AMMI). Mechanisms include chronic inflammation, metabolic dysregulation and muscle deterioration. UPFs also contribute to cardiometabolic disease and obesity, reinforcing frailty as a cumulative outcome of prolonged exposure. Some studies reported inconsistencies, particularly regarding obesity and physical activity as potential confounders.
