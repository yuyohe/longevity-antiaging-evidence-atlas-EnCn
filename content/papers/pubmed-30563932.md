## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Eighty-eight women received metformin (n = 45) or placebo (n = 43) and completed treatment. There was no overall difference in posttreatment Ki-67 between the metformin and placebo arms, in an ANCOVA analysis adjusting for baseline Ki-67 expression (mean difference -0.57%; 95% CI, -7.57%-6.42%; P = 0.87). Metformin did not affect expression of markers of the PI3K-Akt-mTOR or insulin signaling pathways, and did not result in weight loss.
