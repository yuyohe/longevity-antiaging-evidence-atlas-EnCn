## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 408 patients were randomly assigned (205 metformin, 203 placebo). After a median follow-up of 36 months, 144 participants experienced progression (70 metformin, 74 placebo), with no significant difference in PFS (hazard ratio [HR], 1.09 [95% CI, 0.79 to 1.52]; P = .59). Negative biopsy rates at 36 months were 41.0% (metformin) versus 31.1% (placebo; P = .181).
