## Main Finding / 主要发现

EN: Hypertension in midlife is associated with increased dementia risk, yet its relationship with early pathological changes of Alzheimer's disease and related dementias (ADRD) remains unclear. Unlike office blood pressure (BP), 24-h ambulatory BP (ABPM) may better reflect cardiovascular risk, but associations between 24-h BP measures with ADRD biomarkers are unknown. This cross-sectional study examined associations between BP and cerebrospinal fluid biomarkers of ADRD in mid-to-late life. Dementia-free participants (n = 77; mean age = 67 [SD, 6]; 39% women) from the community-based Brain and Cognitive Health (BACH) cohort underwent office and 24-h ABPM assessments to calculate mean BP, nocturnal dipping, blood pressure variability (BPV, coefficient of variation) across 24-h, awake and asleep periods.
