## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 1269 patients (median age, 81 years; 57.1% men), 275 died during follow-up. Kaplan-Meier analysis revealed a stepwise increase in death across the lowest handgrip tertiles, which remained significant after multivariable adjustment (tertile 2 versus tertile 1: hazard ratio [HR], 1.64 [95% CI, 1.14-2.37]; P=0.007; tertile 3 versus tertile 1: HR, 2.03 [95% CI, 1.42-2.90]; P<0.001).
