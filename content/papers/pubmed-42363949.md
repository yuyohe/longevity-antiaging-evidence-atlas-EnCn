## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During a median follow-up time of 12.3 years, 11,672 premature mortalities were documented. Compared to women who did not have a spontaneous miscarriage, the multivariable-adjusted hazard ratios (aHRs) for all-cause premature mortality according to the number of spontaneous miscarriages were 0.98 (95% confidence interval [CI] 0.93-1.04) for one, 1.16 (1.06-1.27) for two, and 1.24 (1.10-1.39) for three or more, respectively (P-trend < 0.001). In contrast, the ever-versus-never comparison (any miscarriage) was not significant after adjustment for lifestyle factors. We found a significant interaction on an additive scale between a greater number of spontaneous miscarriages and the unhealthy lifestyle score (P for additive interaction = 0.03) on the risk of premature mortality.
