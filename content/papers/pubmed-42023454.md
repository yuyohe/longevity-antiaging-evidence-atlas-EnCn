## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The cohort comprised 61 (40.67%) moderate-to-severe and 89 (59.33%) mild impairment patients. Compared with the mild impairment group, the moderate-to-severe group showed significantly poorer subjective (higher AIS) and objective sleep profiles, including reduced total sleep time, efficiency, and N2/N3 sleep and increased N1 sleep, latency and awakenings (p < 0.05).
