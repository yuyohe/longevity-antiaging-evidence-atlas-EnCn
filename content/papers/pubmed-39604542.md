## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During up to 5-year follow-up, a total of 265 deaths (8.08%) were documented. Both increased levels of ANGPTL8 and RC were associated with a higher risk of death for all-cause, CVD, and cancer risk. The level of RC ≥ 33 mg/dL could identify individuals at a higher risk of death independent of low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) levels in Chinese general populations. Furthermore, RC significantly mediated the relationship between increased ANGPTL8 levels and higher risk of all-cause, CVD, and cancer death (proportion of mediation effect: 13.10%, 9.22%, and 6.07%, respectively, all p < 0.05).
