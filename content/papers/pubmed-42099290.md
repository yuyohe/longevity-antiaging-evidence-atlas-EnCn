## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Nineteen studies with 84 212 patients (mean follow-up: 68.9 [±13.3] months) were included. Common preoperative cardiovascular comorbidities included hypertension (74.4%), dyslipidemia (43.6%), and coronary artery disease (27.6%). At five years, the mean all-cause mortality was 29.78%, and cardiovascular mortality was 11.98%, with an annual increase of 6.59% and 2.46%, respectively (R2=0.809, p<0.001 and R2=0.824, p<0.001).
