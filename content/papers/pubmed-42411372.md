## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Ten studies involving 10,573 patients were included. Preoperative cognitive impairment was consistently associated with adverse postoperative outcomes, including postoperative delirium, postoperative cognitive dysfunction, long-term cognitive decline, nonhome discharge, and mortality. The pooled analysis demonstrated a significant association between preoperative cognitive impairment and adverse postoperative outcomes (OR 2.38; 95% CI 1.78-2.98; P < 0.001). Although substantial heterogeneity was observed (I² = 76.7%), the direction of effect was consistent across studies.
