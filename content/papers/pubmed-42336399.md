## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: In this large-scale, observational, real-world study of wearable users, the probability of tobacco use declined over the first 72 weeks of adoption. Among participants with end-of-follow-up data, more than one-quarter reported no tobacco use during Q6. Tobacco use was consistently associated with less favorable cardiopulmonary and sleep measures, while reductions in tobacco use over time co-occurred with directionally favorable changes in these measures, although such changes may also reflect broader lifestyle or health changes. Overall, this study provides large-scale, longitudinal evidence that sustained reductions in tobacco use co-occur with favorable changes in physiological markers within a digital self-monitoring environment.
