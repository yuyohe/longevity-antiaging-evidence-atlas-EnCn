## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Metformin users had lower serum B12 (569.3 vs. 825.3 pg/mL; p = 0.026) but better cognitive performance (DSST 47.5 vs. 39.5; p < 0.001). Fully adjusted regression showed a 3.75-point higher DSST score with metformin (95% CI -0.06 to 7.55; p = 0.053), significant under multiple imputation (β = 3.49; p = 0.006). Across mediation frameworks, 95.1% of the effect was direct and B12/MMA-independent. Benefit was concentrated in adults aged 60-69 years (interaction p = 0.003) and lower DSST quantiles.
