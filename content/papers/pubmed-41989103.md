## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Overall, 19 670 patients met selection criteria (6545 PCSK9i mAb initiators; 13 125 non-initiators). Baseline characteristics were well-balanced. Under ITT, estimated 5-year event rates were 17.5% [95% confidence interval (CI) 15.5%, 19.5%] with PCSK9i mAb vs 25.4% (95% CI 23.6%, 27.1%) without PCSK9i, yielding a RRR of 30.9% and ARR of 7.8%. Individual endpoints showed RRRs of 28.3% for myocardial infarction (P < .0001), 26.4% for ischaemic stroke (P = .02), and 28.5% for all-cause mortality (P < .0001). Among initiators, mean baseline and follow-up LDL-C were 117.8 and 54.7 mg/dL (on-treatment analysis), respectively, representing an absolute reduction of 63.1 mg/dL and percent reduction of 53.6%.
