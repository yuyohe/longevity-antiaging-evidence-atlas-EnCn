EN: The final dataset consisted of 25 estimates of VO2peak from 12 trials. ## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Workplace-based physical activity interventions consisting of at least moderate-intensity activity improve cardiorespiratory fitness. At the present time, we surmise that no single group of employees (e.g. older employees or less fit individuals) can be definitively identified as standing to benefit more from workplace physical activity interventions than others.
