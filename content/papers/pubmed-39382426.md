## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Immune cell alterations may play a role in the development of atrial fibrillation (AF). Our objective was to comprehensively characterize immune cells in AF, and investigate the potential mechanisms. Single-cell RNA sequencing and multicolour flow cytometry revealed that T cells constituted the most significant subset alterations in AF, and senescent CD8+ T cells were AF-associated subset. Senescent CD8+ T cells increased in both peripheral veins (P < 0.0001) and the left atria (P < 0.05) in patients with AF compared to non-AF control. Senescent CD8+ T cells were independently associated with AF prevalence (odds ratio = 2.876, P < 0.05) and postprocedural recurrence (hazard ratio = 22.955, P < 0.0001) using a cross-sectional study and a subsequent prospective cohort study.
