## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 7462 eligible participants were included in the analysis. Hyperuricemia was positively associated with frailty status (OR = 1.54, 95% CI: 1.02-5.84) and with the incidence of stroke (OR = 4.07, 95% CI: 1.25-13.19). Pri-frail and frail participants exhibited a substantially higher risk of incident stroke (OR = 1.55, 95% CI: 1.11-2.92 and OR = 2.87, 95% CI: 1.29-6.37, respectively).
