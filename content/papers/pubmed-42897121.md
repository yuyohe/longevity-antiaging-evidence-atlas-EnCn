## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among this cohort of 48 658 adults, 14.5% were started on semaglutide using high doses. The off-label high-dose regimen was associated with decreased 1-year persistence (p = 0.01), and a 25% increase in early discontinuation risk (HR: 1.25, 95% CI: 1.17-1.34, p < 0.001) independently of age, obesity status, co-morbidity measures and cost. Odds of high-dose initiation were increased in the West and South (p < 0.001), for endocrinologists (p = 0.02), for patients who were male (p < 0.001), younger (p = 0.02), with obesity versus overweight (p = 0.002), and for treatment initiation in 2018 compared with 2019-2021 (p < 0.001).
