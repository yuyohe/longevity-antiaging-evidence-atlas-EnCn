## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Parturition depends on precise communication between the mother and fetus. While fetal lung signals are known to help initiate labor, the role of the placenta has remained unclear. Here we show that in steroid receptor coactivator (Src)-1 and -2 double-knockout mice, reduced placental amine oxidase, copper-containing 1 (Aoc1) leads to increased spermidine levels. In trophoblast cells, spermidine induces autophagy via hypusination of eukaryotic translation initiation factor 5 A (EIF5A), reducing estrogen and prostaglandin production.
