## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Compared with controls, taurine-associated metabolites were consistently downregulated across the encephalitis groups. Arginine and proline metabolism were identified as commonly dysregulated pathways in the encephalitis groups. Highly similar metabolic network topologies were observed between VE and AE, despite different key node metabolites (lysophosphatidylcholine vs. acylcarnitine, respectively). Three diagnostic models developed to distinguish VE from other groups achieved an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve > 0.8 in the training and validation sets. Glial fibrillary acidic protein antibody-positive AE exhibited a unique carnitine metabolism signature. Longitudinal follow-up identified seven metabolites significantly associated with clinical remission in patients with AE.
