## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Compared to healthy sleep patterns, poor sleep patterns were linked to higher all-cause mortality risk in hypertensive individuals (HR = 1.22 95% CI: 1.10-1.35). Specifically, long sleep duration (HR = 1.32 95% CI:1.18-1.49), sleep difficulties (HR = 1.11 95% CI: 1.02-1.20), and sleep disorders (HR = 1.25 95% CI: 1.11-1.42) increased mortality risk respectively. Among short sleepers, a duration of ≤ 4 h conferred a 24% greater risk relative to 7-9 h of sleep (HR = 1.24 95% CI: 1.07-1.45).
