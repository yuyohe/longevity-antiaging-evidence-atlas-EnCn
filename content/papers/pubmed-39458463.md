## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The study enrolled 201 patients (non-oral frailty group, 123; oral frailty group, 78). After 1 year, the oral frailty group had a significantly higher proportion of patients with worsening nutrition status (GNRI, p = 0.0011; NRI-JH, p = 0.0019; MNA-SF, p < 0.001) and sarcopenia (p = 0.0024). There was no significant between-group difference in the proportion of patients in a worse risk category for physical frailty after 1 year.
