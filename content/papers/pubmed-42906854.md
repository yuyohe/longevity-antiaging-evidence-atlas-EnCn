## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Evidence supports a hierarchical but certainty-graded model. Protein-anchored strategies (≥1.0-1.2 g/kg/day, leucine-enriched, paired with resistance training) and vitamin D repletion in deficient older adults are the most robustly supported (moderate-to-high certainty) and align with existing guideline thresholds. Mediterranean-style diets provide population-level protection (moderate certainty, largely observational). The gut-muscle axis is an emerging fourth pillar (low-to-very-low certainty) requiring large-scale standardised trials before clinical adoption. Recommendations are restricted to the general aging population (≥60 y); prescriptive gut-microbiota targeting is proposed as a research priority rather than current practice. Heterogeneity in diagnostic criteria and outcome reporting remains the key barrier to firm clinical recommendations.
