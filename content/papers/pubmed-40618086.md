## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Based on the inverse variance weighted method, the forward MR analysis showed that intrinsic epigenetic age acceleration (IEAA) was associated with an increased risk of allergic asthma (OR = 1.051, 95% CI 1.006 to 1.098, p = 0.025). The reverse MR analysis also indicated a significant causal relationship between allergic asthma and IEAA (OR = 1.410, 95% CI 1.111 to 1.791, p = 0.005). However, there was a lack of evidence supporting a causal relationship between IEAA and allergic conjunctivitis, atopic dermatitis, allergic rhinitis and allergic urticaria (all p > 0.05). Quality control assessments demonstrated that our study results were reliable and robust.
