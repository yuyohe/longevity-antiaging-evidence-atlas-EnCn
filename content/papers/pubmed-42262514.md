## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Dietary intervention did not influence dementia mortality (n=1,386) (HR: 0.94, 95% CI: 0.85-1.05), with similar findings for Alzheimer (HR: 1.00, 95% CI 0.85-1.17) and non-Alzheimer dementia mortality (HR: 0.90, 95% CI 0.77-1.05).
