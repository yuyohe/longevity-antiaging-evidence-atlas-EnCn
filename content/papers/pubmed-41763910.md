## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Three studies with 3088 patients (mean age 64 ± 11, 37% female) were selected. Obicetrapib significantly reduced LDL-C (-31.75%), non-HDL-C (-29.35%), triglyceride (-5.61%), Lp(a) (-35.67%), ApoB (-19.37%), and increased HDL-C (+125.94%) with no difference in total cholesterol levels. Obicetrapib was not associated with increased risk for any AE, AE leading to discontinuation, AKI, transaminase or CK elevation, and hypertension.
