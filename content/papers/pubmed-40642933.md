## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The study cohort had a median (IQR) age of 46.0 (32.0, 60.0) years, with 7949 (51.8%) males. During a median follow-up of 101.0 months (IQR: 67-137), 1771 (8.8%) all-cause mortality events were observed; 443 (2.1%) deaths were attributed to cardiovascular diseases, while 109 (0.5%) resulted from cerebrovascular diseases. Low apoB and LDL-cholesterol levels were independently linked to an elevated risk of all-cause and cardiovascular mortality. Compared with participants having apoB <90 mg/dL and LDL-cholesterol levels between 100-129 mg/dL, those with LDL-cholesterol <70 mg/dL (HR, 1.81; 95%CI: 1.39-2.36) and 70-99 mg/dL (HR, 1.28; 95%CI: 1.01-1.62) demonstrated a higher risk of all-cause mortality. Additionally, reduced apoB levels contributed to an increased risk of cardiovascular mortality among individuals with low LDL-cholesterol levels.
