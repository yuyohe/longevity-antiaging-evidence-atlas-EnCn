EN: OCT showed delayed endothelialization at 6 months in patients above 65 years compared to younger cohorts (uncovered struts, 11.7% vs. 6.1%; P < 0.001), along with accelerated late-phase NIH progression (3.33 vs. 1.67 μm/month; P < 0.001).
