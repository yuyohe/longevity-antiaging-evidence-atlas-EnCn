## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During a median 15.3 years, 10 233 of 267 335 participants (mean age 56.5 ± 8.1 years; 44.6% men) developed HF. Groups with confirmed sarcopenia (n=1993) and low handgrip strength (normalized to body mass index) only (n=18 796) were associated with higher HF risk (hazard ratio [HR], 1.63 [95% CI, 1.44-1.85]; HR, 1.76 [95% CI, 1.66-1.85]) compared with the reference group, with stronger effects observed in younger adults and women.
