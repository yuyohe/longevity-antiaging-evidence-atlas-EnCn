## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of the 32 articles included, 7 explored the association between oral health or function-related factors and physical disability, 22 investigated mortality outcomes, and 3 examined both outcomes. In total, 441,508 participants were included. Edentulism (OR, 1.73, 95% CI [1.48-2.01], I2 = 0%) was significantly associated with physical disability. Regarding mortality, significant risk factors included reduced swallowing function (OR, 2.11, 95% CI [1.88-2.37], I2 = 0%), oral frailty (OR, 2.06, 95% CI [1.87-2.27], I2 = 0%), fewer than 20 teeth (OR, 2.04, 95% CI [1.67-2.49], I2 = 89.7%), edentulism (OR, 1.87, 95% CI [1.35-2.59], I2 = 97.0%), and chewing difficulty (OR, 1.77, 95% CI [1.08-2.91], I2 = 95.9%).
