## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Twenty-two randomized clinical trials with 920 participants were included in the review; 17 studies were included in the meta-analysis. Findings indicated that interventions meeting physical activity guidelines for at least 4 weeks significantly reduced fatigue severity (standardized mean difference = -1.46; 95% CI = -2.11 to -0.81) and fatigue impact measured with the modified Fatigue Impact Scale (mean difference = -11.88; 95% CI = -20.57 to -3.19) and Fatigue Impact Scale (mean difference = -21.08; 95% CI = -31.01 to -11.15). All findings were clinically relevant, with effect sizes exceeding the established minimal clinically important differences for the fatigue measures. Some methodological concerns were noted, and the evidence level ranged from very low to moderate.
