## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Median age was 69 years, and 360 patients were men. Nocturnal hypertension was present in 301 patients. During a median follow-up of 4.2 years, 50 patients experienced heart failure hospitalization and 31 died. At 4 years, the cumulative incidence of heart failure hospitalization was 7.4% versus 2.2% in patients with versus without nocturnal hypertension, and that of all-cause death was 7.7% versus 4.0%. Per 10-mmHg higher mean nocturnal systolic blood pressure, the subdistribution hazard ratio for heart failure hospitalization was 1.24 (95% CI, 1.08-1.42), or 1.19 (1.04-1.37) after adjustment for office systolic blood pressure; the corresponding subdistribution hazard ratio for office systolic blood pressure was 1.24 (0.99-1.55).
