## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: For single-task TUG, a significant main effect of time was observed (p < .001), with similar improvements in both groups (0.62-0.80 s). Dual-task TUG performance improved over time for both cognitive conditions, with significant group × time interactions favoring the IRT group (p ≤ .049). Dual-task cost did not change significantly. Balance confidence improved significantly over time in both groups (mean increase ≈14 points; p < .001), whereas concern about falling remained unchanged.
