## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Multi-organ biological aging clocks derived from clinical phenotypes and neuroimaging data have emerged as valuable tools for studying human aging and disease. Plasma proteomics provides an additional molecular dimension to enrich these clocks. In this study, I developed 11 multi-organ proteome-based biological age gaps (ProtBAGs) using 2,448 plasma proteins from 43,498 participants in the UK Biobank. Here I highlight methodological and clinical considerations for developing and using these clocks, including correction for age bias, organ specificity of proteins, sample size and underlying pathologies in the training data, which can affect model generalizability and clinical interpretability.
