## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 37 participants (64%) were assigned to NR-NR, and 21 participants (36%) to PBO-NR. There was a 32.4% and 51.4% dropout in the NR-NR group at 10 weeks and 20 weeks, respectively, vs. 14.3% dropout at each timepoint in the PBO-NR group. In the NR-NR group, NAD+ levels increased by 2.6- to 3.1-fold after 5-10 weeks of supplementation, respectively, and remained elevated at 20 weeks.
