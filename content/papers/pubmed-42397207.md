## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Differences (mean ± SEM) between Stage A/B (n = 109) and Stage C HF (n = 52) were, respectively: stroke volume index (SVI) 36.8 vs. 31.9 mL/m2 (P < .001), heart rate (HR) 68.1 vs. 76.4 bpm (P < .001), and arterial stiffness (pulse pressure/SVI) 1.47 vs. 1.65 mmHg/mL/m2 (P < .05); cardiac index and total vascular resistance index did not differ between groups. Robust inverse correlations between SVI and HR were found in both Stage A/B and Stage C HF (P < .001); each 10 beat/min change in HR was accompanied by a reciprocal SVI change of 3 mL/m2; overall, SVI was 5%-10% lower in Stage C compared to Stage A/B HF. Hemodynamic profiles, especially high HR and low SVI, were quantitatively similar in Stage C HF patients with low or high EF. Low SVI (<35 mL/min/m2) conferred a specificity for Stage C HF of 71%; adding high HR (>75 bpm) increased specificity to 84%.
