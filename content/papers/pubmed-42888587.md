## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 95 male patients (mean age: 48.12 ± 10.21 years) completed the study. Baseline characteristics were comparable between the groups, except for heart rate. After adjustment for baseline heart rate using mixed analysis of covariance, no significant group × time interaction was observed for heart rate (P = .476, partial ηp2 = .008). Similarly, no significant group × time interactions were observed for systolic blood pressure, diastolic blood pressure, or peripheral oxygen saturation (all P > .05). Although significant time effects were observed for some physiological parameters, the magnitude of these effects varied across outcomes, and none was accompanied by a significant group × time interaction. Therefore, these temporal changes were not interpreted as intervention-specific effects. No adverse events were reported.

## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Heart rate changed significantly over time following the interventions; however, after adjustment for baseline heart rate, the pattern of change did not differ significantly between the fascial mobilization and control groups. Similarly, no significant between-group differences in changes over time were observed for blood pressure or oxygen saturation. These findings do not demonstrate a specific acute effect of multiregional fascial mobilization on the physiological parameters assessed. Further studies using advanced measures of autonomic function and longer follow-up periods are warranted.
