## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: At 12 months, weight loss ranged from 5.0 to 5.8 kg between groups. Between months 12 and 24, most participants either maintained weight (n = 112) or continued to lose weight (n = 58), whereas a smaller group regained >5% of baseline weight (n = 20). This group had the largest initial caloric reductions. Weight regain reversed improvements in insulin area under the curve and the ratio of insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1) to insulin-like growth factor-binding protein 1, and sustained weight loss maintained metabolic benefits and was associated with greater reductions in biological age.
