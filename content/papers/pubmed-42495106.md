## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Fifteen studies with a total of 5048 patients, predominantly (>95%) with transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy (ATTR-CM), were included. The prevalence of frailty ranged between 6.7% and 75% across studies with a pooled mean of 33.9% (826/2435 frail). Pooling 2141 patients with outcomes available, both pre-frailty (RR 2.22, 95% CI 1.37-3.60, p < 0.01) and frailty (RR 3.93, 95% CI 2.09-7.40, p < 0.01) significantly associated with increased risks of all-cause mortality after adjustment for age and frailty assessment tool. 6MWD, a functional surrogate of frailty, predicted poorer outcomes both using baseline 6MWD (<350/300 m: HR 2.22, 95% CI 1.15-4.31, p = 0.02) and 1-year interval change in 6MWD (>35 reduction: HR 1.80, 95% CI 1.52-2.14; >5% reduction: HR 1.89, 95% CI 1.60-2.23; both p < 0.01).
