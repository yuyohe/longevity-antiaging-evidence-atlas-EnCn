## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The 27 cohort studies included in the meta-analysis included 3,162,083 participants. Compared to the control group, the pooled HR of all-cause mortality was 1.19 (95 % CI, 0.86-1.65) for mild OSA, 1.28 (0.96-1.69) for moderate OSA, and 2.13 (1.68-2.68) for severe OSA. The pooled HR of cardiovascular mortality was 1.24 (0.53-2.55) for mild OSA, 2.05 (0.57-5.47) for moderate OSA, and 2.73 (1.94-3.85) for severe OSA. All-cause mortality (HR 0.66; 0.59-0.73) and cardiovascular mortality (HR 0.37; 0.16-0.54) were significantly lower in CPAP-treated than in untreated patients. There were no differences in cardiovascular mortality in CPAP-treated OSA patients vs. normal control subjects (HR 0.82; 0.52-1.29).
