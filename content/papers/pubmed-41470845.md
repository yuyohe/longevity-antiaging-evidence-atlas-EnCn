## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In the overall analysis (primary combined cohort), the SMI showed no significant change between the HSP and control soymilk groups (p = 0.18); however, a significant difference in the change in the SMI between the HSP and control groups was revealed for pre-frail elderly subjects (mean difference in SMI change: 0.08 kg/m2 [95% CI 0.004, 0.15], p = 0.044). Furthermore, in four distinct subgroups restricted to the pre-frail elderly individuals-step counts (≥5000 steps/day), TEE (≥30 kcal/kg BW/day), TEI (≥30 kcal/kg BW/day), and male sex-the HSP group also showed significant differences in SMI change.
