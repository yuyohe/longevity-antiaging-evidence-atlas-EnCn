## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Semaglutide improved PCS at weeks 15 and 30, with effect sizes exceeding the minimally important difference. Indirect effects via weight loss were positive but not statistically significant, although at week 30 approximately half of the total PCS effect was estimated to be mediated through weight change. No total or mediated effects were found for MCS. For PANSS-6, no meaningful mediation was observed.
