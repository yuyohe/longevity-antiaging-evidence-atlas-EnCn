## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: In adults with insomnia with diverse clinical backgrounds, blue light therapy may provide modest improvement in sleep quality and certain sleep parameters related to advancement and continuity of sleep (sleep fragmentation, sleep onset latency, waking after sleep onset, number of awakenings, and sleep efficiency), and may alleviate excessive daytime sleepiness. These findings suggest that blue light therapy may be considered potential non-pharmacological treatment for insomnia that can be implemented by healthcare providers in home or clinical settings. However, these findings should be interpreted with caution. Further studies are needed to confirm these findings.
