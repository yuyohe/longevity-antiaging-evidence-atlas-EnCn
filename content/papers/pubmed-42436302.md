EN: Social frailty is defined as the risk of losing the necessary resources to meet basic social needs. This study aimed to assess social frailty and its associated factors in older adults in Kashan in 2023. In this cross-sectional study, 250 older adults were selected through multistage random sampling from comprehensive health centers. Data collection instruments included a demographic questionnaire, abbreviated mental test, social frailty scale, Lubben social network scale, and a specific quality of life scale.
