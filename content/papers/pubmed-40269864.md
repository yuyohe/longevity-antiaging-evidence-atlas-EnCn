EN: After an average follow-up of 12.55 years, healthy sleep score group were associated with an decreased all-cause mortality risk compared to poor/intermediate sleep score group (HR: 0.873; 95% CI: 0.767-0.995). ## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Poor sleep quality is associated with a elevated risk of all-cause mortality. Stroke survivors meeting recommended MVPA levels exhibit lower mortality risk, even among those with poor sleep quality. Future intervention studies are needed to establish whether increasing PA to recommended levels among stroke survivors directly reduces mortality risk linked to poor sleep quality.
