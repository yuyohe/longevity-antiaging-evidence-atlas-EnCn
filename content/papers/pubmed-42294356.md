EN: Among 124,836 participants (mean [SD] age 56.2 [7.8] years; 55.8% women), there were 5780 deaths, 3420 T2DM cases, 6078 CVD cases, and 9437 cancer cases. Higher adherence to healthy plant-based diets-whether high- or low-UPF-was associated with 8-28% lower risk of all-cause mortality [HRQ4vsQ1 (95% CI): high-UPF hPDI, 0.92 (0.85-1.00); low-UPF hPDI, 0.91 (0.84-0.98)] and type 2 diabetes [high-UPF hPDI, 0.89 (0.79-0.99); low-UPF hPDI, 0.72 (0.65-0.79)]. Higher adherence to the high-UPF hPDI was also associated with 11% lower cardiovascular disease risk [0.89 (0.82-0.96)], while no clear association was observed for the low-UPF hPDI. Nutrient quality was similar across high- and low-UPF hPDI patterns.
