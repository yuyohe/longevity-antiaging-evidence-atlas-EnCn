## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 437 hemodialysis patients with positive blood cultures were identified, yielding 709 microbial isolates. The study population had a median age of 58 years, and obesity was prevalent (41.7% with BMI >30 kg/m 2 ). CVCs were present in 92.7% of BSI episodes, while arteriovenous fistulas or grafts accounted for 7.3%. Hypertension (88%) and diabetes mellitus (60%) were the most common comorbidities. Staphylococcus aureus was the most frequent gram-positive pathogen, while Enterobacter cloacae and Klebsiella pneumoniae were the leading gram-negative isolates.
