## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 14 independent reports, including 1,055,309 subjects and 9457 events, were analyzed. The pooled HR (95% CI) was 1.27 (95% CI, 1.19-1.36) for total cholesterol, 1.21 (95% CI, 1.09-1.35) for low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C), and 0.60 (95% CI, 0.50-0.72) for high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL-C). We observed a linear association between serum cholesterol (TC, HDL-C) levels and CVD mortality in this meta-analysis.
