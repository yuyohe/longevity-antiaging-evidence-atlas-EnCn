## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Protein lactylation has emerged as a crucial regulator in the pathogenesis of Alzheimer’s disease (AD). Although the Klotho gene (KL) has been clinically linked to AD, its role in lactylation remains unclear. Here, we investigated the influence of Klotho genotype on lactylation and related inflammatory responses.
