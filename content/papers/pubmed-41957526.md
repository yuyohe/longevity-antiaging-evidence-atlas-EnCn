## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Here we show that specific gut microbial co-abundance group is associated with plasma acetic acid levels. Higher plasma levels of acetic acid are associated with lower plasma triglyceride levels, higher high-density lipoprotein cholesterol levels, lower body mass index, lower body fat mass, higher thalamic volume, and higher cognitive performance in certain domains. Additionally, we show that plasma acetic acid mediates the relationship between gut microbiome on these health measures.
