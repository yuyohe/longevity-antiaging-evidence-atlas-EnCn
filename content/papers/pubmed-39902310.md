## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 11 eligible cohort studies (13 datasets) were eventually included in this meta-analysis. Participants in the highest quintile of both the PDI and hPDI had a significantly decreased risk of all-cause mortality (pooled HRPDI = 0.85; 95% CI: 0.80-0.90; pooled HRhPDI = 0.86; 95% CI: 0.81-0.92) compared to participants in the lowest quintile. In contrast, the highest uPDI was associated with an increased risk of mortality (pooled HRuPDI = 1.20; 95% CI: 1.11-1.31). Dose-response meta-analysis showed that there was a non-linear relationship between PDI or hPDI level and all-cause mortality (p = 0.001 and p < 0.001 for non-linearity, respectively). No evidence of a non-linear relationship was observed for uPDI (p = 0.596 for non-linearity).
