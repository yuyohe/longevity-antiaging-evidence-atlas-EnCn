## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 43.2% of adults engaged in 1+ CAM practice. After full adjustment and FDR correction, yoga (HR=0.73, 95% CI: 0.62-0.87) and Pilates (HR=0.64, 95% CI: 0.49-0.85) were associated with lower mortality. Each additional CAM practice was associated with 6% lower mortality (HR=0.94, 95% CI: 0.92-0.96). Recent users had lower mortality than past-only users (HR=0.79 vs. 0.90), and multiple practice users showed the strongest association (HR=0.76, 95% CI: 0.68-0.86). Exploratory subgroup analyses revealed suggestive but largely non-robust patterns.
