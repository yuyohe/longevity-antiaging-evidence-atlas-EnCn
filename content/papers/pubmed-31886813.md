## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: We included 41 studies with a total of 34955 participants. The prevalence of sarcopenia in community-dwelling individuals in the included studies were 11% (95% CI: 8-13%) in men and 9% (95% CI: 7-11%) in women. The prevalence of sarcopenia in nursing-home individuals in the included studies were 51% (95% CI: 37-66%) in men and 31% (95% CI: 22-42%) in women and in hospitalized individuals were 23% (95%, CI: 15-30%) in men and 24% (95% CI: 14-35%) in women.
