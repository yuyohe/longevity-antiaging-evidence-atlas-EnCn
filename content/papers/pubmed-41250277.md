## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Preclinical studies suggest that taurine may exert neuroprotective effects. However, its relevance to dementia risk in human populations remains unclear. We investigated the associations between mid-life dietary taurine intake, circulating taurine concentrations, and the risk of late-life all-cause dementia, Alzheimer's disease (AD), and vascular dementia (VaD) in a large prospective cohort. This study utilized data from 27 786 participants of the Malmö Diet and Cancer Study with baseline examination from 1991 to 1996. Dietary taurine intake was estimated from a detailed diet history and adjusted for energy intake. Plasma taurine concentration was measured in a subset of 3693 individuals.
