EN: Use of BZDs and Z-drugs was associated with increased odds of AD, with variation across drug classes and pharmacokinetic profiles. Short-acting agents, BZDs overall, and Z-drugs were associated with higher risk, whereas broad-acting BZDs were not; this finding should be interpreted with caution given subgroup heterogeneity and limited statistical power.
