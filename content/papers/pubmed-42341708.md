## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Studies (n = 38) involving 8551 adults that investigated the lifestyle-integrated functional exercise, zero-time exercise, walking, and standing categories were included. The results of the meta-analysis indicated that the interventions significantly improved moderate-to-vigorous physical activity, vigorous physical activity, sedentary time, step counts, step time, systolic blood pressure, self-efficacy, and depression. EN: Unstructured physical activity interventions provide a convenient approach to improve adults' physical activity and psychological outcomes.
