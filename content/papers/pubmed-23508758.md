EN: There is an ongoing controversy regarding the safety and effectiveness of metformin in the setting of heart failure (HF). ## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: The totality of evidence indicates that metformin is at least as safe as other glucose-lowering treatments in patients with diabetes mellitus and HF and even in those with reduced left ventricular ejection fraction or concomitant chronic kidney disease. Until trial data become available, metformin should be considered the treatment of choice for patients with diabetes mellitus and HF.
