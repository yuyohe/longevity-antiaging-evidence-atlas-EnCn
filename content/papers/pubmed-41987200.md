## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A lytic phage, designated S18-3, was isolated from environmental water using E. hormaechei strain HZW1 isolated from diseased broiler liver. Phage S18-3 forms clear plaques (~ 0.6 cm) and exhibits an icosahedral head with a long tail. Optimal infectivity occurs at an MOI of 0.1. S18-3 remains stable from 30 to 50 °C and tolerates a broad pH range (pH 4-11). Whole-genome sequencing revealed no known virulence factors or antimicrobial resistance genes, indicating favorable genetic safety. In a murine intestinal infection model established by antibiotic pretreatment and oral challenge, E. hormaechei caused significant weight loss and mortality. Oral administration of S18-3 markedly improved survival and mitigated weight loss compared with infected controls. EN: Phage S18-3 is a stable, genetically safe lytic phage that effectively alleviates intestinal E. hormaechei infection in a murine model without observable adverse effects.
