## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: High glucose impairs cognitive function in type 2 diabetes, but the underlying mechanism is unclear. In this study, guided by lactylome analysis, we reveal that high glucose induces LRPPRC K223 lactylation in hippocampal neurons by upregulating lactyltransferase AARS2, which weakens LRPPRC-SLIRP binding, reduces mitochondrial mRNA stability, subsequently leads to mitochondrial dysfunction, and ultimately results in neuronal apoptosis and cognitive decline. Notably, a novel short peptide designed to competitively inhibit LRPPRC K223 lactylation remarkably ameliorates cognitive impairment in diabetic mice.
