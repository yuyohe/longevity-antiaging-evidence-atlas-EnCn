## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 2396 patients, 65.3% were robust, 18.0% pre-frail, and 16.7% frail. Increasing frailty severity was independently associated with lower odds of 1-year excellent functional outcome (pre-frail: odds ratio [OR], 0.6; 95% CI, 0.4-0.8; frail: OR, 0.2; 95% CI, 0.2-0.3; P for trend <0.001). Frail patients also had higher stroke recurrence risk (hazard ratio [HR], 1.90; 95% CI, 1.33-2.71) and lower likelihood of favorable functional outcome. Similar dose-response relationships were observed at 3 and 6 months, with consistent findings in sensitivity and subgroup analyses.
