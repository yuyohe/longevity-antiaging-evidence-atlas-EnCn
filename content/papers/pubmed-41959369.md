EN: Nutrient metabolism influences HIV-1 replication, antiviral immunity, and chronic inflammation, yet is difficult to leverage for therapeutic gain. We sought to modulate metabolism in the non-human primate model of HIV-1 by caloric restriction (CR), a modality canonically known for its antiaging benefits. Four months of 30% CR was safe and resulted in broad and systemic metabolic reprogramming in healthy adult male and female rhesus macaques.
