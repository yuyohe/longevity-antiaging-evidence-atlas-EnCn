## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Cross-sectionally, higher adherence to the continuous MedDiet was associated with better global cognition, and higher adherence to the continuous MedDiet and DASH was associated with better executive function. Substantial between-study heterogeneity was observed for DASH, MIND, and AHEI-2010. Greater consumption of red and processed meat was associated with poorer executive function. Longitudinal analyses showed limited and inconsistent findings. Only the MIND diet, assessed in three U.S. cohorts, was associated with more favorable executive function trajectories.
