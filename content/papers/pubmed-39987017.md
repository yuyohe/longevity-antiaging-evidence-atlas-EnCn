## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of the participants, 31.37% were frail, 17.2% had pMCI, and 8.64% exhibited both conditions. The cohort was stratified into four groups based on frailty and pMCI status. After a median follow-up period of 6.5 years, frail individuals with pMCI had the highest all-cause (75.23 per 1,000 person-years) and CVD (32.97 per 1,000 person-years) mortality rates.
