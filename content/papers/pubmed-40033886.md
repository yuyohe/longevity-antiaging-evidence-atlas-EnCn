EN: The presence of senescent cells causes age-related pathologies since their removal by genetic or pharmacological means, as well as possibly by exercise, improves outcomes in animal models. An alternative to depleting such cells would be to rejuvenate them to promote their return to a replicative state. Here we report that treatment of non-growing senescent cells with low-frequency ultrasound (LFU) rejuvenates the cells for growth. Notably, there are 15 characteristics of senescent cells that are reversed by LFU, including senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP) plus decreased cell and organelle motility.
