## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Age-associated changes in organelle structure are often viewed as passive deterioration. Our recent work challenges this view by identifying an evolutionarily conserved, age-onset remodeling of the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) that is actively driven by ER-phagy. Across multiple cell types and organisms, the ER undergoes a reduction in volume and a shift from rough ER sheets to tubular networks. ER compositional shifts accompany these changes in morphology, with declines of the proteostasis machineries enriched within rough ER and preservation of lipid-associated enzymes tied to tubular subdomains.
