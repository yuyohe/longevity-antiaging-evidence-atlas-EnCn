## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Higher NEE was independently associated with significantly lower StO₂ (B = - 0.0931, β=-0.193, p = 0.001), while MAP showed no significant correlation with StO₂. Patients in the highest NEE quartile (> 0.28) exhibited the lowest StO₂ and the highest 30-day mortality (41.8%). StO₂ partially mediated the relationship between vasopressor load and arterial lactate. StO₂ generally improved after shock reversal defined as NEE ≤ 0.05, lactate < 2mmol/l, MAP ≥ 65mmHg for at least 24 h (+ 5.8%, p < 0.001).
