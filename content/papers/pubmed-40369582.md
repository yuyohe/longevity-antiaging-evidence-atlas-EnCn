EN: In CHARLS, the median follow-up period was 9.00 years, with a baseline age of 58 (52, 65) years. Obesity, KDM-BAacc, and CVD were all significantly associated with each other. For each 1-year increase in KDM-BAacc, the risk of incident stroke, heart disease and CVD increased by 68% (OR 1.68, 95% CI 1.35-2.09), 35% (OR 1.35, 95% CI 1.15-1.59), and 44% (OR 1.44, 95% CI 1.25-1.65), respectively.
