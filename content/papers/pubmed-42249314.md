## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Mortality among patients with TB/HIV-coinfected in Kigali remains high, particularly during the early treatment period. Predictors of mortality included older age, lack of TPT and CPT, comorbidities, undernutrition, fair ART adherence, low CD4 count and extrapulmonary TB. Increasing TB/HIV integrated care through improved adherence support, nutritional supplementation, comorbidity management, increased uptake of cotrimoxazole and TPT, and timely interventions are critical to reduce mortality.
