## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 6065 ICU patients were analysed (mean age: 74.4 ± 12.7 years; 63.6% male). The prevalence of GLIM-defined malnutrition was 85.4% (n = 5181). Compared with no malnutrition, severe malnutrition was independently associated with higher in-hospital mortality (subdistribution hazard ratio 1.58 [1.16-2.15], p = 0.004), higher overall mortality (hazard ratio 1.62 [1.32-1.99], p < 0.001), and longer length of hospital stay (+4.09 [2.72-5.46] days, p < 0.001).
