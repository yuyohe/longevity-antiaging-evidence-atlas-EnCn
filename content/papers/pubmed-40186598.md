## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During 15.9 years of follow-up, 24,310 individuals (53.2%) died. The adjusted association between CRF and mortality risk was inverse and graded. For each 1-MET increase in CRF, the adjusted HR for mortality was 12% lower (HR, 0.88; 95% CI, 0.875 to 0.885; P<.001). When risk was assessed across CRF categories using the least-fit CRF category as the referent, the adjusted HRs and CIs were 0.76 (95% CI, 0.73 to 0.78), 0.63 (95% CI, 0.61 to 0.66), 0.49 (95% CI, 0.47 to 0.51), and 0.33 (95% CI, 0.30 to 0.35), for low-fit, moderate-fit, fit, and high-fit individuals, respectively. The pattern of the CRF mortality risk association was similar regardless of age, race, or sex.
