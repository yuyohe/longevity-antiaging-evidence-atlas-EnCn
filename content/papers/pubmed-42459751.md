## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Data from 272,594 patients (either sex, aged between 20 and 106 years) were analyzed, excluding those undergoing cardiac, brain, and obstetric surgery. Mortality did not differ significantly between the four hypertension groups and the true normotension group. Compared with the true normotension group, the odds of ischemic heart disease were higher in the controlled, Stage 2, and Stage 3 hypertension groups. The odds of cerebrovascular disease and acute renal failure increased with hypertension stage, whereas the odds of aortic dissection were elevated only in Stage 3 hypertension.
