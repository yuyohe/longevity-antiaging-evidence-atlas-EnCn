## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 616 patients were included (mean age, 81.3 years; mean BMI, 24.0 kg/m2). The 180-day cumulative incidence of SGLT2i-specific AE-related discontinuation was 9.1%. Multivariable analysis identified Clinical Frailty Scale ≥5 (hazard ratio [HR], 4.67; P < 0.001) and BMI <22 kg/m2 (HR, 2.53; P = 0.003) as independent predictors of discontinuation.

## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: SGLT2 inhibitors were generally well tolerated among adults aged ≥75 years.
