## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Sarcopenia, the age-related loss of muscle mass and quality, is an indicator of frailty that can be objectively measured using computed tomography (CT) images acquired for other routine indications. CT-based muscle mass has been shown to predict adverse outcomes in many diseases, although evidence in acute pulmonary embolism (PE) is scant. A retrospective cohort study included patients ≥ 70 years diagnosed with acute PE between 2015 and 2019. Axial CT images at the carina and mid-thigh were used to measure chest muscle area (CMA), thigh muscle area (TMA), and thigh intramuscular fat area (TFMA) using semi-automated segmentation tools.
