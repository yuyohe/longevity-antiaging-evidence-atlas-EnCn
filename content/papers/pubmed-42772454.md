## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Non-inferiority of the vegetarian diet was not established for protein intake (-0.33 g/kg/d; 95% CI -0.49, -0.17) and appendicular muscle mass (-0.33 kg; 95% CI -0.69, 0.03). Both groups met their protein intake and plant:animal protein ratio targets: 1.24 (1.14, 1.35) g/kg/d and 76% plant-based protein in the vegetarian group, and 1.58 (1.48, 1.69) g/kg/d and 67% animal-based protein in the omnivorous group. Appendicular muscle mass significantly increased in both the vegetarian [+0.38 (0.12, 0.64) kg, p=0.004] and the omnivorous group [+0.66 (0.40, 0.93) kg, p<0.001]. Leg press strength and chair stand test significantly improved in both groups, with no between-group differences.
