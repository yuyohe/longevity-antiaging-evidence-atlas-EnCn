## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 25 783 semaglutide 2.4 mg users and 8 948 913 non-users were identified. Semaglutide 2.4 mg was associated with significantly lower risks for incident prediabetes or type 2 diabetes, type 2 diabetes only, hypertension, ASCVD, heart failure and CVD (HRs 0.45-0.75; all p < 0.05), as well as greater improvements in BMI (-4.1 kg/m2), SBP (-6.2 mmHg), HbA1c (-0.4%), LDL cholesterol (-7.7 mg/dL), triglycerides (-28.6 mg/dL) and HDL cholesterol (+1.9 mg/dL; all p < 0.001). At 12 months, semaglutide 2.4 mg users (N = 161) demonstrated a greater reduction in ASCVD risk score (-0.7% vs. +0.3%; between-group difference -1.0%, p < 0.001), with a decrease in the proportion at intermediate-high ASCVD risk (14.9%-9.3% vs. 17.7%-18.7%; OR 0.3 [95% CI: 0.1, 0.6], p < 0.001).
