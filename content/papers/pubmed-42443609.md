## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Brain white matter undergoes structural and functional alterations linked to late-life cognitive decline, but the cellular and molecular basis of its selective vulnerability remains incompletely defined. Here, in naturally aged mice, we demonstrate that senescent and disease-associated microglia (DAM) phenotypes converge in hippocampal-adjacent white matter, particularly in the fimbria.
