## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 590 participants (mean age: 70.3 y; females: 65%) included in the analysis, 116 (19.7%) showed no improvement, 224 (38.0%) showed modest improvement, and 250 (42.3%) showed substantial improvement in their MIND diet score. Compared with participants with no change in MIND diet score, those with greater improvement (mean absolute increase of 4 points on the 0-14 scale) demonstrated a significant annual increase in global cognitive composite scores [β = 0.035; 95% confidence interval (CI): 0.006, 0.064], along with higher plasma lutein-zeaxanthin (β = 0.088; 95% CI: 0.038, 0.137) and β-carotene (β = 0.075; 95% CI: 0.001, 0.150). Modest improvement was not associated with significant changes in cognitive function. The associations between changes in MIND diet score and cognition were not modified by dietary intervention arm (P for interaction > 0.167).
