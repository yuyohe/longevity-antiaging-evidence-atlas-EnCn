## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Axillary percutaneous ventricular assist devices (pVADs) are increasingly utilized for refractory cardiogenic shock, yet standardized mobilization protocols are lacking. This study describes a structured mobilization protocol and evaluates its implementation in 196 patients supported with an axillary pVAD from December 2020 to June 2025. Of this cohort, 131 (67%) were mobilized per a progressive multidisciplinary exercise protocol. Mobilized patients achieved significantly higher functional status by intensive care unit (ICU) discharge via Johns-Hopkins Highest Level of Mobility (JH-HLM) scoring (p < 0.001) and hospital discharge (p = 0.002).
