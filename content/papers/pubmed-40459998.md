## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Werner syndrome (WS) is a rare hereditary progeroid syndrome caused by mutations in the WRN gene. Patients frequently develop various age-associated diseases prematurely, often leading to early mortality (≤ 60 years of age). Depletion of nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD)+ has been reported in patients with WS, suggesting a key role in the pathogenesis of WS. NAD+ supplementation may improve the condition of WS and other accelerated aging diseases. Therefore, we conducted a double-blind, randomized, crossover, placebo-controlled trial in patients with WS to evaluate the safety and efficacy of the NAD+ precursor, nicotinamide riboside (NR).
