EN: We confirmed and extended the relationship between glucose homeostasis and Blastocystis spp. and subtypes ST1 and ST4, showing its association with glucose and insulin levels in all cohorts. Importantly, we observed that glucose homeostasis may shape Blastocystis spp. abundance in the gut, rather than the reverse, based on clinical trial data showing that metformin (not placebo) increased Blastocystis spp. in recently diagnosed T2D patients.
