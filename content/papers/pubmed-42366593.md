## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Evidence regarding statin adherence among patients with hypertension in primary care is limited. We assessed statin adherence in Swedish primary care and examined associations between patient characteristics and adherence. In this retrospective register-based cohort study from a large Swedish region, data from all primary health care centres were linked to data on comorbidities, dispensed drugs, and socioeconomic factors. Patients with hypertension who initiated statin therapy in 2012-2015 were included. Adherence during 2 years after initiation was measured using the proportion of days covered (PDC). Explainable machine learning with XGBoost and SHAP values identified baseline characteristics associated with adherence.
