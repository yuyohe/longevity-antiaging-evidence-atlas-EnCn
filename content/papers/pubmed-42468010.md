EN: Seven RCTs reported in 8 articles were included; 6 contributed to outcome-specific meta-analyses, and 1 was analyzed descriptively. After short-term training, URT showed no significant improvements over SRT in lower limb strength and mobility outcomes; for the Five-Times Sit-to-Stand test, SRT demonstrated a nonsignificant trend toward better performance than URT.
