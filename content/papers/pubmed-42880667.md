## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Diabetes was present in 36.9 % of patients. ACS hospitalization rates declined by 8.3 % annually (95 % CI 6.1-10.4), with no diabetes-by-time interaction (p = 0.17). In-hospital case fatality was 6.3 % and did not differ by diabetes status. During a median 4.9-year follow-up, diabetes was associated with higher all-cause mortality (adjusted HR 1.22, 95 % CI 1.11-1.33), with a stronger association in women than men (interaction p = 0.009). Only 28.9 % achieved LDL-C < 55 mg/dL, with no temporal improvement; female sex independently predicted poor LDL-C control (adjusted OR 1.65, 95 % CI 1.38-1.97).
