EN: A total of 88 eligible patients with obesity and NAFLD (mean [SD] age, 32.0 [9.5] years; 49 men [56%]; and mean [SD] body mass index, 32.2 [3.3]) were randomly assigned to the TRE (n = 45) or DCR (n = 43) group. The IHTG content was reduced by 8.3% (95% CI, -10.0% to -6.6%) in the TRE group and 8.1% (95% CI, -9.8% to -6.4%) in the DCR group at the 6-month assessment. In addition, liver stiffness, body weight, and metabolic risk factors were significantly and comparably reduced in both groups.
