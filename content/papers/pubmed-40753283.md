## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Extremes of sleep duration and occasional or frequent insomnia symptoms were associated with higher risks for incident type 2 diabetes. In the UK Biobank and replication cohorts, adding sleep traits to the QDiabetes risk score did not improve type 2 diabetes prediction (C statistic: QDiabetes alone 0.8933; QDiabetes + sleep duration 0.8939; QDiabetes + insomnia 0.8931; QDiabetes + sleep traits 0.8935).
