## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among participants (4446 males, 5309 females; mean age 48.55 (SD 9.53) years), the prevalence of tinnitus and hypertension was 7.4% and 19.3%, respectively. Hypertension was significantly associated with higher odds of tinnitus (adjusted OR=1.34; 95% CI 1.10 to 1.62). Notably, even participants with controlled hypertension had a 27% increased odds (OR=1.27; 95% CI 1.02 to 1.59) compared with normotensive individuals. The odds were highest in those with uncontrolled grade II hypertension (OR=2.08; 95% CI 1.25 to 3.47), demonstrating a dose-response relationship.
