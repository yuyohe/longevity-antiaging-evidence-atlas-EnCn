## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: There was no significant difference in the proportion of hospitalizations for stroke between those who received the influenza vaccine and those who did not (7.50 per 1000 vs. 8.12 per 1000; adjusted sHR: 0.96, 95 % CI: 0.90-1.02).
