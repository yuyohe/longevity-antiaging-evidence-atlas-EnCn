## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over a median 13.6 years, 2551 (7.5%) MACE events occurred. In continuous models, IL-6 and ApoB were each independently associated with MACE (IL-6 HR 1.11 per SD, 95% CI 1.07-1.16; ApoB HR 1.12 per SD, 95% CI 1.07-1.17), and a simultaneous 1-SD increase in both biomarkers conferred 22% higher MACE risk. Associations were endpoint-specific: ApoB was strongest for MI (HR 1.20, 1.14-1.27), IL-6 for cardiovascular death (HR 1.20, 1.12-1.29) and all-cause mortality (HR 1.17, 1.14-1.20).
