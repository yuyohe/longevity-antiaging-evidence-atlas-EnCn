## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The interaction between VO2peak and KL-VSHET was significant for tTau (P=0.05), pTau181 (P=0.03), Ng (P=0.02), sTREM2 (P=0.03), and YKL-40 (P=0.03), such that lower levels of each biomarker were observed for KL-VSHET who were more fit.
