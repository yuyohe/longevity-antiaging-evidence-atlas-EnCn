## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: At the time of PMR intervention, women are older, have more symptoms, and worse long-term outcomes compared with men. Older female age alongside higher prevalence of ≥ moderate TR and TVr explained a significant proportion of this heterogeneity in sex-related all-cause mortality, whereas differences in left ventricular and left atrial parameters had no significant influence.
