## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Across 216 valid days median daily MVPA was 52.5 min/day (IQR: 30.9-80.2), with no significant differences by disease severity. Age was the only significant predictor of MVPA and SB: each additional year was associated with 7.6 fewer minutes of MVPA and 27.6 more minutes of SB per day (p < 0.01).
