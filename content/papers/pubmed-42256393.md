## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 31,927 eligible men, 1,803 5-ARI users were matched with 5,409 non-users (mean age 71.2 years). Long-term 5-ARI use was associated with significantly lower all-cause mortality (hazard ratio [HR]: 0.88; 95% CI: 0.79-0.94; p < 0.001). Although no significant associations were observed in patients undergoing surgery (HR: 0.94; 95% CI: 0.79-1.07) or systemic therapy (HR: 1.15; 95% CI: 0.82-1.55), a distinct survival benefit was evident in the no-surgery/no-systemic therapy group (HR: 0.82; 95% CI: 0.73-0.92; p < 0.001). Within this untreated subgroup, the benefit was most pronounced in patients with low comorbidity (CCI 0-1: HR: 0.78; 95% CI: 0.65-0.91) compared with those with high comorbidity (CCI ≥2: HR: 0.89; 95% CI: 0.76-1.03).
