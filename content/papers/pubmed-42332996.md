## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of the 73,552 participants, 1775 (2.4%) had ISH. Over a median follow-up of 12.1 years, 102 composite CVD events were documented in patients with ISH. Individuals with ISH exhibited a significantly higher risk of CVD than those in the normal group (Hazard eratio [HR], 2.10, 95% confidence interval [CI], 1.67-2.65). The CVD risk remained lower in the high-normal and IDH groups than in the ISH group, whereas SDH conferred a greater risk. Compared to the normal group, the risk of stroke (HR, 2.41, [95% CI, 1.92-3.20]) and HF (HR, 1.89, [95% CI, 1.16-3.08]) were significantly increased in the ISH group, whereas the risk of MI was not (HR, 1.17, [95% CI, 0.63-2.17]).
