## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 5,520 patients across three comparative studies were included. The cohort was 65.96% female (n=3,641), with a mean age of 55.09 ± 14.65 years, and 76.67% (n=4,232) had a BMI >30. GLP-1 RA use was generally associated with neutral or improved perioperative outcomes. Findings included lower rates of pseudarthrosis following subtalar and triple arthrodesis and reduced early hardware removal after ankle fracture fixation.
