## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Frailty prevalence was 8.5 % (76.1 ± 5.8y, 45.1 % women). The accuracy of the EC questionnaire for frailty was optimal when the total scores of 4/5 were used as the threshold. Compared to the low-risk group (<5), the high-risk group (≥5) had a significant association between frailty with an adjusted odds ratio (aOR) of 4.68 (95 %CI, 3.10-7.05). Moreover, the high-risk group also had a significant association with sarcopenia, with an aOR of 1.82 (1.27-2.61).
