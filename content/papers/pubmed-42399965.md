## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: At baseline, 30% of participants (n = 452) reported CP and 45% (n = 666) were APOE-ε4 carriers. CP and APOE-ε4 status did not independently predict baseline CSF Aβ42/Aβ40, PET Aβ burden, or CSF p-tau181. Longitudinally, the CP+/ε4 + group showed greater AD-like changes in CSF Aβ42/Aβ40 and CSF p-tau181, thinning of the precuneus (β=-0.09, p<.001) and faster decline in episodic memory (β=-0.08, p<.001), compared to all other groups. Many of these associations remained when excluding participants with MCI or restricting to Aβ-negative participants.
