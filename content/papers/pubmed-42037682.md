## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Thirteen cohort studies comprising 835,992 participants were included. The meta-analysis showed that frailty was associated with significantly higher odds of all-cause dementia (pooled OR = 1.76, 95% CI: 1.48-2.10). For Alzheimer's disease, the pooled estimate suggested increased odds but did not reach statistical significance (OR = 1.91, 95% CI: 0.86-4.20), and the evidence was limited by the small number of contributing studies (k = 4) and substantial heterogeneity.
