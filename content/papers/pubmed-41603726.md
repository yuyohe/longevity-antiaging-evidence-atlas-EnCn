## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Metformin duration (per 6 months) was not significantly associated with the overall CH growth rate in WHI [β = -0.05%/year; 95% confidence interval (CI), -0.11 to 0.01; P = 0.08; n = 543] and in BioVU (β = -0.09%/year; 95% CI, -0.22 to 0.05; P = 0.20; n = 561). EN: In our cohorts, the duration of metformin use among diabetic users was associated with a small reduction in CH growth rate (-0.06%/year), which is modest compared with typical DNMT3A clonal growth rates of 5% to 7% annually.
