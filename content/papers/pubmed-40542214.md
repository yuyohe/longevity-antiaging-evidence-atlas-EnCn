## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Cochlear aging causes substantial hearing impairment in older adults, yet primate-specific mechanisms remain poorly characterized. Our comprehensive analysis combining single-cell and histopathological profiling in aging Macaca fascicularis demonstrates progressive cochlear degeneration featuring accelerated sensory hair cell loss, senescent spiral ganglion neurons with elevated neuroinflammation, and marked stria vascularis atrophy. We discovered that downregulation of transmembrane transport proteins, particularly SLC35F1, serves as a critical biomarker of hair cell aging.
