## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of ten eligible studies on endometrial, ovarian, and cervical cancer were included, collectively reporting 3,867 deaths. High levels of post-diagnosis physical activity, compared with low levels, were associated with lower mortality (HR: 0.65; 95% CI 0.54-0.78). This inverse relationship was evident in both endometrial and ovarian cancer survivors (endometrial cancer: HR: 0.60; 95% CI 0.43-0.83; ovarian cancer: HR: 0.71; 95% CI 0.58-0.86). Medium levels of physical activity tended to be inversely associated with mortality (HR: 0.88; 95% CI 0.76-1.02).
