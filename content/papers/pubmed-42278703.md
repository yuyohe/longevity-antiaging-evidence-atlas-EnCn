## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: This study examined commercial exergames from the perspectives of emotional experience and mental health. Both commercial exergames and traditional indoor exercise were associated with improvements in immediate mood states. The results provide preliminary evidence regarding the role of digital exercise in mood regulation among older adults.
