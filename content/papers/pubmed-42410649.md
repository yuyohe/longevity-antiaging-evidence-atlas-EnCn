## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Twelve studies (2 RCTs and 10 matched cohort studies) with thirteen arms involving 418,282 participants were included. Treatment with GLP-1RAs was associated with a significantly lower risk of major limb events by 27% (thirteen arms, RR 0.73, 95% CI 0.65-0.82). Reduced risks were also observed for LEA (RR 0.76, 95% CI 0.66-0.87), revascularization (RR 0.81, 95% CI 0.77-0.86), gangrene (RR 0.80, 95% CI 0.77-0.85), MACE (RR 0.76, 95% CI 0.63-0.90) and all-cause mortality (RR 0.67, 95% CI 0.61-0.73). Subgroup and sensitivity analyses did not substantially change point estimates.
