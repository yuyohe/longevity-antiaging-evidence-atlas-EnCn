## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Reduced NAD+ levels were found in RA samples, in line with altered activity and expression of genes involved in NAD+ consumption (sirtuins, poly[ADP-ribose] polymerase, CD38), transport (connexin 43), and biosynthesis (NAMPT, NMNATs). Unsupervised clustering analysis identified a group of RA patients with the highest inflammatory profile, the lowest NAD+ levels, and the highest disease activity (as shown by the Disease Activity Score in 28 joints). NAD+ levels were modulated by anti-TNF therapy in parallel with the clinical response. In vitro studies using PBMCs from RA patients showed that nicotinamide riboside and nicotinamide increased NAD+ levels via NAMPT and NMNAT and reduced their prooxidative, proapoptotic, and proinflammatory status.
