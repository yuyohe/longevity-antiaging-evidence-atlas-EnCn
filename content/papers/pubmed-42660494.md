## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 124 777 participants (mean age 56 ± 8 years; 56% female) followed for 12 years, 2931 (2.35%) HF events and 441 (0.35%) HF deaths occurred. In Model 1, highest vs lowest adherence was associated with lower HF risk for both diets (Med: aHR 0.88, 95% CI 0.79, 0.99; DASH: aHR 0.79, 95% CI 0.70, 0.89). ## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Higher adherence to Med and DASH diets is associated with lower HF risk; but only DASH retained an independent significant association after adjusting for cardiometabolic mediators.
