## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In the unadjusted analysis, we observed modest epigenetic age deceleration estimated using the Horvath pan-tissue clock among those with endometriosis/adenomyosis compared to those without (mean difference in the z-score, -0.15; 95% confidence interval, -0.28 to 0.02). No notable differences were observed in the estimates of epigenetic age acceleration using the other established clocks, where the mean differences in the z-scores ranged between -0.10 and 0.06.
