## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In the surveys, 21,720 individuals had a BMI ≥27 kg/m2 and a SCORE2-derived baseline risk ≥7.5%. Observed 10-year CVD incidence was 13.82% (95% CI: 11.94%-15.71%). Emulated GLP-1RA therapy lowered projected CVD incidence to 10.83% (95% CI: 9.27%-12.39%), an absolute reduction of 2.99% (95% CI: 2.67%-3.31%) and a relative reduction of 22%. Absolute reductions were larger in men than in women (3.14% [95% CI: 2.82%-3.47%] vs 2.7% [95% CI: 2.23%-3.16%]), with similar potential relative reductions across sexes (21% vs 23%). Modeled risk reductions were attenuated in nonobese and lower SCORE2 groups and diminished further with lower compliance assumptions.
