## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During a mean 13.54 ± 2.78 years of follow-up, we documented 669 CRC. Multivariate Cox regression analysis showed that compared with the NMN group, MASLD participants demonstrated an increased risk of developing CRC (HR 1.54 95 %CI 1.12-2.13).
