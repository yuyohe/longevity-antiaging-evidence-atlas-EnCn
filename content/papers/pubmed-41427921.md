## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 36 trials covered varied physiotherapy and structured physical-activity regimens. Participants, mostly aged ≥60, were mostly women (approximately two-thirds of the sample). The majority of investigations found that resistance training increased muscle strength and decreased frailty by 20%-35%. Exercises involving balance reduced fall risk by 25%-35%, while aerobic workouts improved gait and cardiovascular fitness. Multimodal interventions reversed frailty in 41%-50% of individuals and improved cognitive and emotional outcomes the highest. Physical performance, quality of life and functional independence improved across frailty models in weighted summaries.
