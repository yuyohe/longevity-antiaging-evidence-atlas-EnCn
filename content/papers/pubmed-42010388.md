## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Four RCTs (n = 1615 patients) were included. Olezarsen significantly reduced triglycerides (MD -47.71%, 95% CI -56.78 to -38.64, p < 0.0001), non-HDL-C (MD -22.11%, 95% CI -28.48 to -15.75, p < 0.0001), ApoC-III (MD -68.93%, 95% CI -77.54 to -60.31, p < 0.0001), VLDL-C (MD -48.52%, 95% CI -57.16 to -39.87, p < 0.0001), and ApoB (MD -10.67%, 95% CI -16.83 to -4.51, p = 0.0007), while increasing HDL-C (MD 35.13%, 95% CI -27.30 to -42.96, p < 0.0001). LDL-C showed no significant change. The risks of any or serious adverse events were comparable to placebo. Olezarsen was associated with fewer acute pancreatitis events (p = 0.035) but higher rates of liver enzyme elevations ≥ 3× ULN (p = 0.046).
