## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Using the CT- and BIA-based diagnostic criteria, sarcopenia was diagnosed in 189 (30%) and 177 (28%) patients, respectively. The overall agreement, kappa and AC1 between the two methods were 89% (95% CI 86%-91%), 0.73 (95% CI 0.67-0.79), and 0.81 (95% CI 0.77-0.86), respectively. Using the BIA-based criteria as a non-reference standard, the positive and negative percent agreement of the CT-based criteria were 84% (95% CI 77%-89%) and 91% (95% CI 88%-93%), respectively. Patients with sarcopenia-based on either CT-or BIA-based criteria-took longer to complete the 4-m gait speed, Five Times Sit-to-Stand, and Timed Up and Go tests than those without sarcopenia.
