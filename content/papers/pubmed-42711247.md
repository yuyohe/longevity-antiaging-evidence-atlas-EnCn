## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Resistant maltodextrin (RMD) is a water-soluble dietary fiber with prebiotic properties. This study investigated the effects of RMD on skeletal muscle atrophy and associated molecular and microbial changes in eighteen-month-old mice fed a standard chow diet (SCD) or high-fat diet (HFD) for 12 weeks. RMD supplementation improved muscle strength, endurance, and muscle mass under both dietary conditions. In HFD-fed mice, RMD also reduced fat accumulation and adipocyte size. Gut microbiota analysis showed distinct responses to RMD according to dietary condition, including enrichment of Lactobacillus and Alloprevotella under SCD and Allobaculum and Akkermansia under HFD.
