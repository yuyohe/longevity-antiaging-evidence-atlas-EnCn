EN: NSCLC exhibited broad decreases in glycerophospholipids, sphingolipids, and triacylglycerols, consistent with membrane-lipid remodeling. LightGBM showed the best discrimination in internal validation. Key discriminant lipids included lysophosphatidylcholine (LPC(O-18:1)), decanoylcarnitine, and sulfatide (SL) (SL 38:5).
