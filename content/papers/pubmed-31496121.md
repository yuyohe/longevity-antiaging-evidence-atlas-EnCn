## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of the 108 255 sampled subjects, 2094 (1.9%) were diagnosed with AMD, including 214 (0.2%) with neovascular AMD, during a mean follow-up period of 5.1 ± 2.8 years. Insomnia patients were more likely to have subsequent AMD than those without insomnia (2.5% versus 1.8%, p < 0.001). Further, the incidence of exudative AMD was also higher in the insomnia cohort than the control cohort (0.3% versus 0.2%, p = 0.002). The adjusted HR was 1.33 (95% confidence interval [CI], 1.18-1.48, p < 0.001) for AMD and 1.67 (95% CI, 1.20-2.33, p = 0.002) for exudative AMD.
