## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 170,778 Medicare beneficiaries aged 66 + years of age at the start of the study period, 50.2% identified as female, 7.0% Black, 4.4% Hispanic, and 80.9% White. Fewer beneficiaries residing in high SVI (higher vulnerability) regions were diagnosed with cataracts (Quartile 4 55.9% vs. Q1 67.1%, p < 0.001) and underwent cataract surgery (Q4 19.4% vs. Q1 21.1%, p < 0.001) compared to beneficiaries in low SVI regions.
