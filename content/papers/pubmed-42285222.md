## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Eight trials with 231 hemodialysis patients were eligible; seven contributed to quantitative synthesis. Nutritional interventions involved mainly oral supplements, and exercise (resistance, aerobic, or neuromuscular electrical stimulation) was performed two to three times/week for 3-12 months. Combined therapy significantly improved the 6MWT distance (MD 45.17 m, 95% CI 12.60-77.75), gait speed (MD 0.09 m/s, 95% CI 0.02 to 0.17), and TUG time (MD -1.44 s, 95% CI -2.63 to -0.25). No significant differences were observed in the Sit-to-Stand test, lean body mass, BMI, intake, or QOL.
