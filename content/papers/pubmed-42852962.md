EN: Several HRV parameters (6 out of 11 variables) were significantly lower (P<0.05) in patients with chronic kidney disease compared to healthy controls. ## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: CKD patients with diabetes have worsened heart rate variability and cardiorespiratory fitness compared to those without diabetes. However, there is no association between heart rate variability and cardiorespiratory fitness, regardless of diabetes status. Future research should investigate other pathological systems which may be contributing to reduced cardiorespiratory fitness in the CKD population.
