EN: Over a follow-up period of 10 years, there were 429 (4.4%) deaths, including 185 cases due to CVD and 124 cases due to cancer. ## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Abnormal levels of serum lipids, specifically low HDL-C concentration, are associated with an elevated risk of both non-CVD and CVD mortality.
