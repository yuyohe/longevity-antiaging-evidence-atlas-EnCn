## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: LDL-cholesterol (LDLC) to apolipoprotein B (ApoB) ratio is an accessible and reliable proxy for the LDL-particle size. The aim of the present study was to investigate whether type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) modulates the prognostic value of LDL-C/ApoB ratio in statin-treated patients with coronary artery disease (CAD). The study included 21,382 patients with CAD who had received statin therapy at baseline with a median follow-up of 3.1 years. The primary endpoint was major adverse cardiac events (MACE), including all-cause mortality, non-fatal myocardial infarction (MI), and ischemia-driven revascularization. Higher LDL-C/ApoB level (≥1.2) was associated with a decreased risk of MACE in the T2DM group (adjusted HR: 0.81, 95% CI: 0.69-0.96).
