## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 14,694 people were included in the study, of whom 5200 used GLP-1RA. Semaglutide was associated with a 21% (aHR 0.79, 95% CI 0.69-0.91) lower risk of psychiatric hospitalisation compared with non-use of GLP-1RA in that same individual. Liraglutide and dulaglutide were not specifically associated with a lower hospitalisation risk.
