## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Eight studies with 262,952 pooled participants were included. The prevalence of frailty ranged from 14.3% to 36.4%, with a pooled estimate of 24.3% (95% CI, 19.0%-30.4%; I²=99.8%). Four test-negative studies were pooled for LCI cases. Vaccination was associated with reduced odds of LCI (pooled OR, 0.53; 95% CI, 0.37-0.76; I²=4.0%), with a 95% prediction interval of 0.30-0.96. Stratified analyses showed pooled ORs of 0.43 (95% CI, 0.24-0.75) and 0.66 (95% CI, 0.35-1.25) in non-frail and frail individuals, respectively; no statistically significant effect modification was detected (p = 0.10). Hospitalization and mortality outcomes were not pooled because of heterogeneity. The certainty of evidence was low for LCI and very low for other outcomes.
