## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over a median follow-up of approximately 10 years, 73,159 participants (15.9%) developed FCMD, 12,183 (2.6%) developed CMM, and 33,259 (7.2%) died. Compared with normal sleep duration (7-9 h/day), abnormal sleep duration (<7 or >9 h/day) was associated with higher risks of FCMD (HR = 1.04, 95% CI: 1.02-1.06), CMM (HR = 1.10, 95% CI: 1.05-1.14), and all-cause mortality (HR = 1.07, 95% CI: 1.04-1.09). Insomnia was similarly associated with higher risks of FCMD (HR = 1.08, 95% CI: 1.06-1.11), CMM (HR = 1.10, 95% CI: 1.05-1.16), and all-cause mortality (HR = 1.07, 95% CI: 1.04-1.11). Joint analyses showed a graded pattern, with the highest risks consistently observed among participants with both abnormal sleep duration and insomnia. Associations were broadly consistent across population subgroups.
