## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 200 patients, 109 (54.5%) experienced swallowing decline at discharge. The incidence decreased across energy-intake quartiles, from 68.0% to 36.0% (p for trend < 0.001). In multivariable analysis, lower energy intake was independently associated with swallowing decline (adjusted odds ratio 0.927, 95% confidence interval 0.885-0.971, p = 0.001). ROC analysis identified an optimal cutoff of 15.3 kcal/kg/day (area under the curve 0.648; sensitivity 69.6%; specificity 57.4%).
