## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Following analysis of 630 545 eligible patients (80.3% female, mean BMI 36.1 kg/m2 [SD 5.8]), mean weight loss in those with recorded weights was -13.5% (95% CI: -13.56 to -13.51) at 6 months (n = 270 309) and -17.4% (95% CI: -17.5 to -17.3) at 12 months (n = 58 434). At 6 months, 89.9% achieved ≥ 5% weight loss and 72.1% achieved ≥ 10%. More regular ordering cadence was associated with greater weight loss, and clinically meaningful outcomes were frequently achieved without escalation beyond intermediate doses. Treatment persistence was higher among patients from less deprived areas, with 26.1% more prescription orders than those from the most deprived areas. Patients reported improvements in mobility (46.7%) and mood (45.9%), and reduced alcohol consumption (26.7%).
