## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 11 studies (comprising 16 RCTs) were included, involving 604 participants (intervention group: 314; control group: 290), age range: 63.6 to 85.8 years old. Meta-analysis revealed that exercise interventions significantly increased serum IGF-1 levels in older adults with frailty and/or sarcopenia (SMD = 0.42, 95% CI: 0.23-0.60, p < 0.0001, I 2 = 15%).
