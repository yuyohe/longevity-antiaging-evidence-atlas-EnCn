## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In this study population, the mean age was 32.0 years (SD: 7.2). 65.4% were females and 21.0% were Black. Over a median 3.0-year follow-up, 507 participants developed HF. After mutual adjustment and accounting for demographic and clinical factors, extreme BMI and elevated WHtR were associated with incident HF. Specifically, the hazard ratios were 2.9 (95% CI: 1.3-6.3) for underweight, 1.8 (95% CI: 1.1-2.9) for class III obesity, and 1.8 (95% CI: 1.1-2.8) for WHtR≥0.5. These associations were observed for HFpEF but not for HFrEF. Adding WHtR to BMI modestly improved risk discrimination, suggesting incremental value beyond BMI alone for risk stratification (C-index: 0.790 vs. 0.783 for HF, and 0.880 vs. 0.873 for HFpEF).
