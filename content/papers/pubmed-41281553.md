## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Thirteen studies met the inclusion criteria. Random-effects meta-analysis revealed a significant positive association between accelerated biological aging and stroke risk (OR = 1.16, 95% CI 1.13-1.19, I 2 = 98.9%, p < 0.001). Stratified analysis by stroke event demonstrated a stronger association with incident stroke (OR = 1.28, 95% CI 1.25-1.35, I 2 = 92.6%, p = 0.001) compared to stroke recurrence (OR = 1.11, 95% CI 1.06-1.16, I 2 = 63.6%, p = 0.041). Sensitivity analyses confirmed the robustness of these findings.
