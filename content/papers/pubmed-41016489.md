## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 7 studies met the inclusion criteria (total n = 2344) enrolling both adults aged ≥ 60 years (6/7 studies) and a younger cohort (42 years [IQR 31.8-51]). The first meta-analysis, based on 4 studies (n = 1393), showed significantly higher levels of GDF-15 in sarcopenic individuals (r = 0.482).
