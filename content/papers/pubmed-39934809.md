## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: There was a decrease in peroxisomal abundance in the sciatic nerves of diabetic mice, and palmitic acid (PA) induced a reduction in peroxisomal abundance by inhibiting peroxisomal biogenesis in Schwann cells. Mechanistically, PA induced the palmitoylation of PEX11B at C25 site, disrupting its self-interaction and impeding peroxisome elongation. Fenofibrate, a PPARα agonist, effectively rescued peroxisomal dysfunction caused by PA and restored the peroxisomal abundance in diabetic mice. Lastly, MR analysis indicates a notable causal influence of DN on MS, with its onset and progression intricately linked to peroxisomal dysfunction.
