## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 8,694 eligible aged Medicare beneficiaries with persistent DMT use, 1,025 (11.79%) discontinued DMTs. The discontinuation cohort had a mean age of 69.7 ± 3.9 years, with 79.5% females and 91.1% Whites. Older age, recent years, and clinical complexity, such as frailty and disability, were associated with lower odds of discontinuation. However, patients with dual eligibility and certain comorbidities were more likely to discontinue therapy.
