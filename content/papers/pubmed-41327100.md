## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The final sample comprised 500 individuals with possible sarcopenia (mean age: 83.0 ± 3.76 years; 34.4% men) and 1,261 without possible sarcopenia (mean age: 81.0 ± 3.40 years, 51.6% men). Logistic regression demonstrated the best predictive performance among the four models, with the highest recall of 0.700 and F1-score of 0.654. The most influential predictors for possible sarcopenia were lower body mass index, walking aid use, cognitive impairment, older age, and exhaustion.
