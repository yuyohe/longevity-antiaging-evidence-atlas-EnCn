EN: Compared to participants in the lowest quintile of UPF consumption, those in the highest quintile of UPF consumption showed a reduction of 0.411 kg [95% confidence interval (CI), 0.148, 0.674] in absolute handgrip strength, a reduction of 0.229 kg/m (95% CI, 0.071, 0.387) in handgrip strength by height, and an OR of 1.32 (95% CI, 1.15, 1.53) for muscle weakness.
