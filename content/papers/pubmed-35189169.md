## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of the 13,362 participants, 3954(29.6%) were > 65 years, 5792(43%) were Caucasian, and 6773(50.7%) had hypertension. Mean(SD) Klotho was 0.85(0.31) ng/mL and pulse pressure was 55.8(18.5) mmHg. In unadjusted and adjusted models, each ng/mL increase in Klotho was associated with a 3.88mmHg (95%CI = -5.19,-2.57; P < 0.001) and 1.63mmHg (95%CI = -3.01,-0.24; P = 0.02) decrease in pulse pressure, respectively. Similarly, participants in the highest quartile of Klotho had lower pulse pressure than those in the lowest quartile (-3.05mmHg; 95%CI = -4.05,-2.05; P < 0.001), and this difference remained significant in adjusted models (-1.10mmHg; 95%CI = -2.20,-0.01; P = 0.05).
