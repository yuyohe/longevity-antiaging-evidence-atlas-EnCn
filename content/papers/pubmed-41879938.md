## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Klotho (Kl) is an anti-aging protein primarily produced in the kidney and the choroid plexus (CP, where it regulates cerebrospinal fluid composition and exerts neuroprotective effects. Here, we investigated the age-dependent consequences of a CP-specific Kl deletion on CP structure and function using mice lacking Kl exclusively in CP epithelial cells (KlΔCP). In control mice, aging markedly disrupted CP architecture and cilia organization both in the lateral (LV-CP) and fourth ventricle (FV-CP). While CP-specific Kl deletion alone caused no major structural changes it induced region- and age-dependent calcification: FV-CP calcification increased in both aged and young KlΔCP mice, whereas LV-CP calcification emerged only in older KlΔCP mice.
