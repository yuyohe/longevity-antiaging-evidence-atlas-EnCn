## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Our analysis of 33 studies (n = 939,064) found that glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP1RAs) offered the strongest protection against acute exacerbations (hazard ratio vs insulin: 0.40, 95% credible interval 0.29-0.56) and had the highest first-rank probability (72.95%), followed by sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 inhibitors (SGLT2is; 46.19%).
