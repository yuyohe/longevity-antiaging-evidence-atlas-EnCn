## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 10 625 patients were included in each matched cohort. Semaglutide was associated with statistically significant 29% (hazard ratio [HR] 0.71; p = 0.046) and 22% (HR 0.78; p = 0.040) reductions in the risk of rMACE-3 and rMACE-5, respectively, compared with tirzepatide. In the per-protocol analysis, semaglutide continued to be associated with a significantly lower risk of rMACE-3 (HR 0.43; p = 0.005) and rMACE-5 (HR 0.57; p = 0.003) compared with tirzepatide.
