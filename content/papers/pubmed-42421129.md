## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 622 children (mean age 17.9 months, 51.4% female), documented coverage of dose 1 was 56·8% (95% CI 53.5-61.7), dropping to 46·6% for dose 2 and 39·0% for dose 3. Perceived free access (aRR 1.97, 95% CI 1.47-2.64) and knowledge of a nearby vaccination facility (aRR 1.86, 95% CI 1.41-2.45) were strongly associated with uptake. In the cross-sectional analysis, children who received ≥ 2 doses were associated with a 50% lower occurrence of malaria episodes (aRR 0.50, 95% CI 0.34-0.73, p = 0.001) compared with those receiving ≤ 1 dose. Minor adverse events occurred in 27% (fever 93%, injection site swelling 10%).
