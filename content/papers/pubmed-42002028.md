## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: UHRF1, a DNA methylation regulator, was significantly downregulated in SOP-MSCs, which resulted in reduced DNA 5-mC levels and contributed to impaired osteogenesis in SOP. In terms of mechanism, UHRF1 deficiency-mediated lower DNA 5-mC levels impeded histone deacetylase 1 (HDAC1) recruitment, leading to elevated H3K27ac levels and the super-enhancers (SEs) redistribution. This epigenetic shift promoted aberrant SE formation and the overexpression of transglutaminase 2 (TGM2), which impaired autophagic flux by interfering with the interaction between Beclin1 and the endoplasmic reticulum and therefore impeded the osteogenic differentiation of SOP-MSCs. Targeting the UHRF1-TGM2 axis rescues senile osteoporosis in mice.
