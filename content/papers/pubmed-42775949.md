EN: In fully adjusted models, Stage III-IV periodontitis was associated with higher biological age acceleration compared with healthy/gingivitis/Stage I for three of the four clocks examined: PhenoAge +2.12 years (95% CI 0.64-3.59), HannumAge +1.48 years (0.34-2.62), and GrimAge2Mort +1.35 years (0.52-2.63).
