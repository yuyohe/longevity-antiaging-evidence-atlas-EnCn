## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: CIN occurred in 427 patients (33.2%). In multivariable analysis, age (OR 1.03 per year, 95% CI 1.02-1.04), chronic kidney disease (OR 5.05, 95% CI 2.64-9.63), and the IRIS score (OR 2.72 per standard deviation increase, 95% CI 2.16-3.42) were independently associated with CIN.
