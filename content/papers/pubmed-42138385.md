## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 1850 PWH at baseline, 1368 had FIB-4 <1.3 (no/low fibrosis), 428 had FIB-4 1.3-2.67 (moderate fibrosis), and 54 had FIB-4 >2.67 (advanced fibrosis). Most PWH had cardiometabolic conditions (diabetes 59%, dyslipidemia 62%, hypertension 80%, BMI ≥30 80%), drank alcohol with low levels of use (57%, mean AUDIT-C 1.6), and did not have hepatitis B (93%) or hepatitis C infection (86%). Mean time on semaglutide was 218 days. Semaglutide was associated with reductions in liver fibrosis score in PWH with baseline moderate-to-advanced fibrosis (FIB-4: ≥1.3; ΔFIB-4: -0.11, P < 0.01) with greatest decreases in advanced fibrosis (FIB-4: >2.67; ΔFIB-4: -0.64, P < 0.001).
