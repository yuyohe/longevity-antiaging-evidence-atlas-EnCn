## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Possible osteosarcopenia, observed among 50.3% of the cohort, was associated with lower IC at baseline, including cognition capacity. Furthermore, baseline possible osteosarcopenia was associated with poor cognitive function at six months post-baseline, particularly among those with loss in cognitive capacity at baseline. This was driven by impaired delayed recall and poor orientation abilities, indicators of both early onset and gradual decline of cognitive impairment. On the other hand, higher education, social networks, resilience, and quality of life were associated with better IC (at baseline) and cognition (at baseline and at six months post-baseline).
