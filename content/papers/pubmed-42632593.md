## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 145 critically ill patients were evaluated. High nutritional risk was present in 47.6% (n=69) and frailty in 37.9% (n=55) of the sample. In the adjusted model, the combination of frailty and high nutritional risk (CFS ≥5 + mNUTRIC ≥5) was significantly associated with a 2.68-fold increase in the odds of in-hospital mortality (OR 2.68; 95% CI 1.01-7.11; p=0.047). Frailty or nutritional risk alone did not show a significant association with mortality in the adjusted analysis.
