## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: In patients with MASLD, intermittent fasting was associated with greater reductions in body weight, BMI, and LDL-C than continuous energy restriction. Although CAP-based analyses suggested a possible advantage of intermittent fasting for hepatic steatosis, this finding was not confirmed by MRI-PDFF and was no longer statistically significant in sensitivity analyses. Further large-scale, long-term randomized trials are needed to confirm these findings.
