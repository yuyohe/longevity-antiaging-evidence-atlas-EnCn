## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: This study investigated whether the daily intake of jaboticaba peel and seed powder (JPSP) for three weeks could modify urolithins metabotype (UM-A, UM-B, or UM-0) and urinary excretion in Brazilian adults (n = 59). Volunteers consumed JPSP or placebo in a crossover design, and urine samples were collected at four time points. Even though most volunteers (66%) maintained their initial metabotype, several exhibited multiple metabotype changes during the trial.
