## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Eleven studies met the inclusion criteria. In a meta-regression of RCTs, all P-values for interaction were > 0.05, indicating no evidence that T2D modified treatment effects. In pooled RCT analyses, incretin-based therapies significantly reduced HF hospitalization and events (hazard ratio [HR] 0.68 [95% confidence interval {CI} 0.52-0.88]) and composite of cardiovascular death or HF hospitalization and/or events (HR 0.76 [95% CI 0.65-0.87]). Pooled observational data demonstrated significant reduction in HF hospitalization and events (HR 0.60 [95% CI 0.58-0.62]). Exploratory network meta-analysis found no significant difference in efficacy between individual agents.
