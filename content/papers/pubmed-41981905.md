## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Available evidence indicates that sleep is an active physiological state that facilitates cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) exchange with interstitial fluid and promotes the removal of neurotoxic solutes from the brain. Glymphatic transport appears to be most active during non-rapid eye movement sleep, particularly during slow-wave activity, when interstitial space expands and CSF-interstitial fluid exchange increases.
