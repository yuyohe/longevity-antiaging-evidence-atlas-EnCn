## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: One thousand, nine hundred and sixty-eight records were reviewed and 10 articles reporting on nine studies retained and extracted. Data were primarily from cohort studies. Only one reported a randomized controlled trial comparing CCBs with placebo. Populations, comparator groups, follow-up times, outcomes and exposure varied and overall results were mixed. It was not possible to combine all studies, but those reporting Alzheimer's disease outcomes were combined to produce an overall risk ratio of 0.79 (95% confidence interval 0.53-1.17).
