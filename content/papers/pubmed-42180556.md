EN: The best-performing descriptive model (Random Forest with data-driven feature selection and full microbiome) exhibited AUROC 0.813 ± 0.110 , consistent with structured clinical-microbiome patterning within the cohort. Global differential abundance analysis (ANCOM-BC2, adjusted for maternal age and pre-pregnancy BMI) identified Mycoplasma as the only genus achieving FDR-corrected significance (LFC = + 1.004 , q = 0.049 ), with ten additional genera reaching nominal significance ( p < 0.05 ).
