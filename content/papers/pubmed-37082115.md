## Main Results / 主要结果

## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Preliminary studies suggest that overweight and obese patients with moderate-to-severe CKD with weight loss needs, and who were under strict medical supervision by healthcare professionals, performed TRF with good compliance.
