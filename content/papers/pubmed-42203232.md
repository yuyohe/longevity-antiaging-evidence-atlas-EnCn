## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 11 studies (171 COPD participants, 105 controls) met inclusion criteria. Compared with controls, COPD was associated with markedly higher MSNA, burst frequency (+18.5, 95% CI 9.4-27.7 bursts·min-1) and burst incidence (+21.3, 95% CI 9.2-33.4 bursts per 100 heartbeats). Resting heart rate was also elevated (+10.7, 95% CI 6.1-15.3 beats·min-1), while blood pressure did not differ significantly. Three studies on noninvasive ventilation or inhaled β2-agonists found no significant pooled changes in MSNA.
