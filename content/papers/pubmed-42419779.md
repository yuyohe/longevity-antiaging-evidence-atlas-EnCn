## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 43 unique outcomes were identified across 240 eligible RCTs. Physiological (89.5%), clinical (85.0%) and life impact outcomes (63.8%) were most frequently assessed, whereas resource use (39.6%), safety (36.7%) and mortality (16.7%) outcomes were less commonly reported. Only lung function (76.3%) and health-related quality of life (57.5%) appeared in over half of the trials. Exacerbations were reported in 40.4% of studies, while several patient-prioritised outcomes, particularly activities of daily living (5.4%) and exercise tolerance (18.3%), were infrequently assessed. Industry-sponsored RCTs more often reported lung function, resource use and adverse events; non-industry trials more frequently included biomarkers. Concordance between registered and published outcomes was acceptable (79.7%), although safety outcomes and instruments were sometimes under-reported.
