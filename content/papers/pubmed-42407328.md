## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 10,916 participants, 76.2% had ms-OSA. In males, BMI yielded an AUC of 0.715 (95% confidence intervals [CIs]: 0.701, 0.729), whereas the highest AUC was observed for the Deurenberg body fat percentage (BF%) formula: 0.741 (95% CIs: 0.728, 0.754). In females, BMI yielded an AUC of 0.681 (95% CIs: 0.662, 0.701), whereas the Lean BF% formula performed best: 0.739 (95% CIs: 0.721, 0.758). In the testing set, BF%Deurenberg >25.94% in males yielded 90.5% sensitivity and 79.4% overall accuracy; BF%Lean >43.15% in females yielded 88.0% sensitivity and 72.5% overall accuracy.
