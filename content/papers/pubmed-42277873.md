## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Elevated RC (HR 2.38, 95% CI: 1.26-4.50, P = 0.008) and TG (HR 1.38, 95% CI: 1.06-1.79, P = 0.015) were independently associated with higher allograft loss risk and showed dose-response patterns. Similar results were observed in categorical analyses. Non-HDL-C was not significantly associated with allograft loss in the primary analysis (HR 1.64, 95% CI: 1.00-2.70, P = 0.051), and LDL-C, HDL-C, and TC showed no consistent associations with graft outcomes. No lipid parameter was significantly associated with all-cause mortality. The TG-allograft loss association was stronger among recipients with fewer human leukocyte antigen mismatches (P for interaction = 0.012). Adding RC to the baseline risk model modestly improved model performance (ΔC-index = 0.008, P = 0.007; ΔAIC = - 4.78).
