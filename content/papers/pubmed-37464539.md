## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Nine intestinal microflora were found causally associated with sepsis, and 11 intestinal microflora were causally associated with 28-day death in sepsis. Among them, Order Victivallales had a strong causality with lower risk of sepsis (OR = 0.86, 95% CI: 0.78-0.94, p = .00165) and lower 28-day mortality of sepsis (OR = 0.68, 95% CI: 0.53-0.87, p = .00179) after Bonferroni-corrected test. No pleiotropy was detected.
