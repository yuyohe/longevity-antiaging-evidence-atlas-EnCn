EN: This investigation aimed to determine the effects of 24 weeks of resistance training (RT) on cardiac function in older women. The RT program was conducted over 24 weeks and consisted of three sessions a week. Participants performed eight exercises for the whole body in three sets of 8-12 repetitions.
