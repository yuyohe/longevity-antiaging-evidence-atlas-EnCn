## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: In critically ill patients with PH, mortality was best associated with systemic arterial pressure and integrated hemodynamic relationships (pulmonary-to-systemic ratios and SPP). These findings highlight the prognostic value of combining or integrating pulmonary and systemic hemodynamics. The absence of a significant association between CO and SV with outcome points toward the physiological importance of compensatory mechanisms to maintain right ventricle-pulmonary artery coupling, including ventricular interdependence.
