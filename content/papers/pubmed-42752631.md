## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Overall prevalence was 18.04% (95% CI: 17.2%-18.9%), higher among women (18.87%) than men (16.97%). The chi-square test suggests that hypertension was significantly associated with age, BMI, diabetes, wealth index, education, household size, and region (p < 0.05). Among the machine learning and deep learning models, weighted logistic regression (WLR) achieved the highest accuracy (0.817), precision (0.444), specificity (0.981), AUC-ROC (0.751), and AUC-PR (0.357). However, WLR exhibited low recall (0.070). In contrast, the random forest (RF) model achieved the highest recall (0.687) and F1-score (0.460) on the test data, indicating greater sensitivity in identifying individuals with hypertension. Additionally, age, BMI, sex, family size, and educational level were identified as the most important predictors among the variables included in the study.
