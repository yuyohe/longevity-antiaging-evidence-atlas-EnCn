## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 10,186 participants contributed 18,688 interval-level observations. The most frequent transitions were to death (n = 5,063) and ADL disability only (n = 2,048). Compared with participants with at least 20 teeth, those with 0 teeth had higher hazards of ADL disability only (HR: 1.20, 95% CI, 1.04-1.39) and death (HR: 1.18, 95% CI, 1.06-1.31). In joint analyses, those with 0 teeth and dentures had the highest hazard of ADL disability only (HR: 1.30, 95% CI, 1.10-1.53), whereas those with 0 teeth and no dentures had the highest hazard of death (HR: 1.33, 95% CI, 1.18-1.49). Dementia-related transitions were sparse.
