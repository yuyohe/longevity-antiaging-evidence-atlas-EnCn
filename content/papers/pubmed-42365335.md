## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: From 10,514 records, 133 RCTs involving 289,558 participants (mean age: 64.7 years) were included. Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RAs) significantly reduced cardiovascular mortality (RR = 0.85, 95% CI: 0.75-0.98; high-certainty evidence) and stroke (RR = 0.83, 95% CI: 0.74-0.93; high-certainty evidence). SGLT2 inhibitors significantly reduced heart failure (RR = 0.64, 95% CI: 0.53-0.77; high-certainty evidence) and hospitalization for cardiovascular events (RR = 0.72, 95% CI: 0.68-0.77; high-certainty evidence). An indirect comparison suggested lower cardiovascular mortality with DPP-4 inhibitors versus GLP-1 receptor agonists, although this finding should be interpreted cautiously due to network imbalance.
