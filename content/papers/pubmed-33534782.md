EN: BACKGROUND Serum uric acid (UA) is involved in the development of hypertension. However, its impact on mortality in hypertension remains unclear. We aimed to assess the association of cardiovascular and all-cause mortality with UA in a hypertensive population. MATERIAL AND METHODS This study included 15 583 hypertensive patients from the NHANES study during 1999-2014. Weighted Cox regression analyses and cubic spline fitting were used to assess the relationship between UA and mortality risk.
