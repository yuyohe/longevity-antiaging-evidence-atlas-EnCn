## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: After matching, the 30-day landmark cohort included 4,668 patients per group and the 90-day landmark cohort included 6,665 patients per group. In the 90-day analysis, GLP-1 use was associated with significantly lower rates of chronic diarrhea (8.9% vs. 10.6%), chronic constipation (19.8% vs. 22.0%), abdominal pain (31.6% vs. 35.8%), and abdominal bloating/distension (8.3% vs. 10.9%) compared with non-GLP-1 controls (all p < 0.001). Differences in outcome recurrence (number of instances) were smaller than differences in incidence.
