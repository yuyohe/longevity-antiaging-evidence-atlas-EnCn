## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over the follow-up, 452 participants died. Higher daily steps were associated with reduced mortality risk in all socioeconomic groups. However, socioeconomically advantaged individuals experienced greater risk reductions per 1000 daily steps compared to disadvantaged groups (RERI for low vs. high RFIP: -0.115, 95 % CI: -0.189, -0.042). Individuals with low RFIP required 1.2-2.3 times more steps to match the mortality benefits seen in high RFIP groups.
