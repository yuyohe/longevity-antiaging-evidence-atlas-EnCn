## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In GBCS, of 9512 participants, the mean phenotypic age was 58.8 (standard deviation = 9.2) years. A dietary aging risk index (DARI) was constructed using nutrients and phenotypic age, with median (interquartile range) being 0.03 (0.01, 0.06). During an average follow-up of 16.1 years, after adjusting for twelve potential confounders, higher DARI were associated with older phenotypic age (β = 0.08 years, 95% confidence interval (CI) = 0.06-0.10), higher risks of accelerating age (odds ratio = 1.63, 95% CI = 1.38-1.93) and all-cause mortality (hazards ratio (HR) = 1.10, 95% CI = 1.04-1.17). The association with all-cause mortality was more pronounced in current smoker (HR = 1.29, 95% CI = 1.12-1.50). In NHANES, higher DARI were associated with lower α-Klotho levels (β=-0.020 pg/ml, 95% CI=-0.036 to -0.004).
