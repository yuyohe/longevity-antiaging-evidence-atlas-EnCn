## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: We included 4939 patients. There were 2070 patients in the metformin group, and 2869 patients in the nonmetformin group. The frequency of lactic acidosis was 5.7% (118/2070) in the metformin group and it was 4.3% (122/2869) in the nonmetformin group. There was a statistically significant difference between the two groups (P < 0.05). The lactate level in the metformin group was higher than in the nonmetformin group (2.78 ± 2.23 vs. 2.45 ± 2.24, P < 0.001).
