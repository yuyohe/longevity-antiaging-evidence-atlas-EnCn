## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Our findings suggest that digital health interventions can support LDL-c reduction and lipid control in patients with ASCVD. Multi-component interventions were most effective, particularly when combined with high-frequency delivery, bidirectional communication, and telemonitoring. High heterogeneity and high risk of bias were observed across studies, and LDL-c reduction was not a primary outcome in most trials, warranting careful interpretation. Nevertheless, digital health shows potential for integration in lipid management, and warrants future research to optimise effectiveness and support implementation in clinical practice.
