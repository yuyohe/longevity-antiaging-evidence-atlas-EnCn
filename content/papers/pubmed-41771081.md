## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Anaemia is common in patients with chronic heart failure (CHF), worsening functional status and prognosis. The role of erythropoiesis-stimulating agents (ESAs) to reduce hospitalization remains unclear. A pre-registered systematic search was performed of randomized controlled trials in adults with CHF and anaemia, comparing any ESA at any dose versus placebo or no treatment.Fourteen trials were included with 3128 participants, with moderate quality of evidence for most outcomes and variable risk of bias.
