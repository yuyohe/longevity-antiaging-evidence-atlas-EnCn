## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Records of 368 PD patients, 61% male, mean age 60.9 ± 16.1 years, body mass index (BMI) 26.2 ± 5.1 kg/m2 were reviewed, with 71 classified as frail (19.3%; CFS > 4), and frailty associated with age (odds ratio (OR) 1.047, 95% confidence interval (CL) 1.01-1.085, p = 0.012), Stoke-Davies co-morbidity (OR 1.808, 95%CL 1.129-2.895, p = 0.014) and negatively with HGS (OR 0.906, 95% CL 0.897-0.992, p = 0.033); 17.7% met muscle loss and HGS criteria for sarcopenia, with fair agreement with frailty (kappa 0.24 (CL 0.09-0.38)). Only two patients (0.5%) met all four criteria for PEW, 26.1% met the reduced BMI criteria, 4.6% the serum albumin, 32.9% the PNA and 39.4% the reduced muscle mass. HGS correlated with lean muscle mass (r 2 = 0.42, p < 0.001).
