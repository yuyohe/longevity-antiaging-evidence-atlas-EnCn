## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: At baseline, participants smoked a mean of 76 cigarettes per week, declining to 35 cigarettes at 12 weeks. Both groups showed a significant linear decline in cigarette consumption over time (mean change: -3.7 cigarettes/week; 95% CI: -5.2, -2.4), with no significant between-group difference. Smoking abstinence increased significantly (McNemar χ2=5.33, p<.05). Psychiatric symptoms decreased continually, with scores dropping by 0.29 units per week (95% CI: -0.50, -0.09) in both groups.
