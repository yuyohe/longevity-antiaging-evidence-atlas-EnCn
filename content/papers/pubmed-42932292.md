## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Confirmed sarcopenia was identified in 55 of 209 participants (26.3%; 95% CI: 20.6% to 32.8%). Participants with sarcopenia were older and had lower body mass index (BMI), poorer physical performance, and greater motor impairment than those without sarcopenia. In the multivariable model, lower BMI (OR = 0.70; 95% CI: 0.59 to 0.83), positive SARC-CalF screening (OR = 6.37; 95% CI: 2.03 to 20.05), and lower Short Physical Performance Battery (SPPB) scores (OR = 0.83; 95% CI: 0.69 to 0.99) remained independently associated with sarcopenia. Motor impairment severity, assessed using Fugl-Meyer motor categories, was also independently associated with sarcopenia.

## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: DXA-confirmed post-stroke sarcopenia affected approximately one in four individuals undergoing rehabilitation and was independently associated with lower BMI, poorer physical performance, and greater motor impairment. These findings indicate that sarcopenia is closely linked to functional impairment after stroke and reinforce the importance of integrating muscle health and functional assessment during rehabilitation. Early identification of individuals at risk may support more individualized rehabilitation strategies and improve clinical decision-making.
