## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 80 participants (80%) completed the study. After 16 weeks of intervention, the COM group exhibited significantly greater reductions in body weight, BMI, and waist-to-hip ratio (WHR) compared to the MET group (all P < 0.01). The COM group experienced an average weight loss of 6.09 ± 3.34 kg, while the MET group lost only 2.25 ± 4.27 kg. The COM group also demonstrated greater improvements in testosterone (TEST), Chinese visceral adiposity index (CVAI), and CRP levels compared to the MET group. Additionally, the COM group showed higher rates of menstrual cycle recovery than the MET group. From weeks 16 to 40, the COM group demonstrated a significantly higher natural pregnancy rate than the MET group (35% vs. 15%, P < 0.05).
