## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: The AHA PREVENT equations may more comprehensively capture the projected cardiovascular benefit of orforglipron than the legacy PCE. In these modeled scenarios, exclusive reliance on the PCE may provide a less complete estimate of the projected cardiometabolic risk-factor benefit of orforglipron.
