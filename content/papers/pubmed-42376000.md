## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Both HFDs shortened lifespan but only the highest saturated-fat source (coconut oil) uniquely produced a female-specific decline in climbing ability and significant neurodegeneration, compared to lard and control diets. Mechanistic evaluation revealed no activation of the NF-κB homolog relish in response to a coconut oil HFD however chronic HFD feeding led to elevated neuronal reactive oxygen species and early impairment of autophagic flux, preceding autophagosome accumulation during aging.
