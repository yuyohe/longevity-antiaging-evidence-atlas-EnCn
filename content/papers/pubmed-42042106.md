## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Background/Objectives: To evaluate the utility of phase angle (PhA) derived from bioelectrical impedance analysis as a screening indicator for pre-frailty in community-dwelling older adults. Methods: This cross-sectional study included 171 participants (36 men and 135 women) in Japan in 2023. PhA at 50 kHz was measured using bioelectrical impedance analysis and evaluated as a potential screening indicator for pre-frailty.
