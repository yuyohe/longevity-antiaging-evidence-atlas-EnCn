## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Current sedentary behavior (SB) guidelines primarily emphasize total time spent sedentary. We explored differences between interrupted and prolonged SB in relation to a range of cancer outcomes. This study included 91,292 UK Biobank participants with valid accelerometer data. Participants were followed for a median of 12.38 years (interquartile range 11.56-13.15 years). A two-step approach based on a random forest model was used to classify SB.
