## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Following the landmark period, over a median follow-up of 5.2 years (IQR, 2.8-8.4; total 785,564 person-years), the overall risk of the composite neurologic outcome was similar between groups (aHR, 0.97; 95% CI, 0.87-1.09). Metformin users had lower observed all-cause mortality than DPP-4 inhibitor users (aHR, 0.75; 95% CI, 0.73-0.77). In a 5-year landmark sensitivity analysis, no association was observed (aHR, 0.98; 95% CI, 0.88-1.09). In exploratory analyses, a lower observed risk was seen among individuals surviving beyond 10 years (aHR, 0.85; 95% CI, 0.75-0.97; p = 0.0152), but this analysis was conditioned on long-term survival and should be interpreted as exploratory and hypothesis-generating.
