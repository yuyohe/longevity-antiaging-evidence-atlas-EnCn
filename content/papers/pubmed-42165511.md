## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RAs) are widely used for type 2 diabetes and obesity and produce significant weight loss. However, concerns have emerged about potential loss of skeletal muscle mass (SMM) with pharmacologic weight reduction, particularly in older adults at risk for sarcopaenia. The effects of GLP-1 RA therapy on SMM among patients participating in intensive cardiac rehabilitation (ICR) remain incompletely characterized.
