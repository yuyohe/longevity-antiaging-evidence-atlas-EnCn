## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 15,291 subjects were analysed (897 with consumption of probiotics/prebiotics and 14,394 without). The use of probiotics/prebiotics showed an inverse correlation with urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) (P < 0.05). Probiotics/prebiotics use was associated with lower risk of CKD in subjects with hypertension, hyperlipidaemia and diabetes mellitus. The consumption of probiotics/prebiotics was associated with a significantly lower risk of all-cause mortality in different regression models (P < 0.001, for all), but the lower risk of cardiovascular mortality did not reach statistical significance (P > 0.05, for all)]. MR analysis showed negative associations between the genetically predicted genus Flavonifractor and risk of CKD and diabetic kidney disease (DKD).
