## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 1,401 older adults were included (median age: 73 years [IQR: 69-78]; 66% female). The median SHR was 0.80 (IQR: 0.73-0.89). Restricted cubic spline analyses demonstrated significant nonlinear associations between SHR and geriatric outcomes, with lower SHR values associated with higher odds of malnutrition, probable sarcopenia, and frailty. In fully adjusted analyses, low SHR remained independently associated with probable sarcopenia (OR: 1.51, 95% CI: 1.02-2.25; p = 0.040) and frailty (OR: 1.62, 95% CI: 1.05-2.50; p = 0.031), whereas the association with malnutrition was no longer significant. Associations were more pronounced among participants without diabetes, particularly for probable sarcopenia (p for interaction = 0.038).

## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Lower SHR values were associated with increased vulnerability to geriatric syndromes, particularly probable sarcopenia and frailty, in older adults.
