## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: At ordinal regression analysis the KLB rs17618244 variant was associated with hepatic fibrosis (OR 1.23, 95% C.I.1.004-1.51; p = 0.04), but not with steatosis, inflammation and ballooning. By stratifying patients according to the presence of obesity, the KLB A allele was further associated with lobular inflammation (OR 1.32, 95% C.I.1.02-1.72; p = 0.03) and cirrhosis (OR 2.51, 95% C.I.1.23-5.05; p = 0.01) Moreover, hepatic KLB expression correlated with that of fibrogenic genes.
