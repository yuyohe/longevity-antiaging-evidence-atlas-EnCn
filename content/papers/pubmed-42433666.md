EN: Extreme heat and fine particulate matter (PM2.5) may increase cardiovascular disease (CVD) mortality, but their combined impact on overall CVD incidence is unclear. We aimed to evaluate their independent effects, identify vulnerable populations, and explore interactions, using a 2019 cohort of 5,070,689 adults. This year's average PM2.5 and extreme heat (annual ratio of extreme heat days (AREHDs), annual ratio of extreme heat hours (AREHHs)) exposures were analyzed via accelerated failure time (AFT) models.
