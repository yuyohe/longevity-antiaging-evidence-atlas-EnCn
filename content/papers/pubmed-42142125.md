## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The analysis included 10,290 patients (5,145 OSA, 5,145 non-OSA; mean age 66.5 ± 9.0 years). On multivariate analysis, OSA was associated with increased risk of 90-day composite complications (OR: 1.84; 95% CI 1.51-2.23; p < 0.001) and pulmonary complications (OR: 2.17; 95% CI 1.74-2.71; p < 0.001). OSA patients also experienced longer LOS (OR: 1.12; p = 0.001). The adjusted predicted complication probability was 5.62% for OSA vs. 3.13% for non-OSA.
