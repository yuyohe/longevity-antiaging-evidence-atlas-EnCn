EN: Senescence-impaired bone repair poses a significant clinical challenge, primarily due to the metabolic microenvironment imbalance driven by senescent cells. Current biomaterial strategies are largely confined to providing passive mechanical support and/or delivering therapeutic factors, failing to fundamentally reverse this specific pathological microenvironment. Here, we propose a senescent metabolism-reprogramming therapeutic strategy by developing a hierarchical bioceramic scaffold, comprising an outer 3D-printed β-tricalcium phosphate (β-TCP) hollow tube and an inner freeze-casting MnTCP ceramic rod with the loading of nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN).
