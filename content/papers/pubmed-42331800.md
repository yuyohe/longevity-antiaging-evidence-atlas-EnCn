EN: HRS-7535 is an orally active small-molecule glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist that showed weight-loss potential in a phase 1 study. In this completed multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2 trial conducted at 29 centers in China, we evaluated the efficacy and safety of HRS-7535 in adults with obesity without diabetes. 235 participants with a body mass index of 28.0-40.0 kg/m² were randomized a 1:1:1:1:1 ratio to once-daily oral HRS-7535 at target doses of 30 mg (n = 48), 60 mg (n = 47), 120 mg (n = 46), or 180 mg (n = 48), or placebo (n = 46); all randomized participants were included in the analyses.
