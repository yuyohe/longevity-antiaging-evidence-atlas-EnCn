EN: Among 112 596 patients with CKD, 16460 received SGLT2i and GLP-1RA combination therapy, and 96 136 received SGLT2i monotherapy. The matched cohort included 32 448 patients (16 224 per group). Over a median follow-up of 12 months, the addition of a GLP-1RA was associated with lower rates of the primary outcome of MAKE (HR 0.70; 95% CI 0.65-0.74; p < 0.001) compared with SGLT2i monotherapy.
