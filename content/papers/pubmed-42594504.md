## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Baseline characteristics were comparable between groups. At 6 months, participants receiving the intervention showed significant improvements in physical performance (SPPB: +1.6 points vs. + 0.3 in controls; between-group difference: +1.8 points, p = 0.001), and muscle strength (handgrip: +3.2 kg vs. -0.1 kg in controls; between-group difference: +3.8 kg, p = 0.026). No significant between-group differences were found for the 6-minute walk test or anthropometric measures. Appendicular skeletal muscle mass (ASMM) tended to be preserved in the intervention group while declining in controls (between-group difference approaching +0.9 kg, p = 0.057). Sarcopenia prevalence decreased in the intervention group (37.1% to 15.4%) and remained stable in controls (28.4% to 29.9%), with a borderline group-by-time interaction (p = 0.109).
