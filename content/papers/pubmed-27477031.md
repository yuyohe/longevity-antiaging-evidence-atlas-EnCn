## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Fifteen studies including 3136 participants met the inclusion criteria. Exercise had a beneficial effect on reduction of fall-related fractures, with pooled estimates of RR 0.604 (95% CI 0.453 - 0.840, P = 0.003, I 2 = 0%). The rate of falls (RaR 0.856, 95% CI 0.778 - 0.941, P = 0.001, I 2 = 45%) and leg strength (SMD 0.613, 95% CI 0.119 - 1.107, P = 0.015, I 2 = 76.7%) were also potentially affected by exercise interventions.
