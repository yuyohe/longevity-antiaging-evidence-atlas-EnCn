## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 1,318 patients (mean age 67 years, 61% women, 29% with moderate or greater STR, median RV free wall longitudinal strain 19%), WHO groups 1, 2, 3, and 4 PH were present in 33%, 11%, 32%, and 12%, respectively. Patients with moderate or greater STR were more symptomatic and congested and had worse RV function and pulmonary pressures. Over a median 1.5 years (Q1-Q3: 0.3-4.6 years), 454 patients died. Moderate or severe STR was associated with mortality independent of various RV function measures or RV-pulmonary arterial coupling. This remained true across the individual WHO groups of PH.
