## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Higher baseline residential traffic-related air pollution indicators were associated with lower multidimensional SAI in this cross-sectional analysis, after adjustment for sociodemographic and lifestyle factors. These findings suggest that reducing traffic-related air pollution may be relevant to population healthy ageing, although longitudinal studies are needed to determine whether such interventions can extend healthy lifespan or reduce late-life morbidity.
