## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over median follow-up periods of 6.95 years in CHARLS and 13.74 years in ELSA, 727 and 1064 participants developed frailty, respectively. Each 1-SD increase in eCRF was associated with a lower risk of incident frailty in CHARLS (HR = 0.92; 95% CI, 0.86-0.97) and ELSA (HR = 0.82; 95% CI, 0.72-0.97). Compared with low eCRF, high eCRF was associated with substantially lower frailty risk in both cohorts. Age modified the association in CHARLS, while sex and BMI were significant modifiers in ELSA.
