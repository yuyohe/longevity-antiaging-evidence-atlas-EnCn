EN: More than 14.2 million people worldwide and 280 000 in the US experience a hip fracture each year. The median 1-year mortality rate after a hip fracture is 22% and approximately 42% to 71% of patients regain their prefracture level of basic activities of daily living within 6 months. Hip fractures are classified as intracapsular or extracapsular and most commonly occur after a fall. Intracapsular hip fractures include femoral neck (34%) and femoral head (rare). Extracapsular fractures consist of intertrochanteric (48%) and subtrochanteric fractures (5.8%).
