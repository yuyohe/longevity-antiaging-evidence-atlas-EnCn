EN: Naked mole-rats (NMRs, Heterocephalus glaber) display unusual longevity and resistance to age-related decline, and accumulating evidence suggests that their autophagy-lysosome pathway (ALP) is regulated differently from that of conventional mammalian models. However, most studies in NMR cells have relied on static biochemical or ultrastructural readouts, leaving the dynamic organisation of autophagy in living cells poorly defined.
