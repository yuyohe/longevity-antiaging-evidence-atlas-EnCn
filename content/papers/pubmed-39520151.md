## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: SC and RC were matched for mean age ± SD (SC: 53.3 ± 5.7 vs. RC: 54.3 ± 8.2 years). The mean length of hospitalization (days) was significantly shorter in SC (9.0 ± 5.7) than in RC (12.7 ± 6) (p < 0.02). Metformin use was associated with reduction in mortality risk (OR: 0.106, 95% CI = 0.039-0.287; p < 0.001). The calculated number needed to treat for metformin was 3.1.
