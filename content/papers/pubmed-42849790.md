## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 10,462 participants (mean age 60.0 years, 55% men, median follow-up 12.5 years), 2,101 deaths occurred. Estimated CRF showed a curvilinear inverse association with mortality: relative to the median (27.9 ml·kg-1·min-1), a VO2max of 12.5 ml·kg-1·min-1 was associated with 59% higher mortality (HR 1.59;95% CI 1.28-1.98), with risk declining continuously without plateau (HR 0.71;0.56-0.89 at 44.5 ml·kg-1·min-1). Handgrip strength showed a weaker, curvilinear inverse association that plateaued at the median (32 kg): at 12 kg, mortality was 37% higher (HR 1.37;1.11-1.67), with no further reduction beyond the reference. Fifteen-year survival rose linearly from 39.3% to 77.4% across the estimated CRF range versus 49.8% to 71.7% for handgrip strength.
