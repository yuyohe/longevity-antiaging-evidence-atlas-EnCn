EN: Cardiovascular disease (CVD) remains a leading cause of global morbidity and mortality, with self-management playing a pivotal role in improving outcomes. Voice-assisted artificial intelligence (AI) technologies such as virtual assistants and voice-controlled applications have emerged as innovative tools for healthcare delivery.
