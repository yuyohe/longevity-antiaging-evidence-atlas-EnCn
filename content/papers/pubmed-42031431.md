## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Differences were observed in baseline characteristics, IE type and survival. Females had worse overall adjusted survival, suggesting a less favourable overall prognosis. In terms of surgical decision-making, surgery was withheld more often in females despite surgical indication, and after excluding these patients, surgery appeared more protective in females than in males. These findings may reflect sex differences in surgical selection.
