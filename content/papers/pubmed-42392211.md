EN: Age-related macular degeneration (AMD) is the leading cause of legal blindness in Australians over 50 years, accounting for 60% of all blindness. AMD progresses from asymptomatic early and intermediate stages to late neovascular ('wet') or atrophic ('dry') forms that cause irreversible central vision loss.
