## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 15 studies were included in the meta-analysis, SDB/OSA was diagnosed with at-home polysomnography in six studies, while five studies relied on self-report or questionnaires. In the remaining studies, International Classification of Diseases (ICD) codes determined the diagnosis of SDB. The overall pooled analysis showed that patients with SDB/OSA had higher risk of cognitive impairment and/or all-cause dementia (HR 1.52, 95% CI: 1.32, 1.74), when compared to patients without SDB/OSA. However, when studies with diagnosis of SDB based on polysomnography were pooled together, the strength of association for all-cause cognitive impairment was weaker (HR 1.32, 95% CI: 1.00, 1.74).
