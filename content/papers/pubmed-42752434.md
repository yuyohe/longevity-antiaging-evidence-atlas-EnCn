## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Previous studies utilizing self-reported data or polysomnography have found that poor sleep quality is associated with increased risk of morbidity and mortality. However, the association between real-world sleep patterns, including sleep stages, duration, and fragmentation, measured objectively with physiological data and the risk of incident disease among middle-aged and older adults has not yet been systematically evaluated.
