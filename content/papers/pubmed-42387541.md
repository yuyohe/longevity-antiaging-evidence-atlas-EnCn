## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: In this real-world, multi-center analysis, no detectable signal of increased locoregional recurrence was observed between cohorts. These findings do not establish evidence of oncologic equivalence, but rather an absence of a detectable early signal of increased risk within the observed follow-up.
