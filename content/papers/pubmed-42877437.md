## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 254 participants were included (161 females, 93 males). Sensitivity and specificity for detecting slow gait speed were 65.5% and 65.8% in men and 79.5% and 59.0% in women. For weak grip strength, sensitivity and specificity were 23.0% and 66.7% in men and 49.3% and 93.3% in women. Internal consistency was low (α = 0.314-0.574 across measures). Construct validity was moderate, with expected associations observed for functional and quality of life outcomes.
