## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: PA and KDM were positively associated with UI. The third quartile of PA and KDM conferred the highest risk for SUI (PA: Q3 OR = 1.58, 95% CI 1.33-1.88; KDM: Q3 OR = 1.39, 95% CI 1.20-1.62). Higher quartiles were also linked to greater UI severity.
