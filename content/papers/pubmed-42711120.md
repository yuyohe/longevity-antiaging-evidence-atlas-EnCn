## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: ACM analyses included 64 743 participants (mean age 61.5 (SD 7.8) years; female 37 497; 1697 events). Compared with the reference (fifth PK30 percentile and <5000 steps/day), both the <5000 and 5000-7500 steps/day groups showed a beneficial linear PK30-ACM risk association. Substantially lower ACM risk was observed at higher PK30 in the <5000 steps/day group (eg, 80 steps/min: HR 0.72 (95% CI 0.54 to 0.97)) and lower PK30 in the 5000-7500 group (eg, 60 steps/min: HR 0.70 (0.58 to 0.86)). Among all groups, 7500-10 000 steps/day had the lowest ACM risk, with a U-shaped pattern and the lowest risk was observed at approximately 100 steps/min (0.57 (0.47 to 0.69)). Higher PK30 was associated with lower ACM at 90 steps/min across all step-groups (eg, <5000 steps/day, 0.64 (0.32 to 1.32); above 10 000, 0.71 (0.59 to 0.87)).
