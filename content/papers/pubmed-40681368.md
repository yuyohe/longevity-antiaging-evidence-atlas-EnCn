## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Discordance analysis provides robust evidence that apoB is a more accurate marker of cardiovascular risk than either LDL-C or non-HDL-C, notwithstanding these variables are highly intercorrelated. Thus, neither LDL-C nor non-HDL-C are adequate clinical surrogates for apoB. Accordingly, apoB should be the primary measure in clinical care to estimate the cardiovascular risk attributable to the apoB lipoproteins and the adequacy of lipid-lowering therapy to reduce this risk.
