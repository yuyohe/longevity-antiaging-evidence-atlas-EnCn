## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Individuals in the highest CRF quintile had approximately 74% lower mortality vs. those in the lowest quintile (<5 METs). Hypertension had the greatest population-level impact (PAR 21.8%; 95% CI 15.2-28.0), followed by low CRF (PAR 14.7%; 95% CI 8.1-21.1). In a fully adjusted sensitivity analysis, low CRF demonstrated the strongest independent association with mortality (RR 1.77; 95% CI 1.75-1.79) compared with hypertension (RR 1.33; 95% CI 1.32-1.35). Absolute EIN was lowest for low CRF (2.36), suggesting that for every 100 individuals whose fitness improved over the observed follow-up period, approximately 24 fewer deaths would be expected.
