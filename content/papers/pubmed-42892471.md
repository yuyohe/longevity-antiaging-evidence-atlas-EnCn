## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: At 1 year, GLP-1RA use was associated with significantly lower incident AF (1.63% vs. 2.62%; HR 0.655, 95% CI 0.508-0.844; p = 0.001), a 34.5% relative risk reduction. Reductions were consistent for paroxysmal (HR 0.586), persistent (HR 0.482), and longstanding persistent AF (HR 0.527; all p < 0.05); permanent AF was non-significant at 1 year. Ventricular tachyarrhythmias (HR 0.556; p = 0.002) and hospitalization (HR 0.668; p < 0.001) were also significantly reduced. Benefits persisted across 2- and 3-year follow-up and remained consistent in landmark analyses.
