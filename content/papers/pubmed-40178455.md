## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Immunotherapy has transformed the landscape of cancer treatment, with T cell-based strategies at the forefront of this revolution. However, the durability of these responses is frequently undermined by two intertwined phenomena: T cell exhaustion and senescence. While exhaustion is driven by chronic antigen exposure in the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment, leading to a reversible state of diminished functionality, senescence reflects a more permanent, age- or stress-induced arrest in cellular proliferation and effector capacity. Together, these processes represent formidable barriers to sustained anti-tumor immunity.
