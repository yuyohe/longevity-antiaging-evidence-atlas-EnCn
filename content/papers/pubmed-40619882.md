## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 17 studies, including 732 participants aged 24.27 to 70.5 years (309 participants with metabolic diseases, and 423 without metabolic diseases) and 38 intervention groups, met the inclusion criteria. The combined intervention of exercise and diet significantly reduced body weight compared to exercise alone [WMD = -2.85 kg, p = 0.001], with significant reductions observed in both adults with and without metabolic disease, and for interventions lasting more than 12 weeks. ## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Combined exercise and dietary interventions are more effective than exercise alone in reducing body weight. While VFA was reduced following long-term interventions of exercise in combination with caloric restriction, our analyses showed no change in liver fat, or IMTG.
