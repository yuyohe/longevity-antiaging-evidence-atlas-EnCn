## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Patients with resected, high-risk melanoma receive adjuvant immune checkpoint blockade (ICB), yet clinical benefit remains unpredictable, with 25%-40% of patients experiencing recurrence. To evaluate whether pre-treatment gut microbiome (GMB) features predict recurrence, we analyzed stool samples from 674 patients enrolled in a phase 3 clinical trial, CheckMate 915, which investigated the combination of nivolumab plus ipilimumab versus nivolumab as a single agent across five geographic regions. Region-specific and cross-region meta-analyses identified pre-treatment taxa associated with recurrence, including Eubacterium, Ruminococcus, Firmicutes, and Clostridium. Recurrence prediction was strongest when the validation cohort exhibited GMB profiles similar to those in the discovery cohort.
