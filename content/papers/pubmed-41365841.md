## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: GLP-1RAs are generally not cost-effective for obesity treatment in patients without diabetes in HICs from a healthcare/payer perspective. However, they may be cost-effective in subgroups evaluated over longer time horizons. Most included studies focused on weight-related outcomes, potentially underestimating the broader economic value of GLP-1RAs. This pooled economic evidence may inform the decision-making process.
