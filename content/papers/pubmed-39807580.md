## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: At the 90-day follow-up, the primary outcome of death or functional dependency was not different between early- and delayed-treatment groups according to the history of hypertension; the odds ratios (95% CIs) associated with the early-treatment group were 1.11 (0.91-1.36) and 1.38 (0.92-2.08) for participants with and without a history of hypertension.
