## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over a median follow-up of 8.6 years (interquartile range 6.7-12.5), 335 cardiovascular deaths occurred. Heart failure (30.4%), chronic ischemic heart disease (14.0%), and acute myocardial infarction (11.6%) were the leading causes. Independent predictors were age (hazard ratio 1.08, 95% confidence interval 1.07-1.10, per year), male sex (1.68, 1.29-2.18), active smoking (1.68, 1.30-2.15), hypertension (1.73, 1.29-2.33), atrial fibrillation (2.16, 1.56-2.98), prior myocardial infarction (1.71, 1.25-2.35), and diabetes (1.37, 1.06-1.76). Nocturnal desaturation lost significance after multivariable adjustment. Among patients with an apnea-hypopnea index ≥15 events/h, a good continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) response was associated with lower cardiovascular mortality (0.72, 0.55-0.96). The RSF C-index was 0.80. Six phenotypes showed cardiovascular mortality of 2.9% to 14.4%.
