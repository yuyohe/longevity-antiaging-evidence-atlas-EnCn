## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Combined aerobic exercise (AE) and resistance exercise (RE) significantly improved 6-min walk test (6MWT) (MD = 52.2 m; 95 % CrI, 31.7-73.0). Multi-modality exercise (MME) performed best in enhancing the handgrip strength (MD = 9.1 kg; 95%CrI, 1.8-16.4), also showed potential superiority over AE + RE in improving 6MWT and 30STS performance. Protein oral nutritional supplements positively affected lean body mass (MD = 0.3 kg; 95%CrI, 0.1-0.45).
