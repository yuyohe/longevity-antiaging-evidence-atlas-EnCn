EN: Axon degeneration, driven by the NAD+ hydrolyzing enzyme SARM1, is an early pathological hallmark of numerous neurodegenerative diseases. SARM1 exists in an inactive form and is activated following nerve injury. However, the precise molecular mechanism underlying SARM1 activation remains to be fully elucidated.
