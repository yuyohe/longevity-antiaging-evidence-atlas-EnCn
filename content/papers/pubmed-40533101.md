## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: In addition to a reduced peak oxygen uptake, survivors of preterm birth have impairments in the respiratory, cardiovascular and metabolic domains during cardiopulmonary exercise testing. Given that reduced aerobic capacity is associated with increased morbidity and mortality, exercise interventions that target cardiorespiratory fitness should be prioritised across the lifespan in those born preterm.
