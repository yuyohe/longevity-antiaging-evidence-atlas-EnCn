EN: Abnormal phosphate homeostasis is associated with inflammation, vascular calcification, and endothelial dysfunction in chronic kidney disease (CKD). Apparent treatment-resistant hypertension (ATRH) is common in CKD and is associated with increased cardiovascular mortality.
