## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: We observed that leucine supplementation was ineffective to improve muscle mass and function. Supplementation with whey and soy failed to enhance resistance-training effects. Similarly, supplementation with neither whey nor creatine potentiated the adaptations to resistance training. Finally, no sex-based differences were found in response to whey supplementation. Resistance exercise per se increased muscle mass and function in all sub-investigations. There were no adverse effects.

## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Neither protein (whey and soy), leucine, nor creatine supplementation enhanced resistance training-induced adaptations in pre-frail and frail elderly, regardless of sex. These findings do not support the notion that some widely used supplement-based interventions can add to the already potent effects of resistance exercise to counteract frailty-related muscle wasting and dynapenia.
