## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Overall GI adverse events were 1.86 times higher with *both* agents (95% CI=1.56, 2.21), with tirzepatide showing a greater risk (RR 2.94, 95% CI=2.61, 3.32) than semaglutide (RR 1.68, 95% CI=1.46, 1.94). Semaglutide increased gallbladder-related disorders, particularly cholelithiasis, by over 2.6 times (95% CI=1.40, 4.82), while tirzepatide showed no significant biliary risk. Neither agent significantly increased hepatic or pancreatic adverse events.
