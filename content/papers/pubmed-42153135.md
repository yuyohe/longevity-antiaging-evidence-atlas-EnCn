## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among the included patients, 341 (34.9%) had CVDs. These patients were older and had higher heart rate, blood pressure, inflammatory markers, and lower hemoglobin and lymphocyte (P < 0.05). After multivariable adjustment, CVDs were associated with adverse in-hospital outcomes (adjusted OR 1.43, 95% CI 1.05-1.94, P = 0.023) and a higher risk of 1-year readmission (adjusted HR 1.57, 95% CI 1.21-2.05, P = 0.001). Subtype analyses showed that HF and IHD were significantly associated with adverse in-hospital outcomes, while HF and AF predicted higher 1-year readmission risk (P <0.05). Kaplan-Meier curves showed a higher readmission risk in patients with CVDs (P < 0.001). These associations were generally consistent across subgroups, with a significant interaction observed only for HF in patients younger than 70 years.
