EN: Hutchinson-Gilford progeria syndrome (HGPS) is a premature aging disease caused by progerin, a mutant variant of lamin A. Progerin anchors aberrantly to the nuclear envelope disrupting a plethora of cellular processes, which in turn elicits senescence. We previously showed that the chromosomal region maintenance 1 (CRM1)-driven nuclear export pathway is abnormally enhanced in patient-derived fibroblasts, due to overexpression of CRM1. Interestingly, pharmacological inhibition of CRM1 using leptomycin B rescues the senescent phenotype of HGPS fibroblasts, delineating CRM1 as a potential therapeutic target against HGPS.
