## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 763 participants with diabetes were included in the baseline analysis. During the 4-year follow-up, 62 (8.1%) participants developed new-onset sarcopenia. Compared with the lowest quartile (Q1) of baseline TyG-CVAI, the highest quartile (Q4) was significantly associated with an increased risk of sarcopenia (HR = 2.368, 95% CI: 1.014-5.532) in the fully adjusted model, with a significant trend across quartiles (P for trend = 0.017).
