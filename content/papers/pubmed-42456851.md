## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Hypertension was associated with increased dementia risk (hazard ratio[HR] 1.21 [1.11-1.31]). A significant multiplicative interaction existed between hypertension and internet use (HR 1.21 [1.06-1.38]). Longitudinal mediation analysis estimated indirect associations via PA (HR 1.06 [1.04-1.08]) and internet use (HR 1.21 [1.03-1.42]), with mediated proportions of 4.95% and 11.27%, respectively.
