## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Diet quality was better in females and White individuals compared with males and Black individuals. Generally, DASH, EDIP, and EDIH showed mostly null or modest associations with cancer incidence and mortality. Notably, higher diet quality measured by DASH was associated with lower colorectal (HR = 0.79; 95% CI: 0.65, 0.97) and prostate cancer mortality (HR = 0.56; 95% CI: 0.38, 0.83) among White participants.
