EN: Semaglutide reduced the risk of the main outcome versus placebo [hazard ratio (HR) (95% confidence interval) 0.79 (0.69-0.89); P = .0002]. This risk reduction was greater in subgroups with baseline hemoglobin A1c >8% [HR 0.63 (0.52-0.76)] versus ≤8% [HR 0.93 (0.79-1.11); Pinteraction = .0027], and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) ≥2000 mg/g [HR 0.53 (0.39-0.72)] versus UACR <100 mg/g [HR 0.90 (0.58-1.39); Pinteraction = .0367].
