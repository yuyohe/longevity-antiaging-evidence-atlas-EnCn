EN: Clustering effects, such as those introduced by housing animals in shared cages, are often overlooked in preclinical lifespan studies, despite their potential to underestimate variance estimates and inflate Type I error rates, leading to misleading conclusions. This methodological oversight reduces statistical rigor and may undermine the reliability of findings.
