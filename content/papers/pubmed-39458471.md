## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Body weight significantly decreased with TRE (-3.6 ± 0.3%), contrasting with controls (+0.2 ± 0.5%, p < 0.001). Significant effects of TRE over time were observed on plasma IGFBP2, insulin, HOMA-IR, and 8-isoprostane levels, without affecting other biomarkers. In the sub-analysis, IGFBP2 increased while leptin and 8-isoprostane decreased significantly only in the "higher weight loss" subgroup. Changes in insulin and HOMA-IR were related to TRE adherence.
