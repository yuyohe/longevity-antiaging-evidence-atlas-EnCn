EN: The 18-month risk for ION was 8.5 versus 5.5 per 10 000 among GLP-1RA users versus SGLT2i users (RD, 3.0 [95% CI, 0.4 to 5.7]) and 7.8 versus 4.2 per 10 000 among GLP-1RA users versus DPP4i users (RD, 3.6 [CI, 1.1 to 6.1]).
