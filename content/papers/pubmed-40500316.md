EN: Studies of prolonged fasting produced spectacular weight loss and demonstrated that ketone bodies rise for approximately three weeks before stabilizing as production and utilization rates equilibrate. Although starvation is no longer an accepted obesity treatment, an extended period of fasting is the basis of time-restricted eating which produces metabolic benefits. Nevertheless, the pattern of change in ketone bodies with time-restricted eating has never been investigated. We collected weekly fasting measures of breath acetone from subjects (N = 60) who were on an eight-week, calorie-restricted diet and were randomized to different time-restricted eating windows.
