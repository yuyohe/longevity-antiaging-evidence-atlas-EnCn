## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Traditional observational studies have shown that fatty acids and gut microbiota are crucial in osteoarthritis (OA) progression, but their findings are often conflicting due to biases, confounding factors, and measurement errors. We conducted a two-sample Mendelian randomization analysis using genome-wide association study data on fatty acids from 136,016 individuals, the gut microbiota from 7738 individuals, and osteoarthritis from 314,870 individuals.
