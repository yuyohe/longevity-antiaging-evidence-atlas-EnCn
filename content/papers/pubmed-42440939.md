## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 370 overweight participants (BMI 25.0-29.9 kg/m2), each logarithm unit of alpha-klotho (pg/mL) was associated with 0.51 higher HOMA-IR (95% confidence interval [CI], 0.07-0.94), after covariate adjustment. Alpha-klotho was significantly associated with serum glucose (Beta = 0.56, 95% CI: 0.04-1.09), but not serum insulin after covariate adjustment. No significant associations were found among participants with BMI < 25.0 (n = 279) or ≥30 kg/m2 (n = 124) (P for interaction = 0.18). Alpha-klotho was not significantly associated with incident diabetes, overall or in any BMI subgroup.
