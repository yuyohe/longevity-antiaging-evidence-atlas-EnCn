EN: Klotho KL is an aging factor that has been associated with Alzheimer's Disease (AD) risk. Two common alleles circulate in human populations: the major allele FC and the minor allele VS, which is defined by two SNPs that cause two amino acid substitutions (F352V and C370S) in KL 's second exon. To investigate the possibility that human KL variants influence brain aging and cognition, we developed a novel mouse model with humanized KL alleles.
