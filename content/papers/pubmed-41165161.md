## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During a mean follow-up of 8.1 years, 121 223 dementia cases occurred. Compared with the non-elevated BP group, dementia risk was modestly increased in the elevated BP group [aHR 1.016; 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.996-1.037] and significantly higher in the hypertension group (aHR 1.029; 95% CI 1.006-1.051). The associations were particularly pronounced for VaD (elevated BP: aHR 1.159; hypertension: aHR 1.372), whereas they were not significant for AD dementia. These associations were more pronounced among midlife adults and women.
