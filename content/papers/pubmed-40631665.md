## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 998 aneurysmal men (median age, 69.5 [interquartile range (IQR), 67-72] years; median AAA diameter, 35.4 [IQR, 32-41.2] mm) were included. Statin use was significantly associated with reduced AAA growth rate; an increase of 1 DDD statin per day was associated with an adjusted change in growth rate of -0.22 mm/year [95% CI, -0.39 to -0.06]; P=0.009). The 5-year adjusted hazard ratio for undergoing repair per doubling of statin dose presented a significantly reduced adjusted hazard ratio (HR) of 0.82 ([95% CI, 0.70-0.97]; P=0.023), which was significant after 2.5 years. Statin use was associated with a significantly lower risk of the composite outcome (surgery, rupture, and death) in a dose-dependent manner, with an adjusted HR of 0.83 ([95% CI, 0.73-0.94]; P=0.003) per doubling of statin dose. Findings were robust in a variety of sensitivity analyses.
