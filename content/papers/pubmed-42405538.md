## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 55,363 transplant recipients were included. Among these, 1,503 cases of the four VPDs were identified. Multivariable analysis revealed a significantly higher risk of varicella in children and allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation recipients. Conversely, for IPD, the risk was higher among individuals aged ≥60 years and in solid organ transplantation recipients; however, the difference was not significant. The SIR for varicella was notably higher in adult recipients aged ≥ 20 years (SIR, 2.79; 95% confidence interval [CI], 2.53-3.06; SIR, 6.02; 95% CI, 4.89-7.33), while for IPD, the SIR for all ages was notably increased at 20.3 (95% CI, 18.72-21.96). For mumps, the SIR was significantly lower at 0.24 (95% CI, 0.19-0.30) across all age groups. Conversely, for pertussis, the SIR was 0.67 (95% CI, 0.32-1.24), but the difference was not significant.
