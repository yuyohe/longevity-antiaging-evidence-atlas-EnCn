## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Longer 5T-CST duration was associated with lower handgrip strength and sarcopenia, but not low skeletal muscle mass index. Among 151 patients with normal handgrip strength and survival data, 27 (17.9%) had low physical performance. This subgroup had preserved muscle mass but more ascites and hepatic reserve impairment and lower survival (mortality, 29.6% vs. 12.1%; log-rank p = 0.007). In a principal analysis restricted to this subgroup, adjusted for mALBI grade and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), low physical performance independently predicted mortality (hazard ratio, 4.70; 95% CI, 1.94-11.37; p < 0.001). In the overall cohort (n = 237), it remained predictive after adjustment for age, sex, mALBI grade, HCC, and sarcopenia (hazard ratio, 2.74; 95% CI, 1.45-5.16; p = 0.002).
