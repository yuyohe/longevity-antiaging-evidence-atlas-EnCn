## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Follow-up data were available for 194 participants (63%). After a mean of 10.8 months of follow-up, adjusted models demonstrated frailty was associated with higher mortality (CFS HR 2.26, 95% CI 1.34-3.83; B-FIT HR 2.63, 95% CI 1.45-4.77, p = 0.001). The two instruments showed similar discriminatory power. Neither was associated with length of stay. Among survivors, frailty was associated with decline in physical function, but not social function.
