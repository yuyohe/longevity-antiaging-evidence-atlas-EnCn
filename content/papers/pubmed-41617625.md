## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 424 patients, 61 ASCVD events occurred (41 prevalent, 20 incident). ApoB/LDL-C ratio ≥0.31 g/mmol associated with higher event rates (27.6% vs 11.8%, P = .0022). Adjusted hazard ratio was 38.55 (95% CI 3.72-399.36), though marked by extreme instability from sparse data stratification and small event counts.
