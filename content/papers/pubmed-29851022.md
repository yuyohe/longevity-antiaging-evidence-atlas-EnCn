## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 4,588 initial hits, 31 studies including 258,540 individuals with dementia and 2,246,592 controls were analyzed. In longitudinal and open-label studies, AChEIs were associated with a significantly greater incidence of hypertension (n=1,573, 4%, 95% CI=2-8%, I2 =47%) and bradycardia (n=13,703, 2%, 95% CI=1-6%, I2 =98%). AChEIs were associated with a decrease in heart rate (SMD=-1.77, 95% CI=-3.58-0.03, I2 =78%) and an increase in PR interval (SMD=0.10, 95% CI=0.008-0.19, I2 =3%) from baseline.
