## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Forty-five studies involving over 730 000 participants were included. Higher MD adherence was associated with reduced risk or prevalence of Alzheimer's disease (odds ratios = 0.92), mild cognitive impairment (RR = 0.93), depression (RR = 0.96), and Parkinson's disease (RR = 0.90), with moderate certainty of evidence. Limited evidence suggested reduced anxiety and lower mortality among patients with Alzheimer's disease. No significant associations were observed for dementia prevalence or progression from mild cognitive impairment to dementia.
