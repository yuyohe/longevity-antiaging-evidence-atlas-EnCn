## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Sixteen reports represented eleven studies, all assessing handgrip strength; one small randomized trial also assessed knee extension strength. Ten studies (660 semaglutide-treated participants) contributed to the primary meta-analysis of uncontrolled within-group changes, showing a nonsignificant pooled handgrip strength change (-0.16 kgf; 95% confidence interval: -1.98 to 1.65), with substantial heterogeneity (I2 = 90.8%) and a 95% prediction interval from -5.96 to 5.63 kgf. Duration-stratified and sensitivity analyses were also nonsignificant. Individual study findings were heterogeneous. Certainty was very low.

## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Available evidence is insufficient to determine whether semaglutide is associated with changes in handgrip strength because the principal synthesis was based on uncontrolled within-group changes, with very low certainty and significant heterogeneity. These findings do not establish strength preservation or a causal treatment effect. More robust comparative studies with standardized strength assessment and longer follow-up are needed.
