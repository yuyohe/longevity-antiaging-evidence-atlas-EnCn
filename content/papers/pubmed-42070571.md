## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: From June 10, 2023, to Feb 4, 2025, 108 participants (53 women and 55 men) were enrolled, with 54 participants in each of the semaglutide and placebo treatment groups, and all were included in the data analysis. Overall, 88 participants (81%) completed the full intervention. Semaglutide was associated with a reduction in heavy drinking days (-41·1 percentage points from baseline, 95% CI -48·7 to -33·5) compared with placebo (-26·4, -34·1 to -18·6; estimated treatment difference -13·7 percentage points, -22·0 to -5·4; p=0·0015), and had substantial effects on multiple secondary alcohol-related and somatic outcomes. Adverse events were transient, generally mild to moderate gastrointestinal effects, and occurred more frequently in the semaglutide group.
