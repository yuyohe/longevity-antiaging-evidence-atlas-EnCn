EN: Sleep apnoea (SA) is a common condition associated with obesity, conferring significant cardiovascular and metabolic risks. This study investigates the causal effects of obesity phenotypes throughout the life course, from birth weight to adult obesity, on SA risk using Mendelian randomization (MR). A two-sample MR approach was utilized, employing genome-wide association study (GWAS) data from individuals of European ancestry.
