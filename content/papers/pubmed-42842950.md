## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: In a real-world cohort, GLP-1/GIP RAs versus finerenone initiation was associated with a lower relative hazard of adverse kidney outcomes, without evidence of a difference in absolute risk. This was accompanied by total, but not chronic, eGFR slope mitigation. Residual confounding from under-sampled albuminuria is possible, and findings should be interpreted within this specific matched population.
