EN: Age-related eye diseases (AREDs)-including age-related macular degeneration (AMD), glaucoma, and diabetic retinopathy-are leading causes of permanent blindness. Current treatments manage clinical manifestations but do not halt the molecular processes that drive disease progression. This limitation has shifted attention toward "geroscience," a strategy that targets the fundamental biology of aging rather than treating each disease in isolation. Four key hallmarks of aging-mitochondrial dysfunction, loss of proteostasis, cellular senescence, and epigenetic drift-are widely implicated in AREDs.
