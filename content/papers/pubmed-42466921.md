EN: Peripheral artery disease (PAD) is a prevalent manifestation of systemic atherosclerosis, associated with elevated risks of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) and major adverse limb events (MALE). Despite its clinical significance, PAD remains underdiagnosed and undertreated, reflecting substantial gaps in guideline implementation. This review highlights advances in the medical management of PAD, focusing on strategies targeting the metabolic, lipid, immuno-inflammatory, and thrombotic drivers of disease to improve cardiovascular and limb outcomes.
