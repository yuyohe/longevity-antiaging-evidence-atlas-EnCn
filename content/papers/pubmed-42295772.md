EN: Of the 1173 participants (median age, 74 years [IQR, 70-80]; 795 [68%] were female), 997 (85%) experienced greater than or equal to 2 conditions (median, 5 [IQR, 3-7]) by the end of follow-up (316 of 385 [82%], 327 of 385 [85%], and 350 of 403 [87%], respectively, among lifestyle, metformin, and placebo groups).
