## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Greater abundance of Collinsella was detected to increase the risk of GDM. Our study also found suggestive associations among Coprobacter, Olsenella, Lachnoclostridium, Prevotella9, Ruminococcus2, Oscillibacte, and Methanobrevibacter with GDM.
