## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The study revealed significant causal associations: eosinophil counts were positively associated with FI (odds ratio (OR) = 1.063, p = 1.4e-07). Mean corpuscular volume (MCV) was linked to telomere length (OR = 0.978, p = 0.0001). Lymphocyte counts showed causal connections with epigenetic aging clocks (GrimAge acceleration: OR = 0.614, p = 7.0e-08; Hannum age acceleration: OR = 0.519, p = 5.4e-11; and PhenoAge acceleration: OR = 0.519, p = 5.4e-11).
