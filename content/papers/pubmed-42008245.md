## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Prepregnancy care and counseling optimize maternal health before conception to improve outcomes for mothers and infants. In the US, 66.4% of reproductive-aged women have at least 1 modifiable risk factor for adverse pregnancy outcomes. For all individuals desiring pregnancy, recommended interventions include folic acid supplementation; cessation of tobacco, alcohol, cannabis, and opioids; immunizations against hepatitis B virus, varicella, and rubella; and screening for syphilis and HIV. Folic acid use before pregnancy is associated with reduced fetal neural tube defects (relative risk [RR], 0.67; 95% CI, 0.52-0.87).
