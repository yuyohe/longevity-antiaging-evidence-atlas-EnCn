## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Independent analyses demonstrated reduced mortality risk among survivors with sufficient PA (PA ≥ 600 metabolic equivalent minutes/week) and adequate sleep time (7-9 hours/d), whereas those reporting trouble sleeping showed elevated risk. Joint analyses demonstrated that survivors with sufficient PA and either adequate sleep time or without trouble sleeping had significantly lower all-cause mortality (hazard ratio [HR]: 0.50, 95% confidence interval [CI]: 0.39-0.64; HR: 0.48, 95% CI: 0.37-0.62, respectively) and cancer-specific mortality (HR: 0.56, 95% CI: 0.36-0.86; HR: 0.46, 95% CI: 0.30-0.70, respectively). Importantly, abnormal sleep time (<7 or >9 hours/d) and trouble sleeping were not associated with risk of all-cause or noncancer mortality among individuals who achieved sufficient PA.
