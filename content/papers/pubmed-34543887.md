## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: After a comprehensive literature search, only three phase 2 RCTs on the use of metformin as adjunctive therapy for locally advanced and metastatic breast cancer were included. Clinical trials on early breast cancer are still ongoing and none were included in the present review.
