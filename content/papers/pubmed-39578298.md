## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Dietary restriction (DR) extends lifespan in diverse species, from yeast to mammals. However, its underlying mechanisms are not well understood. In this study, through using the tractable model Caenorhabditis elegans, we show a role for the DEAD-box RNA helicase, DDX-23 (homologous to mammal DDX23) as a regulator of healthspan in response to dietary restriction. Meanwhile, DDX-23 is also required for heat and oxidative stress response in C. elegans. Intriguingly, DDX-23 functions in the germline during adult to regulate dietary restriction-induced longevity.
