## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Renal proximal tubular epithelial cell (PTEC) senescence and defective autophagy contribute to kidney aging, but the mechanisms remain unclear. Caveolin-1 (CAV1), a crucial component of cell membrane caveolae, regulates autophagy and is associated with cellular senescence. However, its specific role in kidney aging is poorly understood. In this study, we generated Cav1 gene knockout mice and induced kidney aging using D-galactose (D-gal).
