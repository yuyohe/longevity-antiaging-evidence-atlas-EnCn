## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 16 822 semaglutide and 11 986 active comparator new users, the adjusted risk of incident SI was 0.08% (13.0 events) for semaglutide and 0.05% (8.9 events) for active comparators, and for incident suicidality was 0.08% (14.0 events) for semaglutide and 0.07% (11.0 events) for active comparators. Adjusted RDs were 0.02% (95% CI: [-0.02%, 0.07%]) for SI and 0.02% (-0.03%, 0.07%) for suicidality, with corresponding RRs of 1.36 (0.62, 6.14) and 1.18 (0.57, 3.63).

## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Among patients initiating semaglutide for weight management, semaglutide did not appear to increase the risk of suicide-related outcomes. However, the CIs of the RDs are compatible with a 0.07% increase in 6-month risk and the true RD could be larger due to the potentially low sensitivity of ICD-10 diagnosis codes for and underreporting of suicide-related outcomes.
