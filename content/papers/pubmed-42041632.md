## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Background: Early vascular aging (EVA) is a common complication of type 2 diabetes mellitus. Early identification is crucial in middle-aged individuals with T2DM, as vascular stiffness can occur gradually for years before cardiovascular disease. However, EVA is rarely considered in routine care. Adropin is a vasoprotective peptide that may counter-regulate endothelin-1 (ET-1). Therefore, this study aims to examine the association between circulating adropin, ET-1, oxLDL, MMP-2, VEGFA, and EVA.
