## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 11 articles representing 5 trials were included in our systematic review and meta-analysis. When compared to a placebo, PCSK9 MaB significantly reduced the risk of MACEs ( I 2 = 0%, p = 0.63, RR [95% CI] = 0.88 [0.81, 0.97], p < 0.01) and the recurrence rate of ACS ( I 2 = 45%, p = 0.18, RR [95% CI] = 0.89 [0.83, 0.95], p < 0.01).
