## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Serum klotho levels did not differ significantly in the two treatment arms. In the arms combined, klotho was inversely associated with waist circumference (β= -0.007, p = 0.01) in the women but not in the men, after adjustment for age, physical activity, treatment assignment, kidney function, season and time of klotho measurement. Serum klotho was not associated with odds of falling or with proportion of body fat or lean mass, grip strength, or spine or hip BMD or their 3-year changes in men or women.
