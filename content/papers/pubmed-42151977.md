## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 13,504 records screened, 56 studies were included with a total of 450,520 participants. Using the mMRC Dyspnoea Scale, (n = 43), the pooled prevalence of dyspnoea was 22.2% [95% CI 17.5-27.2]. 6.1% [95% CI 4.3-8.2] reported moderate dyspnoea (mMRC 2), 2.5% [95% CI 1.5-3.6] described moderate-severe dyspnoea (mMRC 3), and 1.2% [95% CI 0.7-1.9] reported severe dyspnoea (mMRC 4). Women reported more dyspnoea than men (6.7% vs. 4.3%). Rates of dyspnoea were higher in North America than in Australia/New Zealand (29.4% vs. 19.6%). No clear differences were noted between low-income countries and high-income countries, although data were limited.
