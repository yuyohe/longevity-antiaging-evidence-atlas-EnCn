## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Nine hundred thirteen individuals with RA and 913 non-RA comparators were included (mean age: 65 years, 65 % female in both cohorts). During the median follow-up of 10.4 years in RA and 11.0 years in non-RA cohort, SD developed in 234 and 206 individuals, respectively. RA patients experienced an increased risk for any incident SD (HR 1.34; 95 % CI:1.11-1.61) and insomnia (HR 1.34; 95 % CI:1.03-1.73). Obesity, dyslipidemia, presence of CVD, depression, anxiety, and more recent calendar year of RA incidence were associated with increased risk of any SD in RA. There were no significant association between SD overall and by subtype with dementia in RA.
