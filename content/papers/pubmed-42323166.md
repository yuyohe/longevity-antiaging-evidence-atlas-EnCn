## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The semaglutide group had significantly larger reductions from baseline in ad libitum energy intake relative to placebo at weeks 20, 40, and 60, consuming a mean ± standard error of 291.9 ± 64.4, 240.2 ± 87.8, and 269.5 ± 83.6 kcal less, respectively. At week 20, semaglutide-treated participants had significantly greater increases from baseline in appetite suppression after the standardized breakfast [mean difference (MD) = 2197 ± 823] and greater reductions in past-week hunger (MD = -17.4 ± 4.8) and food preoccupation (MD = -14.9 ± 4.5). However, the groups did not differ significantly in appetite outcomes at weeks 40 or 60. The semaglutide group had greater reductions in responsiveness to food at weeks 20 and 40 (MD = -0.4 ± 0.2 and -0.6 ± 0.2, respectively).
