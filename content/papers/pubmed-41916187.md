## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: We included 4955 participants (70.8% women) from 15 countries, with a mean age of 69.6 (SD: 10.1) years. Vigorous PA was significantly associated with a higher quality of life. Compared to those who hardly ever engaged in vigorous PA, participants who reported doing so 1-3 times a month (β = 2.357, 95%CI: 1.451-3.262), once per week (β = 2.287, 95%CI: 1.517-3.057) or more than once per week (β = 2.462, 95%CI: 1.832-3.092) had significantly higher CASP-12 scores. Current smoking status and underweight were also associated with lower quality of life, while no significant differences were found in overweight or obese categories.
