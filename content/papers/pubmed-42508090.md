## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Sixteen studies were included in the systematic review; four arms from three trials (209 participants) contributed to the primary meta-analysis. The pooled treatment difference in ad libitum lunch energy intake was -1132 kJ (95% confidence interval [CI] -1449 to -815), approximately -271 kcal, with no heterogeneity (I-squared = 0%; 95% prediction interval -1828 to -436 kJ).
