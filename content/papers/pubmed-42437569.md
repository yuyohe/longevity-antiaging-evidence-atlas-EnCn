## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 46 infants, gestational age (p=0.16) and sex (p=0.55) did not differ between groups. The protein group had higher week 4 Shannon diversity than standard fortification (median 1.2 vs. 0.87, p=0.049). Week 4 Shannon diversity was positively correlated with fat-free mass z-score at 36 weeks PMA (r2=0.34, p=0.02).
