## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Randomized evidence for antihypertensive deprescribing in long-term care remains sparse and clinically heterogeneous. Within individual trials, supervised deprescribing reduced antihypertensive exposure and raised blood pressure, but the magnitude and clinical consequences varied by strategy and follow-up. Current evidence does not establish a common effect on mortality, hospitalization, or other resident-important outcomes, making trial-specific estimates and deprescribing protocols more informative than a single pooled treatment effect.
