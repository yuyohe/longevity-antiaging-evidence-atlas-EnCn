## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Thirty-two participants were randomized. By month 6, body weight did not change significantly in the TRE group (-2.19% [95% CI -5.70, -1.31]) or CR group (-0.47% [95% CI -4.72, 3.78]) relative to a control group, or between TRE and CR groups (-1.73% [95% CI -5.37, 1.92]). HbA1c levels were significantly reduced by the TRE group when compared with the CR group (-0.46% [95% CI -0.80, -0.12]). There were no significant differences in total insulin dose or mean glucose levels across groups. TRE did not increase the risk for diabetic ketoacidosis, severe hypoglycemia, or severe hyperglycemia.
