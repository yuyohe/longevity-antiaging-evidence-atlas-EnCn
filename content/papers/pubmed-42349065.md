## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Twelve studies (2006-2024) met inclusion criteria, including 11 RCTs (92%) and one post-hoc analysis (8%), with cohorts predominantly male and obese and comparators of placebo or standard care. All studies assessed BP; five reported marginal or no benefit and six suggested BP reductions, often adherence dependent. Cardiovascular event findings were inconsistent. Only four trials had extractable SBP data and three for DBP. Pooled estimates showed no significant SBP reduction (-0.42 mmHg; 95% CI -3.52 to 2.68; p = 0.69; I2 = 62%) but a significant DBP reduction (-2.33 mmHg; 95% CI -3.32 to -1.35; p = 0.01; I2 = 0%).
