## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Dietitian-supervised therapy produced significantly greater improvements than physician-led care in SMI (between-group difference +2.50 cm2/m2; 95% CI 1.30-3.70), HGS (men: +7.5 kg, 95% CI 5.39-9.61; women: +4.8 kg, 95% CI 3.12-6.48) and gait velocity (+0.23 m/s; 95% CI 0.13-0.33) (all p < 0.001). Serum albumin increased (+0.48 g/dL between groups) and CTP score improved (-1.30 points), whereas MELD score remained unchanged. Overall multidomain response occurred in 68.9% versus 37.8% of participants (RR 1.82, 95% CI 1.36-2.44; number needed to treat 3.2). Dietitian-supervised therapy independently predicted SMI response (OR 3.82; 95% CI 1.80-8.10). No significant differences were observed in mortality, hospitalisation or other major clinical events.
