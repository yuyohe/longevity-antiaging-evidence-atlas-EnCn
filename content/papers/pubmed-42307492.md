## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over a mean of 14.5 years of follow-up (1,630,964 person-years), 5,459 incident major CVD events occurred. Compared with no resistance training, women performing ≥2 h/wk had a 20% lower risk of major CVD (HR: 0.80; 95% CI: 0.69-0.92; P for trend = 0.007), and each additional 1 h/wk was associated with a 5% lower risk (HR per 1 h/wk: 0.95; 95% CI: 0.92-0.99). The inverse association was stronger for MI (HR for ≥2 h/wk vs none: 0.56; 95% CI: 0.41-0.76) but not evident for stroke (HR: 0.99; 95% CI: 0.80-1.23). Women who met recommendations for aerobic activity (≥15 metabolic equivalent of task hours per week), resistance training (≥1 h/wk), and low sedentary television viewing (<2 h/d) had a lower risk of major CVD (HR: 0.60; 95% CI: 0.53-0.69), than those meeting aerobic and low sedentary television viewing recommendations but not resistance training (HR: 0.73; 95% CI: 0.67-0.80).
