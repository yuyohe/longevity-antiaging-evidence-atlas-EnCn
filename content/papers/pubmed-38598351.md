## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: CPET was safe and well tolerated, with 95.8% of participants able to complete the V̇O 2peak phase of the protocol. Only 56 (6.4%) participants had a risk alert and only two adverse events occurred: a fall and atrial fibrillation. Mean ± SD V̇O 2peak was 20.2 ± 4.8 mL·kg -1 ·min -1 , peak heart rate 142 ± 18 bpm, and peak respiratory exchange ratio 1.14 ± 0.09. Adjudication was indicated in 47 tests; 20 were evaluated as valid and 27 as invalid (18 data collection errors, 9 did not reach V̇O 2peak ). Reproducibility of V̇O 2peak was high (intraclass correlation coefficient = 0.97).
