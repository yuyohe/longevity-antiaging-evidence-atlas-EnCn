## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: All participants completed the trial. For the CM questionnaire, between-group differences were 25.2 points at 12 weeks (95% Confidence Interval [CI], 17.7-29.8; P < 0.001) and 31.5 points at 1 year (95% CI, 26.4-36.5; P < 0.001) in favor of the supervised exercise group. Similarly, at 12 weeks and 1 year, between-group differences in secondary outcomes were also statistically significant in favor of the supervised exercise group and surpassed the minimal clinically important difference threshold. Moreover, after 12 weeks, the percentage of participants who responded positively was significantly higher in the supervised exercise group (P< 0.001). Finally, exploratory analysis revealed that participants in the supervised exercise group showed better short- and long-term responses to treatment (P < 0.001).
