EN: There were 1093 RCTs that included adults ≥18 years; 207 (18.9%) excluded older adults by design. The weighted mean age by trial size across all included studies that reported a mean age (n=1029) was 53.5 years, which differed across trial subject area and country income. Participants in trials conducted in high-income countries (n=581 studies; weighted mean age 63.7 years) were older than those conducted in mixed income (n=168 studies; 49.8 years) and low-income countries (n=272 studies, 27.8 years).
