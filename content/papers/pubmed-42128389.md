## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Knee OA independently increased risks for overall CVD (HR 1.13, 95% CI 1.11-1.16), MI (1.07, 1.02-1.13), stroke (1.14, 1.10-1.18), and HF (1.13, 1.09-1.17). Cardiovascular mortality was not significantly increased (HR 0.99, 95% CI 0.92-1.07). The association was stronger among non-insulin users and patients with diabetes duration < 5 years. Even intermittent PA significantly reduced cardiovascular mortality among OA patients (HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.61-0.78).
