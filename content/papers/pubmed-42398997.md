EN: Of the 469 162 participants without CV disease at baseline, 56.3% were female. The mean age was 57 (SD: 9.6) years. ## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: This large observation study shows that obesity could lead to CV death as early as 10 years follow-up period, while individuals who were obese were more likely to have their first CV event after 4 years follow-up. No association between being overweight and CV death was observed in any follow-up time. Further studies are needed to evaluate the impact of the follow-up period on associations between overweight/obesity and CV outcomes. Screening or early intervention for obesity can be highly valuable for preventing and managing CV outcomes in a primary care setting.
