## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Diminished ovarian reserve (DOR) and primary ovarian insufficiency (POI) are major causes of female infertility. A prospective study (2018-2023) of an international cohort of 120 patients with unexplained DOR was performed using a large custom targeted next-generation sequencing panel including all known POI-causing genes.
