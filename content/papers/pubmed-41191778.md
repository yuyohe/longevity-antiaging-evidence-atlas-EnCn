## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Extrafollicular age-associated B cells (ABCs) excessively expand and produce autoantibodies in systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE), and the regulatory mechanism remains elusive. We found that the m6A demethylase fat mass and obesity-associated protein (FTO) was highly expressed in ABCs from patients with SLE, which was positively associated with renal immune damage. FTO overexpression in murine and human B cells facilitated ABC expansion and exacerbated SLE in lupus-prone mice, whereas FTO ablation ameliorated ABC-driven autoimmunity. FTO expression was up-regulated upon activation of the toll-like receptor 7-myeloid differentiation primary response protein 88 (TLR7-MyD88) signaling pathway. FTO, in turn, promoted TLR7-driven ABC differentiation by targeting ATPase H+ transporting V1 subunit G1 (ATP6V1G1), a subunit of the vacuolar H+-ATPase (V-ATPase), in an m6A-dependent manner.
