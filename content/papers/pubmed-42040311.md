## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Thirty-seven ferroptosis-linked differentially expressed genes (FDEGs) were identified, enriched in ferroptosis, mitophagy, and immune pathways. Three hub genes (MAPK8, CREB1, GPX4) showed robust diagnostic utility (LASSO AUC=0.931; RF AUC=0.993 in GSE76293) and correlated with monocytes/neutrophils/activated NK cells. LPS suppressed their mRNA/protein levels in rats, reversed by FMT.
