## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Overall, 115 participants completed the study. FJH-KO supplementation showed significant between-group differences in platelet aggregation time and ba-PWV. Notably, the high-dose group demonstrated a significant reduction in ba-PWV compared to the placebo group (p = 0.006), reflecting reduced arterial stiffness. High-dose FJH-KO was also associated with a within-group reduction in P-selectin, while intercellular adhesion molecule-1 (ICAM-1) decreased in the low-dose and placebo groups.
