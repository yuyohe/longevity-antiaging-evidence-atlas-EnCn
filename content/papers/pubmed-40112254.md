## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Twenty-two studies were included (n = 968). Exercise induced small to large statistically significant reductions in IL-6 (standardized mean difference [SMD] = -0.85; 95% CI = -1.68 to -0.02; P = .05) and TNF-α (SMD = -0.40; 95% CI = -0.81 to 0.01; P = .05) and a trend for a decrease in CRP. When stratifying by exercise mode, trends toward reduction in IL-6 and TNF-α were observed for combined exercise, while changes were not generally affected by exercise program duration.
