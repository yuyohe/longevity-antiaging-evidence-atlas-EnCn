## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Obesity was independently associated with elevated SA-ARDS incidence under the new definition (adjusted OR 5.61, 95% CI 4.56-6.92; AUC = 0.700), with even higher risk in ARDS patients under Berlin criteria (OR 6.66, 95% CI 5.01-8.91) and HFNC recipients (OR 5.77, 95% CI 3.85-8.85).
