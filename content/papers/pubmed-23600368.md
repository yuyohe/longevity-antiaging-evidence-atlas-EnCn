EN: Following 3 months of treatment, low-density lipoprotein (LDL) particle size increased equally in all groups. Similarly, all treatments were associated with comparable small dense LDL cholesterol reduction. Apolipoprotein B levels decreased by 15%, 7% and 4% in the rosuvastatin, add-on ER-NA/LRPT and add-on fenofibrate group, respectively (P < 0.01 for all compared with baseline, P < 0.01 for all comparisons between groups).
