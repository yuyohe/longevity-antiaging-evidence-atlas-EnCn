## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The 160 participants (pooled tirzepatide doses n = 124, placebo n = 36) with baseline and end of study DXA data were 73% female and had a mean weight of 102.5 kg and body mass index of 38.0 kg/m2. The change in body weight, fat mass and lean mass from baseline to Week 72 was -21.3%, -33.9% and -10.9% with tirzepatide and -5.3%, -8.2% and -2.6% with placebo, respectively (p < 0.001 for all comparisons). Of the body weight lost, approximately 75% was fat mass and 25% was lean mass for both tirzepatide and placebo. These proportions remained consistent across most subgroup analyses.
