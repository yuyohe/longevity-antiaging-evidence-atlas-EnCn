## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Baseline measures were similar between groups (p > 0.05). Both groups improved significantly in 30-s Chair Stand, 2-min Step test, Arm Curl, Chair Sit-and-Reach, Back Scratch, and Handgrip Strength. 2.4-meter Up-and-Go test improved only in the 2SW group. The 3SW group showed greater increases in handgrip strength (+ 28% vs. +7%) and HDL cholesterol (+ 9% vs. +1%), while total cholesterol and LDL reductions were comparable between groups (p< 0.05). No significant changes occurred in fasting glucose or triglycerides.
