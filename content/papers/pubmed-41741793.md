EN: Biological aging reflects complex cellular and biochemical processes that can be measured across multiple omic layers. Using routine clinical laboratory data from ~31,000 participants in the Mass General Brigham Biobank, we developed EMRAge, a biomarker of mortality risk that can be broadly recapitulated across electronic medical records. Here we show that EMRAge can be modeled using elastic net regression with DNA methylation and multi-omics to generate DNAmEMRAge and OMICmAge, respectively.
