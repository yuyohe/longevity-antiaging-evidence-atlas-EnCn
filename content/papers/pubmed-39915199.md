## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Both diets led to improvements in anthropometric measures and liver enzyme levels, with no significant differences between groups. However, the LCD group showed superior outcomes in reducing liver steatosis (-52.40 vs -44.63 dB/m; P < 0.001) and fibrosis (-0.74 vs -0.004 Kpa; P = 0.01) compared to the IF group.
