## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Twenty-six independent studies were included: 13 comparative and 13 single-arm pre-post studies. Fifteen were mixed, six indeterminate, four jointly inconclusive, and one uncontrolled study uniformly concordant for intermediate outcomes; no comparative study or study of patient-important outcomes was uniformly concordant. Eleven studies assessed individual-level associations, responder differences, or mediation, but clear findings were isolated and not replicated across independent studies. No intervention-clock-clinical outcome combination met the criteria for meta-analysis, and no trial-level surrogate-endpoint validation was identified. Certainty was very low across all assessed evidence bodies.
