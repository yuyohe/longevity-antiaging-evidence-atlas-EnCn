## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 1 033 522 adults, 932 659 (90.2%) initiated tirzepatide and 100 863 (9.8%) semaglutide; provider reach was higher among women than men. In September 2025, semaglutide initiation increased by 31.15 percentage points (95% CI: 26.07-36.24) relative to the projected pre-interruption trend.
