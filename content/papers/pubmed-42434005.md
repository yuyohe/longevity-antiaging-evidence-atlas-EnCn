## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Thirty HAE-C1INH patients from 20 unrelated families were included and compared with age/sex matched HC (n = 50). HAE-AS classified disease activity as high in 20% of patients while HAE-QoL was severely impaired in 10% of patients. The CGA documented a similar distribution of MMSE, GDS, IADL, and Tinetti between patients and HC. Comorbidities were similarly represented in both the groups as well as of CCI and CFS.
