## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The prognostic performance of the apolipoprotein panel for MACE showed an AUC (95% CI) of 0.648 (0.626-0.670), compared with 0.579 (0.557-0.602) for the lipid panel. For all-cause death, the apolipoprotein panel had an AUC of 0.699 (0.664-0.733), while the lipid panel had an AUC of 0.599 (0.564-0.635). Adding the apolipoprotein panel significantly improved the performance of the conventional lipid panel (P<0.0001): AUC, 0.659 (0.637-0.681) for MACE and 0.724 (0.691-0.756) for all-cause death. Higher risk for MACE based on the baseline apolipoprotein panel was found to predict greater treatment benefit with alirocumab.
