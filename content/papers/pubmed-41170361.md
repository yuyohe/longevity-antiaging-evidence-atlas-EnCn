## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The review included 28 studies involving 2,134 participants, followed up for an average of 12 weeks. Among these, 15 were RCTs and 13 were observational studies. The participants had a mean age of 45 ± 12 years, with 60% being female. The combined IHT and IF intervention demonstrated superior benefits, with significant weight loss (mean reduction: 6.3 kg, 95% CI: -8.2 to -4.5 kg). Cognitive performance showed domain-specific improvements: memory (SMD = 0.60, 95% CI: 0.43-0.77) and attention (SMD = 0.57, 95% CI: 0.40-0.74), though with significant heterogeneity (I 2 > 50%). Egger's test indicated minimal publication bias (p = 0.18).
