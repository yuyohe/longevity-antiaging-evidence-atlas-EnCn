## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 122,133 participants who were free of CVD at baseline (mean [SD] age, 56.0 [8.1] years; 65,442 [53.6%] women), 24,281 incident CVD cases and 3,614 premature deaths were reported. Restricted cubic splines showed progressively increasing risks of incident CVD and premature mortality associated with higher BA acceleration. Compared with the lowest quartile of KDM-BA acceleration, the largest adjusted HRs for incident CVD and premature mortality were 1.32 (95% CI 1.27-1.37) and 1.10 (95% CI 1.05-1.21) for quartile 4, respectively. For PhenoAge acceleration, the corresponding HRs were 1.23 (95% CI 1.19-1.28) and 1.21 (95% CI 1.10-1.33), respectively.
