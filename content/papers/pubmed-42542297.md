## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The median tirzepatide dose at 6 months was 7.5 mg. At this visit, the mean body weight reduction was 18.04 kg, representing a 17.21% decrease from baseline. A total of 95.35% of participants achieved at least 5% weight loss, and 60.47% achieved ≥15%. Significant improvements were observed in glycaemic and lipid profiles, with enhancements in health-related quality of life and a favourable tolerability profile. The dropout rate was 3.7%.
