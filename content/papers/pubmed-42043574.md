## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: UI women had prolonged lag time (+ 10.0%) and lower thrombin generation reflected by ETP (-17.8%) and formed clots less permeable (- 20.8%; Ks) and more resistant to lysis (+ 17.5% CLT) compared to controls (all p < 0.05). Women with UI and positive pregnancy outcome (n = 19, 27.1%) showed increased KS (+ 67.9%) along with shorter CLT (- 15.9%) with no difference in thrombin generation, when compared to non-pregnant women.
