## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of the 6,500 patients selected for the study; 2,876 patients were prior metformin users. After propensity score matching (PSM), a total of 5,752 patients (2,876 patients in each group) were included in the analysis. The hazard of 30-day mortality in metformin users was not significantly different compared to the control group [hazard ratio (HR), 1.05; 95% confidence interval (CI), 0.97-1.14; P=0.154].
