EN: PURPOSE: Time-restricted eating (TRE) combined with calorie restriction (CR) has been proposed as a promising approach for weight management after bariatric surgery. Evidence on its effectiveness among patients experiencing weight regain following sleeve gastrectomy remains limited. METHODS: In this 12-week randomized controlled trial, 62 patients with ≥ 10% weight regain two years after sleeve gastrectomy were randomly assigned to a TRE + CR diet (8-hour eating window) or a CR-only diet.
