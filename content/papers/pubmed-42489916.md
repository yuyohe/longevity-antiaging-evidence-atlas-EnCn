## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 127 patients, 14.17% developed AKI, 66.7% of which were severe. The 28-day mortality was 11.8% overall and 44.4% among patients with AKI. In the multivariate analysis, elevated serum lactate was associated with higher risk of AKI at 24 h postoperatively (HR 1.61; 95%CI 1.24-2.08). Prolonged CRT at ICU admission was also a significant predictor (HR 1.80; 95%CI 1.03-3.15). Higher surgical complexity (RACHS-1), vasopressor use, and low systolic blood pressure were also associated with AKI.
