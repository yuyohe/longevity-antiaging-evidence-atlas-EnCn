## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Thirteen RCTs comprising 15 exercise-control comparisons involving 356 participants were included. Exercise significantly improved CRF compared with control conditions (SMD = 0.82, 95% CI: 0.54-1.10; I2 = 30.1%). In the MD-based analysis restricted to studies reporting CRF in mL/kg/min, exercise increased CRF by 3.52 mL/kg/min (95% CI: 2.04-5.01; I2 = 65.5%). Exploratory dose-response analyses suggested non-linear associations, with estimated peak points of approximately 590 MET-min/week for weekly exercise volume and 10,715 MET-min for total cumulative exercise volume.
