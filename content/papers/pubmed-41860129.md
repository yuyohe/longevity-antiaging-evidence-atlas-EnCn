## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 6083 patients (median age 73 years, 34% female), follow-up data were available for 72% in year 1, 56% in year 2 and 44% in year 3. Year 3 LDL-C was not measured in 48% of patients, and 19% were at target (<1.8 mmol/L). For SBP, 16% lacked a measurement, and 50% were at target (<140 mmHg). Female sex was associated with lower probability of having LDL-C measured or having LDL-C or SBP at target. CR participation, structured diabetes follow-up or having a chronic ischaemic heart disease code in the EHR was associated with a higher probability of LDL-C and SBP measurements at 2 and 3 years.
