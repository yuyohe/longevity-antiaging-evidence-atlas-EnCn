## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Screening 28 291 abstracts identified 41 articles meeting inclusion criteria. Nineteen articles from 14 unique RCTs conducted 1956-2020, enrolling 14 065 CVD patients, compared fat-replacement, Mediterranean, low-fat, low-sodium, and high-fibre diets with minimal/no dietary advice. Pooled results showed no diet intervention significantly impacted mortality/CVD outcomes. However, stratified meta-analysis revealed a Mediterranean-diet significantly reduced CVD mortality (RR: 0.40; 95% CI: 0.19, 0.83).
