EN: Two hundred and sixty-nine patients without T2DM and 138 patients with T2DM were selected after the propensity score matching. The 5-year OFS of patients with T2DM was significantly lower than that of those without T2DM, both before (P = 0.019) and after (P = 0.014) the propensity score matching.
