## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Bone injury generates a senescent vascular niche that markedly disrupts MSC-mediated regeneration. Targeted rejuvenation of this niche using sustained-release quercetin effectively restores MSC function and significantly accelerates bone reconstruction. These findings highlight the aging microenvironment as a key therapeutic target for improving MSC-based treatments for bone defects.
