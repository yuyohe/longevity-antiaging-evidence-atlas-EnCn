EN: Type 2 diabetes (T2D) is a prevalent disease worldwide that increases the risk of cardiovascular (CV) complications, disability and mortality. While excessive alcohol consumption is harmful, the effects of moderate wine consumption remain debated. This review evaluates whether moderate wine intake affects the risk of developing T2D and its impact on subjects with T2D. Twenty-eight studies were analysed.
