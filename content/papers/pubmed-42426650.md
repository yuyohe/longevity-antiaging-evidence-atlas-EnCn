## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The overall ICU mortality rate was 75% (135/180). Non-survivors were significantly older than survivors (75 vs. 61 years, p = 0.003). Vasopressor use (83.7% vs. 24.4%, p = 0.001) and mechanical ventilation (99.3% vs. 75.6%, p = 0.001) were markedly more common among non-survivors. Multivariate analysis identified age (OR 1.04; p = 0.001), vasopressor use (OR 13.42; p = 0.0001), and mechanical ventilation (OR 14.19; p = 0.018) as independent predictors of mortality. ROC analysis showed good discriminatory performance for vasopressor use (AUC 0.810), mechanical ventilation (AUC 0.780), and age ≥ 68 years (AUC 0.720). No statistically significant association between specific antimicrobial combination regimens and survival was observed.
