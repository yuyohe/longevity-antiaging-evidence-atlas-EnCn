EN: Metformin use was associated with increased grip strength (OR = 2.46; 95% CI 1.49-2.38) and skeletal muscle mass (OR = 1.24; 95% CI 0.20-2.28), as well as reduced mortality (HR = 0.62; 95% CI 0.54-0.71).
