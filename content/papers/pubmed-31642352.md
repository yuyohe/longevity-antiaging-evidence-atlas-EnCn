## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: After the end of intervention, statistically significant between-group differences were seen in favour of the high-dose group in all outcomes: VO2peak 2.6 (mL kg-1 min-1) (95% confidence interval (CI): 0.4-4.8), maximal workload 0.3 W kg-1 (95%CI: 0.02-0.5), isometric muscle strength 0.7 N m kg-1 (95%CI: 0.1-1.2) and muscle power 0.3 W kg-1 (95%CI: 0.04-0.6). After 12 months, a significant between-group difference only persisted in VO2peak and maximal workload.
