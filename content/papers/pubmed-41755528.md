EN: While symptoms of insomnia are common in older adults with mild cognitive impairment (MCI) and predict future cognitive decline, a robust evidence base for treating insomnia in MCI is lacking. This study investigated the feasibility of recruiting and delivering a digital cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (dCBT-I) in this group. In a parallel open-label randomised-controlled feasibility trial, participants were recruited and screened via the internet and assessed for MCI using videoconference methods.
