## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Prophylactic propranolol significantly reduces PSH incidence, shortens MV duration, and decreases ICU stay in moderate-to-severe TBI. Although adding gabapentin further reduces PSH, it prolongs recovery time, suggesting a trade-off between efficacy and sedative effects. These findings suggest that propranolol monotherapy is a promising prophylactic strategy, with gabapentin potentially reserved for refractory cases. However, given the study's limitations, these results should be considered hypothesis-generating and warrant confirmation in larger, multicenter trials. Mortality and neurological outcomes were comparable across groups.
