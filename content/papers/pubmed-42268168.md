## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During a median follow-up of 10.6 years (Q1-Q3: 4.6-15.0 years), 34 patients (6.7%) experienced the primary outcome (HF-related death: 22 [4.4%] and heart transplantation: 12 [2.4%]).
