## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Mean apoB was 89.9 ± 27.6 mg/dL, whereas mean RW-apoB was 103.7 ± 36.3 mg/dL. Overall, 60.0% of participants were reclassified by ≥ 10 percentile points. In individuals with Lp(a) < 75 nmol/L, measured apoB slightly overestimated weighted atherogenic burden (median Δ percentile: -13.2). Conversely, apoB progressively underestimated weighted atherogenic burden with increasing Lp(a), reaching an RW-apoB/apoB ratio of 1.91 and median Δ percentile of +34.7 at Lp(a) ≥300 nmol/L.
