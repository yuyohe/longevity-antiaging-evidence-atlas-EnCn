## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among the participants (42 men and 8 women; m-prehab [n = 25], control [n = 24]; mean age, 65.3 years), those in the m-prehab group consumed significantly more protein than those in the control group (76.8 ± 16.6 vs 61.6 ± 18.0 g/day, p < 0.01). The m-prehab and control groups showed no significant intergroup difference in preoperative daily step counts (7590 and 7910, respectively) or change in AT. Quadriceps strength increased significantly more in the m-prehab group than in the control group (3.7 ± 13.5 vs - 5.3 ± 16.0; p = 0.04), and the skeletal muscle index showed a nonsignificant but increasing trend (4.0 ± 6.5 vs 1.1 ± 4.8; p = 0.09). Estimated protein intake was unassociated, whereas preoperative step count was significantly correlated positively with change in AT (r = 0.412; p = 0.02).
