EN: From 2011 to 2020, the overall pooled prevalence of frailty was the lowest in the VPA group (20.42%) and the normal sleep duration group (19.85%) compared with all other subgroups (P < 0.001). Three distinct frailty trajectories were identified: low-level stable, medium-level increase, and high-level increase.
