## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Senescence of T cells is strongly linked to organismal aging through two interconnected processes: chronic low-grade inflammation and reduced immune surveillance of senescent cells. T cells are particularly vulnerable to thymic involution, hematopoietic stem cell aging, repeated homeostatic proliferation, chronic antigenic stimulation, and metabolic and mitochondrial dysfunction. As a result, aged T cells may lose their capacity to combat infection and eliminate senescent cells, while also contributing to inflammaging through the production of inflammatory cytokines.
