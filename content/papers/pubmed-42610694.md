## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Women in the highest tertile of total midlife PA had 20% lower odds of reporting ≥2 SCCs compared with those in the lowest tertile (OR = 0.80; 95% CI = 0.68-0.95). The inverse association was stronger among never smokers (OR = 0.68; p for interaction=0.029). The inverse association was consistent for moderate and vigorous, but not mild PA. Findings were consistent across sensitivity analyses using alternative SCC definitions, exclusion criteria, and inverse probably weights.
