EN: We investigated associations between food liking and risk of health outcomes in a prospective cohort and explored if associations were potentially explained by body mass index (BMI). We included 182,181 participants from UK Biobank. Food liking was measured using a 9-point hedonic scale, covering liking (points 6-9)/disliking (points 1-5) for high fat savoury (HFSa), high fat sweet (HFSw), vegetable, low fat savoury (LFSa), and fruit-based, low fat sweet (LFSw) foods.
