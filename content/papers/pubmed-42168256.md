## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: This study aimed to compare the effects of a single bout of beach tennis on post-exercise hypotension in men and women with hypertension. In this crossover trial, adults with hypertension were randomized into two experimental sessions: beach tennis and control sessions. For the beach tennis session, they warmed up with basic techniques for 5-min and played three matches lasting 12-min each, interspersed with 2-min recovery intervals. During the control session, the participants remained seated at rest without any exercise. Each protocol lasted for 45-min.
