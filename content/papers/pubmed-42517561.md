## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: USP10 level was decreased in the lung endothelium of human IPAH and rodent PH. AMPK/USP10 loop activation increased ACE2 Ser-680 phosphorylation and Lys-788 deubiquitination, thus contributing to the homeostatic level of ACE2 and lung vascular patency.
