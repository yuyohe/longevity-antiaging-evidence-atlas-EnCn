EN: After excluding 5746 persons without a baseline history of stroke, 15 249 patients with stroke (mean [SD] age, 64.1 [8.8] years; 6999 [45.9%] female; 8250 male [54.1%]) were included. Over a median (IQR) follow-up of 61.2 (60.9-61.6) months, the mean difference in systolic blood pressure was -2.05 mm Hg (95% CI, -3.03 to -1.08 mm Hg). A total of 2735 recurrent stroke events (691 fatal and 2044 nonfatal) and 3242 deaths were recorded. Recurrent stroke was significantly lower in the salt substitute vs regular salt group (rate ratio [RR], 0.86; 95% CI, 0.77-0.95; P = .005), with larger effects on hemorrhagic stroke (relative reduction, 30%; P = .002).
