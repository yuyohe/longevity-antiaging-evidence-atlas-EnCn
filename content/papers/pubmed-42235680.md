EN: Mesenchymal stem cells have emerged as a pivotal focus in regenerative medicine and therapeutic innovation due to their multipotent differentiation capacity and immunomodulatory properties. A major obstacle in maintaining human MSC potency and subsequently, the use of MSCs for cellular therapy is replicative senescence, or progressive aging.
