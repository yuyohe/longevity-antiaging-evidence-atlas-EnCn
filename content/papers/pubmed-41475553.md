## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Two hundred eighteen participants began the trial and are included in the analyses [143 CR, 75 AL; 69.7% females, age 38.1 ± 7.2 y (mean ± SD), BL body mass index: 25.1 ± 1.7 kg/m2]. One hundred eighty-eight participants completed the trial (82% CR, 95% AL). Average CR achieved during the 2-y intervention was 11.9% ± 7.2%. Diet quality scores improved during CR according to all 3 metrics, both within group and when compared with AL (all P < 0.01): PANDiet [CR BL 76.27%, 95% confidence interval: 75.45%, 77.09%; CR 2 y average 77.38% (76.58%, 78.19%)], HEI: [BL 59.40 (57.62, 61.18); 2 y average 66.83 (65.11, 68.55)], and DII: [BL -0.28 (-0.58, 0.01); 2 y average -1.05 (-1.35, -0.74)]. Nutritional adequacy was not compromised during the CR intervention.
