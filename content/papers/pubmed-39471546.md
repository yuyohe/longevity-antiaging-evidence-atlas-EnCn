## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During a median follow-up of 23.3 years, a total of 11333 participants died. UPF intake showed a nonlinear positive association with all-cause mortality, with more pronounced associations found in females (Pinteraction = 0.044); in females, UPF was linked to a higher mortality risk in a linear manner, while the association was J-shaped in males. Each standard deviation (SD) increment in UPF intake was associated with an increased risk of premature mortality (HR, 1.06; 95 % CI, 1.03-1.09), cardiovascular disease (CVD) mortality (HR, 1.05; 95 % CI, 1.01-1.08) or respiratory disease mortality (HR, 1.08; 95 % CI, 1.01-1.15), but not cancer mortality. The metabolic signature for UPF consumption (with 93 metabolites) was positively associated with all-cause mortality risk (HR per 1 SD, 1.23; 95 % CI, 1.06-1.42).
