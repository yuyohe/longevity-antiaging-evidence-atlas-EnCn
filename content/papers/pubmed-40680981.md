## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: GLP-1 RAs appear to be effective and generally well-tolerated in non-diabetic adults with overweight or obesity. Although some reviews reported more favorable weight outcomes with semaglutide, no definitive conclusions regarding the superiority of individual agents can be drawn from this umbrella review.
