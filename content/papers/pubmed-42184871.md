## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: We estimate that 63,223 out of 707,378 (9%) of participants across all trials were aged ≥65, despite older adults accounting for 18% of the US population and 76% of COVID-19-related deaths. Although only 35% of studies had explicit criteria for an upper age limit, we identified common study exclusion criteria, such as hypertension, diabetes, and cancer that are more prevalent among older adults and associated with lower mean participant ages when applied.
