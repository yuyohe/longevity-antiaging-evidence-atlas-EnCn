## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Twenty-seven studies (2012-2024), encompassing 1,505,821 participants, were included in the analysis. Major adverse cardiovascular events were reported in 21 studies, and all-cause mortality data were available from 19 studies. Meta-analysis revealed a significantly lower risk of both major adverse cardiovascular events (risk ratio [RR]: 0.79; 95% confidence interval [CI]: 0.73-0.84; p < 0.001) and all-cause mortality (RR: 0.79; 95% CI: 0.71-0.88; p < 0.001) in patients with diabetes treated with DPP-4 inhibitors plus metformin compared to those treated with SUs plus metformin. No publication bias was detected.
