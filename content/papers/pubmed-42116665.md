## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: All OFG doses (3, 12, 24, 36 and 45 mg) increased GI AEs compared to placebo, with a clear dose-response trend. High-dose OFG (45 mg) markedly increased nausea (OR 11.48, 95% CI: 6.52-20.21), vomiting (OR 11.48, 95% CI: 6.52-20.21), diarrhoea (OR 3.99, 95% CI: 2.07-7.70) and discontinuation due to GI AEs (OR 10.22, 95% CI: 4.99-20.94). No dose increased pancreatitis risk (p > 0.05). Higher doses significantly reduced ALT, with the most significant reduction observed at 24 mg (MD -11.19 IU/L, 95% CI: -19.18 to -3.21). Doses ≥ 12 mg increased lipase (e.g., 24 mg: +28.52 IU/L, 95% CI: 12.02-45.01) and pancreatic amylase (45 mg: +18.20 IU/L, 95% CI: 9.49-26.91), without corresponding increases in clinical events. AST and ALP levels remained similar to those of the placebo. Subgroup analyses showed consistent effects in patients with or without T2DM.
