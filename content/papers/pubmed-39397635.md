## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The final cohort comprised 737 KTR, with a median follow-up of 2,920 days. A total of 126 patients (17.1%) experienced MACE. High LDL-C levels and MACE risk were correlated by multivariate analysis (HR 1.008 per mg/dL, 95% CI: 1.001-1.016), while low HDL-C levels were not significantly associated with MACE (HR 0.992 per mg/dL, 95% CI: 0.976-1.009).
