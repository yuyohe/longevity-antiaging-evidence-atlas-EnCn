## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over a 12-year follow-up period, 373(7.2%) CVD were documented. The seven IR indices were strongly positively associated with CVD risk. Compare to other IR indices, CVAI was the strongest predictor for CVD (HR:1.84 95% CI: 1.45-2.35; p < 0.001) and showed the highest diagnostic value for CVD (AUC: 0.654 95% CI: 0.640-0.666; p < 0.001). Adding CVAI to the based model exhibited the maximum increased on incremental risk stratification for CVD (NRI: 0.355; IDI: 0.010; all p < 0.001). Moreover, there was a non-linear association between CVAI and CVD, with a threshold point of 96.21. The association between CVAI and CVD risk was more prominent among individuals aged<65 years, female, and non-hypertension (all P interaction<0.001).
