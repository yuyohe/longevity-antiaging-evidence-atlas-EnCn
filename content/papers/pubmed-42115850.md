## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Patients with MACEs had higher baseline ox-HDL-C, ox-LDL-C, and ox-Lp(a), correlating with greater coronary lesion burden and impaired β-cell function. Restricted cubic spline analyses revealed nonlinear, dose-dependent associations, with MACEs risk increasing above thresholds of 19.88 ng/mL (ox-HDL-C), 25.71 ng/mL (ox-LDL-C), and 19.96 μmol/L (ox-Lp(a)), with sexspecific differences observed. In Cox proportional hazards models, individuals in the highest quartile of oxidized lipoproteins had significantly elevated 5-year MACEs risk compared to those in the lowest quartile, and these associations remained robust after adjusting for confounders (ox-HDL-C: adjust hazard ratio [HR], 1.872; 95% confidence interval [CI], 1.408 to 2.489; P<0.001; ox- LDL-C: adjust HR, 2.239; 95% CI, 1.686 to 2.974; P<0.001; ox-Lp(a): adjust HR, 1.917; 95% CI, 1.442 to 2.549; P<0.001).
