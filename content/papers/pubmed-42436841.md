## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 11,924 patients, 701 (5.9%) utilized postoperative gabapentinoids. In sIPTW-weighted analyses, gabapentinoid utilization was associated with reduced mortality risk (HR 0.75; 95% CI, 0.66-0.84; P < .001). Age-stratified analyses demonstrated effect modification, with survival benefit observed in patients aged ≥55 years (HR 0.70; 95% CI, 0.60-0.81) but not in those <55 years. Within older subgroup, both gabapentin (n = 349; HR 0.70; 95% CI, 0.59-0.83; P < .001) and pregabalin (n = 248; HR 0.68; 95% CI, 0.56-0.83; P < .001) independently improved OS. Dose-response analyses suggested lower-mortality exposure ranges (gabapentin: 1,200-2,400 mg/day; pregabalin: >400 mg/day).
