## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During the 9-year follow-up period, 863 participants (10.81%) developed stroke. WHH was identified as an independent risk factor for stroke. Stratified analysis showed that the association between WHH and stroke was stronger among normotensive individuals (HR=1.53, 95% CI: 1.24-1.88, P < 0.001), whereas it was attenuated among those with hypertension (HR=1.18, 95% CI: 1.03-1.36, P = 0.019).
