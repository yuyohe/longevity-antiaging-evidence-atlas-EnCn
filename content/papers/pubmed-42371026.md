## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 3,389,889 matched pairs, psychiatric disorders without medication were not associated with increased RO risk compared with controls (HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.87-1.07, p = 0.49). In contrast, among 2,990,601 matched pairs, psychotropic medication use was associated with a modest increase in RO risk (HR 1.20, 95% CI 1.09-1.33, p < 0.001). Class-specific analyses demonstrated a small but statistically significant association for selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs) (HR 1.17, 95% CI 1.03-1.32, p = 0.013), while no significant associations were observed for other medication classes. Patients who developed RO were older and had a higher burden of vascular comorbidities, including hypertension, diabetes, hyperlipidemia, and obstructive sleep apnea.
