## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Three distinct trajectories were identified for SBP, DBP and MAP. Overall, SBP and MAP exhibited an upward trend, while DBP showed a downward trend. For all three parameters, lower estradiol was correlated negatively to BP in the "high" group. But BMI and WC had a significant positive correlations of changes in BP in all three groups.
