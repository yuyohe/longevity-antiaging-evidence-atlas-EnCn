## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Malnutrition, sarcopenia, and frailty are increasingly recognized as relevant determinants of outcomes in heart failure (HF), yet comprehensive evaluation of body composition and circulating biomarkers is still not routinely implemented. We aimed to characterize body composition, physical function, and selected novel biomarkers in patients with HF and to compare these findings with those of healthy controls. In this single-centre, observational, cross-sectional study, consecutive patients admitted with acute HF between July 2020 and May 2021 were prospectively enrolled.
