## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Vitamin D and insulin levels were not significantly associated with oxidative stress markers in this cohort. These findings highlight the potential of long-term, culturally structured fasting to modulate redox homeostasis and suggest a complex interplay between age, adiposity, and antioxidant defenses.
