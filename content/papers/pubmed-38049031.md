## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: All proposed hallmarks of aging have been observed in articular cartilage and some have also been described in other joint tissues. Hallmarks include genomic instability, telomere attrition, epigenetic alterations, loss of proteostasis, deregulated nutrient sensing, mitochondrial dysfunction, cellular senescence, stem cell exhaustion, altered intercellular communication, disabled macroautophagy, chronic inflammation, and dysbiosis. There is evidence that these age-related changes contribute to the development of OA in part by promoting cellular senescence. Senescence may therefore serve as a downstream mediator that connects numerous aging hallmarks to OA, likely through the senescence-associated secretory phenotype that is characterized by increased production of proinflammatory cytokines and matrix metalloproteinases.
