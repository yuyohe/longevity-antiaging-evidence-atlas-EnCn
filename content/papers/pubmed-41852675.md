## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over a median 35-month follow-up, 662 deaths occurred, including 328 CVD deaths. Higher LCI quartiles showed progressively higher mortality. For all-cause death, adjusted HRs (95% CI) were 1.41 (1.10-1.80), 1.59 (1.25-2.02) and 1.70 (1.34-2.15) for Q2-Q4 vs. Q1. For CVD death, HRs were 1.45 (1.03-2.02), 1.29 (0.92-1.82) and 1.68 (1.22-2.33). A non-linear pattern was observed for all-cause mortality, with risk increasing when LCI exceeded ~20. The association with CVD mortality was stronger in younger patients (<60 years) (P interaction = 0.048).
