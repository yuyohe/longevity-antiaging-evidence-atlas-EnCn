EN: Nine studies providing 1,363 participants were included in the meta-analysis. Pooled results showed a significant reduction in insulin (WMD: - 0.99 U/ml, 95% CI - 1.66, - 0.33), glucose (WMD: - 1.78 ml/dl, 95% CI - 2.96, - 0.60), CRP (WMD: - 0.60 mg/l, 95% CI - 0.88, - 0.33), HOMA-IR (WMD: - 0.45, 95% CI - 0.77, - 0.11), leptin (WMD: - 2.44 ng/ml, 95% CI - 3.28, - 1.61), BMI (WMD: - 0.55 kg/m2, 95% CI - 1.00, - 0.11), and Ki-67 (WMD: - 4.06, 95% CI - 7.59, - 0.54). We did not observe non-linear changes in the dose-response relationship between metformin and biomarkers as outcomes.
