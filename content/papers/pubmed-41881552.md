## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Longer SB (per 30 min/day increase) was associated with a higher risk of all-cause mortality in both cohorts (CKB: HR 1.013, 95% CI 1.010 to 1.017; UKB: HR 1.012, 95% CI 1.009 to 1.015). PA of any intensity was associated with a reduced risk of all-cause mortality. In the CKB, replacing 30 min/day of SB with an equivalent duration of PA showed comparable protective associations (LPA: HR 0.963, 95% CI 0.958 to 0.968; MPA: HR 0.967, 95% CI 0.961 to 0.972; VPA: HR 0.965, 95% CI 0.960 to 0.971). In the UKB, replacing 30 min/day of SB with VPA was associated with the largest reduction in mortality risk (HR: 0.950, 95% CI 0.931 to 0.970).
