## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 194 children (median follow-up 8.7 years), 162 underwent surgery and 32 were observed. Surgical patients showed smaller prenatal relative lung volume (51% vs. 79%, p = 0.02) and higher neonatal acuity (ICU admission 78% vs. 54%, p < 0.01). After matching, postnatal length of stay remained longer after surgery (15 vs. 6 days, p = 0.03). Thoracoscopy was associated with shorter postoperative stay, fewer complications and no mortality.
