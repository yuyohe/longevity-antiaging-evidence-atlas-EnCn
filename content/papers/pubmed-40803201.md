## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: We enrolled 322 participants (mean age 69.6 ± 7.3 years; 59.6 % women). ET was associated with significant improvements in overall motivation at 12 months (B = 0.78, 95 % CI 0.14-1.42, p = 0.017) compared with UC. Furthermore, ET led to significant increases in intrinsic (B = 0.57; 95 % CI 0.29-0.85, p < 0.001) and identified motivation (B = 0.47, 95 % CI 0.21-0.73, p < 0.001) at 12 months compared with UC, with no between-group differences in extrinsic or introjected motivation.
