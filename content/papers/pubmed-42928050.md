## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Higher E-sdLDL-C at a given level of E-lbLDL-C and greater E-sdLDL-C/LDL-C discordance were each associated with increased advanced CKM prevalence across both cohorts (discordantly high vs. low in NHANES: OR: 2.89, 95% CI: 2.39-3.50). E-sdLDL-C at a given level of E-lbLDL-C was positively associated with incident CVD risk in CHARLS, whereas the discordance associations were attenuated after adjustment for baseline CKM stage.
