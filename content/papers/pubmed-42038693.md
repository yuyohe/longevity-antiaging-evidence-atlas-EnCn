## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Pink1 (PTEN-induced kinase 1) is a key guardian of mitochondrial quality via mitophagy; its mutations are tightly linked to early-onset PD. This review synthesizes how aging, exercise, and high-fat diet (HFD) modulate Pink1 activity and thereby PD risk. Aging down-regulates Pink1, impairing clearance of damaged mitochondria and promoting α-synuclein aggregation. Exercise up-regulates Pink1-Parkin signaling, enhances PGC-1α and brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), and protects dopaminergic neurons in humans and rodents. Conversely, chronic HFD suppresses Pink1, exacerbates oxidative stress, microglial activation and insulin resistance, accelerating Parkinson's disease pathology.
