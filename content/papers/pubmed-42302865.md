## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Six distinct HBTs were identified in each sex, including a decreasing-risk trajectory that reflected improvements across multiple behaviors. Among male participants, the higher severe-risk group had markedly elevated all-cause mortality (hazard ratio [HR]: 2.02, 95% confidence interval [CI]: 1.63-2.51) and CVD mortality (HR: 3.17, 95% CI: 2.01-4.99). Among female participants, the severe-risk group had significantly higher risks of all-cause mortality (HR: 1.83, 95% CI: 1.43-2.34), cancer mortality (HR: 1.91, 95% CI: 1.20-3.05), and CVD mortality (HR: 2.68, 95% CI: 1.77-4.05).
