EN: A total of nine RCTs were included in the analysis. For pain relief, metformin demonstrated the largest effect size with a mean difference of - 1.13 (95% CI - 1.48, - 0.78) compared to usual care, followed by Metformin-Phosphatidylcholine (MFPH) (- 0.92, 95% CI - 1.70, - 0.13) and semaglutide (- 0.90, 95% CI - 1.48, - 0.32). In terms of safety, usual care exhibited the lowest risk of adverse events, with liraglutide (0.09, 95% CI - 0.74, 0.92) and semaglutide (0.21, 95% CI - 0.46, 0.88) also showing favorable safety profiles. The SUCRA rankings further supported these findings, with metformin ranking highest for efficacy (SUCRA: 86.8%) and usual care ranking highest for safety (SUCRA: 75.7%).
