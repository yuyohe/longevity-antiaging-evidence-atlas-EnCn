## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Similar risks of lung cancer-specific mortality were observed across most PA variables. Among ever and/or former smokers, higher levels of moderate, lifetime PA were associated with lower lung cancer mortality (HRever = 0.77; HRformer = 0.63). In contrast, ever and/or current smokers who engaged in intermediate to high levels of strenuous, lifetime PA had higher mortality risk (intermediate: HRever = 1.24; HRformer = 1.39).
