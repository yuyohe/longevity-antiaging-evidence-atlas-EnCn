## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Aleniglipron is an oral, small-molecule glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist (GLP1-RA) in development for obesity treatment. The ACCESS phase 2b placebo-controlled, double-blind study randomized 230 adults (mean BMI 39.5 kg m-2, 54% female) with obesity or overweight to examine the effects of once-daily aleniglipron escalated every 4 weeks to 45, 90 or 120 mg.
