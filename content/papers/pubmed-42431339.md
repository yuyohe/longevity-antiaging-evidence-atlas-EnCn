EN: Liver fibrosis arises from a sustained imbalance between oxidative stress, cell death, and matrix deposition. Using the carbon tetrachloride (CCl4) mouse model, we show that the dietary flavonoid, fisetin orchestrates a broad hepatoprotective program that blunts this cascade.
