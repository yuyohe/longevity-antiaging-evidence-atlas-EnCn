## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In cross-sectional analyses, possible sarcopenia was associated with higher odds of prevalent ADL (OR = 1.82, 95% CI = 1.53-2.17) and IADL (OR = 1.45, 95% CI = 1.23-1.71) impairment. Over up to 9 years, it was associated with increased hazards of incident ADL (HR = 1.42, 95% CI = 1.27-1.59) and IADL (HR = 1.36, 95% CI = 1.21-1.52) impairment. In transition analyses, progression to possible sarcopenia was associated with higher hazards of subsequent ADL (HR = 1.23, 95% CI = 1.01-1.49) and IADL (HR = 1.31, 95% CI = 1.07-1.61) impairment. Reversion to no possible sarcopenia was not significantly associated with ADL (HR = 0.84, 95% CI = 0.58-1.22) or IADL (HR = 0.77, 95% CI = 0.52-1.15) impairment.
