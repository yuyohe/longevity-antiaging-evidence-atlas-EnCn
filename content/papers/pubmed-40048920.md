## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: There appears to be a significant correlation between Klotho and cognitive function and dementia. In addition, subgroup analyses suggests that α-Klotho, serum Klotho, and plasma Klotho could be important biomarkers of cognitive function and neurodegeneration. However, the limited number of RCTs and the predominance of observational studies in the meta-analysis mean that the evidence quality is relatively low. Further high-quality RCTs are needed to establish a definitive relationship.
