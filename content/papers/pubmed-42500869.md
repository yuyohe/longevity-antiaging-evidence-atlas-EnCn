## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Among 6313 participants (mean age 61.05 years; 33.9% men), 868 developed incident CVD, 233 died from any cause, and 88 died from CVD during a median follow-up of 7 years. PA was inversely associated with the risk of incident CVD, all-cause mortality, and CVD mortality. Performing guideline-recommended MVPA was associated with 18% reduced risk for incident CVD (95% CI, 0.68-0.99), 42% for all-cause mortality (95% CI, 0.43-0.79), and 49% for CVD mortality (95% CI, 0.31-0.83). A combination of recommended MVPA and moderate light-intensity PA was associated with the lowest risk, with risk reductions of 24%, 59%, and 78%, respectively.
