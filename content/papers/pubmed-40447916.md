## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Using multiple sources, we provide the conceptual justification and statistical support for a multimorbidity outcome associated with obesity-related conditions, which we term the Health Conditions Index (HCI). This index was designed to capture the health effects of multi-year studies of caloric restriction for older adults with BMIs in the overweight or obesity classification. We used a subset of participants in the Health, Aging and Body Composition Cohort Study to evaluate multiple aspects of the index and its components over 5 years of follow-up.
