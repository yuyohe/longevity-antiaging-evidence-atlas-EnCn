## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: a total of 28 studies were included in this meta-analysis (26 observational studies and 2 randomized trials), reporting a total of 679,259 participants from different continents. Our results showed that high adherence to the MD reduces all-cause mortality risk by 23% (95% CI: 0.70-0.83), while it decreases the risk of cardiovascular mortality by 27% (95% CI: 0.64-0.84) and that of non-fatal cardiovascular events by 23% (95% CI: 0.55-1.01).
