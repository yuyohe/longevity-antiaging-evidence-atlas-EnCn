EN: Background: Hypertension (HTN) and type 2 diabetes mellitus are major global health challenges, and diabetic nephropathy (DN) is a critical complication of diabetes. Although observational studies link HTN to DN progression, causal evidence remains limited. We investigated the potential causal relationship between HTN and DN and quantified the global burden of HTN-attributable type 2 diabetic nephropathy (HTN-T2DN). Methods: We integrated two-sample Mendelian randomization (MR), Bayesian weighted MR, and sensitivity analyses with Global Burden of Disease (GBD) 2021 analyses.
