## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 19 articles were included. The highest doses of liraglutide and semaglutide were 3 mg per day and 1 mg once weekly, respectively, in the included studies. The mean differences in weight and body mass index after treatment were -7.02 kg or 3.07 kg/m2, -8.65 or -5.22 kg/m2, and -6.99 kg or -3.09 kg/m2 for treatment durations of ≤ 6 months, 6-12 months, and >12 months with liraglutide, respectively.
