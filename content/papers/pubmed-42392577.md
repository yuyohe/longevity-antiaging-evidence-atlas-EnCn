## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In the entire cohorts at baseline and completion, gastric emptying correlated significantly with energy intake and, at baseline, with one parameter of satiation. Gastric emptying accounted for only 4%-6% of the variance in these appetitive measures. No such correlations were found in the placebo- or liraglutide-treated groups alone. There were no differences in appetitive measures among the subgroups that displayed different gastric emptying patterns during liraglutide treatment.
