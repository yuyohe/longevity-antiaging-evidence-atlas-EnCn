## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Mood disorders are highly prevalent among patients with coronary artery disease; however, pharmacologic treatment with selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs) poses potential bleeding concerns, particularly in patients receiving dual antiplatelet therapy (DAPT) after percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI). Despite biologic plausibility and observational evidence linking SSRIs to impaired platelet aggregation, data specific to PCI populations remain limited. Using the TriNetX Global Collaborative Network, we identified adults (≥18 years) who underwent PCI with 12 months of DAPT (aspirin plus a P2Y12 inhibitor) between January 2013 and August 2024.
