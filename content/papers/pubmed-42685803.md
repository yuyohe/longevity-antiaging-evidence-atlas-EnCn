## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Significant group-by-time interactions were observed for GS and SPPB (both p ≤ 0.001). GS declined in OC but improved only in MME; at Week 12, all exercise groups exceeded OC. SPPB improved in all exercise groups, with BDJ and MME higher than OC at Week 12. For secondary outcomes, the interaction was significant for ASMI but not MGS. MME increased MGS within group, whereas RBG and MME increased ASMI. Exploratory analyses suggested greater lower-limb lean-mass gains after MME than after OC or BDJ, with no clear advantage over RBG.
