## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: During a mean follow-up of 10.2±5.1 years, CRF was independently associated with mortality, with each 1-MET higher CRF associated with a 16% lower hazard of death (hazard ratio 0.84; 95% CI 0.838-0.842; p<0.001). The addition of CRF significantly improved risk prediction, yielding a categorical NRI of 0.06 and a continuous NRI of 0.37 (both p<0.001), with significant improvement in IDI. Model discrimination increased from 0.662 to 0.682 after addition of CRF (ΔC=0.020; p<0.001).
