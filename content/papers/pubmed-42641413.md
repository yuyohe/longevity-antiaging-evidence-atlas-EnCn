## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In total, 64 studies were included. Nine instruments were identified: HALFT, Makizako Social Frailty Index, Social Support Rating Scale, Groningen Frailty Indicator, Tilburg Frailty Indicator, Comprehensive Frailty Assessment Instrument, Carolina Frailty Index, Edmonton Frailty Scale, and the 10-item CGA Frailty Index. These tools differed widely in conceptual focus, item content, scoring ranges, and cut-off values. Most were originally developed for community-dwelling older adults or other non-oncology groups, and cancer-specific psychometric validation was limited. Reported reliability in cancer populations was often only questionable to acceptable.
