EN: Cardiorespiratory fitness (CRF) has a strong genetic component and low CRF is a major risk factor for cardiovascular morbidity and mortality. The purpose of this study was to develop and validate a polygenic score (PGS) for CRF (CRFPGS) and assess its associations with cardiovascular disease (CVD) and all-cause mortality. We hypothesized that the CRFPGS would demonstrate similar cardioprotective benefits as the CRF phenotype.
