## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: 141 children met inclusion criteria, 48 had CM, 93 CNS-M. CNS-M children were younger (mean 40.8 vs. 54.3 months, p=0.005) and male predominant (64.8% vs. 43.8%, p=0.006). 18.4% of the cohort developed PME, with no significant incidence difference between CNS-M (20.4%) and CM (14.6%) groups, p=0.495. Focal epilepsy was more common in CNS-M 78.9% vs. CM 28.6% (p=0.028). There were no differences in qualitative EEG findings. Quantitative EEG measures demonstrated more severe and prolonged cortical dysfunction in CM (p<0.01).
