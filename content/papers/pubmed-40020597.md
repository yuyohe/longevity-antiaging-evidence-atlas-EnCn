## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Of 112 patients, 67 (59.8 %) achieved the ApoB goal and had lower ApoB values at follow-up compared with those not at goal (37.1 ± 15.0 vs 92.7 ± 19.4 mg/dL, P < 0.001). Patients achieving the ApoB goal demonstrated a greater increase in minimum fibrous cap thickness (+44.6 ± 36.0 vs +24.9 ± 38.1 μm, P = 0.007) and a more pronounced decrease in lipid arc (-57.8 ± 52.8 vs -27.0 ± 59.2°, P = 0.005) at follow-up, compared with those who did not achieve the ApoB goal. At follow-up, thin-cap fibroatheroma (TCFA) was less prevalent among patients achieving the ApoB goal compared with those not at goal (9.0 vs. 40.0 %, P < 0.001). Multivariate analysis demonstrated that achieving an ApoB <65 mg/dL at follow-up independently associated with the absence of TCFA at follow-up (P = 0.004).
