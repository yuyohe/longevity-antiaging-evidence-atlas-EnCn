## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Serum glucose and the ApoB/ApoA-1 ratio showed a non-statistically significant positive association with BC severity (proportional OR: 1.25 (95%CI: 0.92-1.70) for glucose (</≥ 5.60 mmol/L) and 1.31 (95%CI: 0.97-1.76) for ApoB/A-1 ratio (</≥ 1).
