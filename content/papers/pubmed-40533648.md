## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In total, twelve studies including 616 participants (Age: 40 ± 9 years; BMI: 33.6 ± 4.8 kg/m2; 87.3% female) were included in this systematic review and meta-analysis. The combined intervention was more effective, leading to greater reductions in fat mass (-0.93 kg [95% CI: -1.69, -0.18]) and waist circumference (-2.51 cm [95% CI: -3.70, -1.32]) when compared to IF alone. Cardiometabolic health parameters also showed greater improvements, with decreased insulin (-3.1uIU/ml [95%CI: -4.25; -1.95]), HOMA-IR (-0.57 [95%CI: -0.83; -0.31]), LDL (-10.67 mg/dl [95%CI: -20; -1.35]), resting heart rate (-2.68 bpm [95%CI: -4.71; -0.64]), along with enhanced cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2 max:1.92 ml/kg/min [95%CI: 0.32; 3.52]). For the remaining outcome variables, no statistically meaningful differences emerged between the groups.
