## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: This study included 1,313 stroke patients, among whom 539 experienced ACM and 209 experienced CVM. In the Cox models, compared with low DLMI, high DLMI had a lower incidence of ACM [Model 1: HR = 0.63 (95% CI: 0.44-0.91, p = 0.013); Model 2: HR = 0.61 (95% CI: 0.44-0.84, p = 0.003); Model 3: HR = 0.63 (95% CI: 0.46-0.88, p = 0.007)]. However, no significant association was observed between high DLMI and CVM. Subgroup and sensitivity analyses generally supported the robustness of these results.
