## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: We included 4 trials (43,456 patients) assessing albiglutide, dulaglutide, exenatide once weekly and liraglutide. GLP-1 RAs reduced MACE by 13% (HR 0.87, 95% CI 0.82-0.93), an effect which was consistent in both subgroups (HR 0.91, 95% CI 0.85-0.97 and HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.72-0.90 with and without metformin, respectively). Presence of metformin at baseline did not affect the overall favorable effect of GLP-1 RAs both on cardiovascular and all-cause mortality. Finally, subgroup meta-analyses suggested that GLP-1 RAs had a neutral effect on stroke, myocardial infarction and hospitalization for heart failure, irrespective of metformin use at baseline.
