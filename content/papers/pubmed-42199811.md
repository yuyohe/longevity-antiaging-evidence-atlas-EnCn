## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Overall, 108 patients were randomized. At week 8, least squares (LS) mean (standard error [SE]) reduction in MSSBP from baseline was greater with T20/SA2.5 than with T40 (-16.97 [2.06] mmHg vs. -7.42 [2.04] mmHg; P<0.001). LS mean (SE) reductions in MSDBP at week 8 were also greater with T20/SA2.5 than with T40 (-7.98 [1.17] mmHg vs. -4.01 [1.15] mmHg; P=0.006). The BP control rate was significantly higher with T20/SA2.5 than with T40 (53.70% vs. 29.63%; P=0.010); the response rate was numerically higher with T20/SA2.5 (12.96% vs. 5.56%; P=0.182). The incidence of TEAEs was lower with T20/SA2.5 than with T40 (9.26% vs. 20.37%, P=0.104); most events were mild.
