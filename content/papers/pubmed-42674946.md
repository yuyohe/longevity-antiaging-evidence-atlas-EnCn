## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: There were 37,541 deaths, including 791 due to accidents. Approximately 70% of participants reported sleeping 6-8 hours per night, while 20.2% reported 4-6 hours, 1.2% less than 4 hours, and 8.7% more than 8 hours. After adjusting for covariates, participants with a shorter sleep duration (4-6 hours) had a 30% increased risk of MVA mortality compared with those with 6-8 hours (HR: 1.30; 95% CI: 1.09-1.55). Those with sleep durations exceeding 8 hours had a similar 31% increase in risk (HR: 1.31; 95% CI: 1.05-1.64). This risk was further elevated among smokers, frequent alcohol drinkers, and sleeping pill users.
