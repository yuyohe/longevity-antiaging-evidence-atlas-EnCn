## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Recruitment was terminated early after enrolling 204 patients: 106 in the intervention group and 98 in the control group. At 6 months, no significant difference was observed in the primary composite endpoint between the intervention and control groups (17.9% vs 19.4%; HR, 0.90; 95%CI, 0.48-1.70). ## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: In elderly patients with chronic HF and malnutrition or at risk of malnutrition, a pragmatic nutritional intervention did not reduce major clinical outcomes.
