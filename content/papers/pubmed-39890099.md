## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Relationships between ultraprocessed food (UPF) intakes and health have been well described in adults, but evidence of these relationships in older adults is rather limited. Demographic shifts in aging emphasize the importance of understanding the role of diet in maintaining health during later life. The aim of this systematic review was to identify health outcomes associated with UPF consumption in those aged ≥60 years.
