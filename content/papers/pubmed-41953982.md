## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 244 participants were assigned to the intervention group (PINGS) and 256 to the usual care group, of whom 43% were women, with mean (SD) age 58 (11) years. Mean change in systolic blood pressure at month 12 from baseline was -5.5 mm Hg (95% CI, -9.6 to -1.4 mm Hg; P=0.008). The primary outcome was achieved in 163 (67%) patients with PINGS versus 109 (43%) in the usual care arm, with a between-group difference of 24% (95% CI, 15%-33%; P<0.001). No significant between-group differences were noted in the secondary outcome of major adverse cardiovascular events or the presumed key mediator of medication adherence. Serious adverse events were 27 of 244 (11.1%) with PINGS versus 18 of 256 (7.0%) in usual care (P=0.12).
