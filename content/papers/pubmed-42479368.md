EN: We included eight studies with a total of 1,483 participants. GLP-1 RAs led to a smaller infarct size indexed to the AAR compared with placebo. The mean reduction was 10.14% points (95% confidence interval [CI]: -14.11--6.17; P < 0.001). The primary infarct-size analysis was based on only three RCTs.
