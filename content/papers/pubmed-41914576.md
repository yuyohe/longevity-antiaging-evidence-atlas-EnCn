## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: In total, 25 trials (17,751 participants) evaluated psychological well-being and 11 trials (1961 participants) evaluated depressive symptoms. GLP-1RA treatment was associated with a small but significant improvement in psychological well-being compared with control conditions (SMD = 0.374, 95% CI 0.093-0.656), whereas no significant effect was observed for depressive symptoms (SMD = 0.079, 95% CI -0.024-0.182). Improvements in psychological well-being were consistently observed in studies using semaglutide, tirzepatide or liraglutide, subcutaneous administration, and in populations with type 2 diabetes mellitus or obesity.
