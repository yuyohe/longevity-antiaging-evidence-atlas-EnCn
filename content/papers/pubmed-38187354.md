## Conclusion Boundary / 结论边界

EN: Compared with traditional cardiac and renal biomarkers, serum α-Klotho could be a more appropriate predict biomarker for AKI after AMI in patients' eGFR >60 ml/min ∗ 1.73 m2. Higher α-Klotho levels are related to the development of AKI during hospitalization and suggest a higher prevalence of CKD after discharge.
