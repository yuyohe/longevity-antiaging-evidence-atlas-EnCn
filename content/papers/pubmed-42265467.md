EN: How the small intestine ages at the cellular and molecular level has been unclear. Here we profile single nuclei from young and aged primate small intestine and find that aging brings barrier dysfunction, chronic inflammation and a shift in stem cell differentiation away from absorptive cells toward secretory cells.
