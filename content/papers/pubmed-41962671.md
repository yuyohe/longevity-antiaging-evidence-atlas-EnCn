## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Compared to participants remaining frail or pre-frail, those achieving sustained frailty remission showed a significantly reduced risk of developing significant depressive symptoms (SHARE: aHR = 0.57; NHATS: aHR = 0.72). The "remain robust" group exhibited the lowest risk (SHARE: aHR = 0.28; NHATS: aHR = 0.50), while sustained deterioration increased this risk in both cohorts.
