EN: Nephrogenesis is critical to mammalian kidney function throughout life. Decreased nephron formation, an embryonic process dependent on ureteric-mesenchymal tissue interactions, is a fundamental feature of Congenital Anomalies of the Kidney and Urinary Tract (CAKUT) and an antecedent to adult-onset cardiovascular and renal disease. Yet, mechanisms controlling the number of nephrons formed during embryogenesis are largely undefined. Here, we elucidated deleterious effects of increased mTOR signaling on murine nephrogenesis. A rare human genetic missense variant (ILKT173I) in Integrin-Linked Kinase (ILK) was identified in a human CAKUT cohort.
