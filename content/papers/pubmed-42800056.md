## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Apolipoprotein B (apoB)-containing lipoproteins contribute heterogeneously to atherosclerosis. While apoB particles are major determinants of plaque burden, Lp(a) has been linked to plaque inflammation and instability. We investigated the associations of non-Lp(a) apoB, Lp(a), and their relative balance with coronary plaque burden and vulnerability.
