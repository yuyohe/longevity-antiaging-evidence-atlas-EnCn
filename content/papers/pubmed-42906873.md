## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: A total of 1275 patients (59% male, age 57±11, MELD 14±6) attended 1973 LT-PT visits. Of these, 355 attended two or more follow-up visits, participating in the prehabilitation program. At baseline, median LFI was 3.8 (3.2-4.4), 6MWT 336 m (243-403), and GST 1 (0.8-1.2); 23%, 26%, and 29% of the cohort were frail by each tool, respectively. Among subjects who improved LFI (n=226/352), the absolute change was -0.39 (-0.32 to -0.46), and for 6MWT (n=212/334) it was 54 m (43-64); fewer than half improved GST. A total of 667 (55%) patients were waitlisted, 462 (37%) transplanted, and 335 (26%) died. LFI improvement by at least 0.4 was associated with a ∼50% reduction in mortality (HR=0.56, p=0.037). Improvement in 6MWT was not associated with a survival advantage.
