## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Malignant tumors are the leading cause of death in individuals over 65 years old, with metastasis as the primary driver. Emerging evidence suggests that age-related metabolic changes and secreted factors increase the risk of metastasis, but the underlying mechanisms remain unclear. Here we demonstrate in mice that extracellular vesicles (EVs) from senescent hepatocytes promote metastasis across tumor types. We show that aged liver tissue exhibits elevated expression of P2X purinoceptor 7 (P2RX7), which is associated with increased EV biogenesis.
