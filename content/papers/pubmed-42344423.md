## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: The meta-analysis of 75 ESs from case-control studies indicated that women with the highest total dietary fat intake had a significantly increased risk of breast cancer compared to those with the lowest intake (ES: 1.23; 95% CI: [1.12, 1.34]; p < 0.001; I² = 67%), suggesting a 23% higher risk. In contrast, the pooled analysis of 59 ESs from cohort studies showed no statistically significant association between total dietary fat intake and breast cancer risk (ES: 1.03; 95% CI: [0.99, 1.06]; p = 0.112; I² = 45%).
