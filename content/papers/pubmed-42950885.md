## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Across both groups, body weight decreased by 2.7 kg (95% CI -2.9 to -2.4), fat mass by 1.7 kg (-1.9 to -1.5), and muscle mass by 0.6 kg (-0.7 to -0.4). Weight and fat mass reductions were greater over time in the 5:2 group than in the 16:8 group, whereas muscle mass changes were similar. Waist circumference and blood pressure decreased significantly, with no between-group differences. Both regimens reduced eating window duration, energy intake, mean and peak glucose levels, and time spent in hyperglycemia.
