## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Evidence regarding the mortality risk associated with physical activity (PA) and multimorbidity is limited. This study investigates trends in the prevalence of multimorbidity in the population and the mortality risk associated with PA and multimorbidity. The association between PA and overall survival was analyzed in 39,940 subjects from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey cohorts.
