## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: Over median follow-up for 7.3 years, 1355 diabetics died. There was a positive and linear association of mortality with phenotypic age acceleration (HRall-cause 1.04; HRCVD 1.04; HRcancer 1.04, p<0.001) and biological age acceleration (HRall-cause 1.03; HRCVD 1.04; HRcancer 1.03, p<0.001). Telomere length was inversely associated with all-cause mortality (tertile (T)3 vs T1: HR 0.67, p<0.05). The concentration of klotho had a U-shaped relationship with mortality (T2 vs T1: HRall-cause 0.62; HRCVD 0.48; HRcancer 0.47, p<0.05). Further, stratified analysis by age and sex found that the associations of ageing-related markers with mortality were more significant in the aged and female subgroup.
