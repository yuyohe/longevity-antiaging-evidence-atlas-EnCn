## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: We included 8 cohort studies in our meta-analysis. CRC patients with T2DM treated with metformin had a lower overall mortality than CRC patients with T2DM who did not receive metformin (HR = 0.80, 95% CI 0.67-0.95). There was no significant difference in CRC-specific mortality between CRC patients with T2DM who used metformin and those who did not (HR = 0.84, 95% CI 0.65-1.08).
