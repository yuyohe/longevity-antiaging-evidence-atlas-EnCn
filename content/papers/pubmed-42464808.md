EN: Over a median follow-up of 12.8 years, 2,644 deaths occurred, including 2,009 attributable to all causes and 635 to cardiovascular causes. After adjustment for potential confounders, each one-unit increment in CTI was associated with a 26% higher risk of all-causemortality [HR, 1.26; 95% confidence interval (CI), 1.15-1.38; p < 0.001) and a 25% higher risk of cardiovascular mortality (HR, 1.25; 95% CI,1.03-1.51; p = 0.021).
