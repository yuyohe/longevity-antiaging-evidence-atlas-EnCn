## Main Results / 主要结果

EN: After matching, 425 app users were compared with 425 usual care participants (mean age 59 years; 74% women; 65% Black). App users experienced greater reductions in SBP (-4.6 mm Hg vs -1.6 mm Hg; p = 0.003) and DBP (-2.5 mm Hg vs -0.9 mm Hg; p = 0.012) than matched controls.
