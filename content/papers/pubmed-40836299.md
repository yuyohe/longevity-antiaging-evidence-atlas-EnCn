EN: Compared with insulin, GLP-1 RAs, DPP4is, and glinides were associated with a lower risk of 3-point MACE (HR: 0.48 [0.31-0.76], 0.70 [0.57-0.85], and 0.70 [0.52-0.94], respectively). SUs were associated with a higher risk of 3-point MACE compared with DPP4is (HR: 1.30 [1.06-1.59]). DPP4is, GLP-1 RAs, and glinides showed a lower risk of 3-point MACE compared with acarbose (HR: 0.62 [0.51-0.76], 0.47 [0.29-0.75], and 0.59 [0.43-0.81], respectively).
