## 本次做了什么 / This Update

**补资料，同时控量。** 补检 2026-10-02 至 2026-10-10，得到 298 个唯一 PubMed 匹配，其中 289 个为新候选。与 10 月 1 日比较，候选新增进入当前层 72 条、旧记录退出 66 条，净增 6 条；findings 新进入 35 条、旧记录退出 9 条，净增 26 条。

**Refresh within fixed limits.** The search found 298 unique matches, including 289 new candidates. Compared with October 1, candidates added 72 and retired 66, net +6; findings added 35 and retired 9, net +26. New findings may come from existing candidates, not newly published papers.

**整理旧资产。** 本轮另外封存 10 月 1 日五张 CSV、57 张 PNG 和两个大网页。历史视觉档案累计 241 个原件、5 个校验 ZIP。旧网页保留提示，9 月和最新 10 月 CSV 继续展开；同月上次快照从 ZIP 恢复。

**Organize historical assets.** October 1 now has separate verified CSV and visual archives. Five visual ZIPs preserve 241 originals. Historical HTML paths show notices; September and the latest October CSVs remain unpacked. Git history is not rewritten or reduced by this organization.

**修正研究类型，解释正反结果。** 准实验或非随机对照组独立标注，不当作完全随机比较。六篇代表文献用短段落讲清对象、发现和限制；例如体能改善不能写成认知改善，细胞与小鼠结果不能写成人体疗效。全部 2,450 个 finding PMID 已核对，全文复核仍待完成。

**Clarify designs and limits.** Quasi-experimental and mixed-allocation studies are labeled separately. Six bilingual abstract-based notes explain both positive and null findings. All 2,450 finding PMIDs were checked against official NCBI records. Full-text review remains pending.

## 图片与边界 / Visuals and Limits

[本期全部图片 / Current PNGs](docs/assets/visual-assets/2026-10/)已重建。117 个成分或主题的撤稿风险查询更新至 2026-10-10：596 条主题匹配行、540 个唯一 PMID。这是历史累计，不是本月新增撤稿数量，也不是疗效排名。

All visuals were rebuilt. Retraction queries cover 117 targets, with 596 matched rows and 540 unique PMIDs. These are historical cumulative counts, not new monthly retractions or efficacy rankings.

50 张成分卡沿用既有暂定评级，分别标明评级资料日期、撤稿检查日期和图片日期。图片更新不等于完成 50 个成分的新一轮全文复核。

Ingredient cards preserve existing provisional grades and distinguish evidence-review, retraction-check and image dates. Updated images do not imply new full-text reviews of all 50 ingredients.

- 动物延寿不等于人类延寿 / Animal lifespan findings do not establish human lifespan extension.
- 指标改善不等于返老还童 / Biomarker changes do not establish rejuvenation.
- 论文多、图颜色深不等于有效 / More papers and darker heatmaps do not prove efficacy.
- A 级草稿不是处方 / An A-grade draft is not a prescription.
- 不提供个人诊断、剂量、停药或购买建议 / No personal diagnosis, dosing, stopping medication or purchase advice.
