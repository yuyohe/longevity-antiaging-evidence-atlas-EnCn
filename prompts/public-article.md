# Codex Prompt — Chinese Public Article

Convert a selected topic page into a Chinese public-facing article.

Audience:

- educated Chinese internet readers
- interested in longevity, anti-aging, supplements, biohacking
- not necessarily medical professionals

Style:

- clear
- evidence-first
- cautious
- no supplement marketing tone

Structure:

1. Opening conflict
2. Evidence hierarchy
3. What is actionable now
4. What requires medical supervision
5. What is only frontier research
6. What is overhyped
7. Final takeaway

Rules:

- Preserve uncertainty.
- Add definitions for technical terms.
- Do not provide prescriptions or dosages.
- Link back to the evidence matrix and paper cards.
